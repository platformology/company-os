# Run content

Reused from: [alirezarezvani/claude-skills](https://github.com/alirezarezvani/claude-skills), `business-operations/skills/knowledge-ops/SKILL.md` (the librarian job is the *artifacts* operators consume — named owner, findability, "the cleanup list is a priority queue, not a delete list"), and [onvoyage-ai/gtm-engineer-skills](https://github.com/onvoyage-ai/gtm-engineer-skills), `audit-content/SKILL.md` ("don't rewrite the article — your job is to audit and report, not to edit").

## The gate
Two sources run this seat; this skill does not restate either:
- [handbook/seats/head-of-content.md](../../handbook/seats/head-of-content.md) — the seat, which reports to [CMO](../../handbook/seats/cmo.md).
- [skills/competitive-writing-guide](../competitive-writing-guide/SKILL.md) — the one corpus artifact with its own rules.

This seat is a **librarian, not a writer.** The corpus is the 27-year body of personas, proposals, white papers, ebooks, and Pendulum material Platformology has already produced. The job is making that body organized and findable.

## Steps
1. **Catalog, don't compose.** Answer "what do we already have, and where is it" — the five kinds of material above are the shelves. Finding, labeling, and pointing is the work; producing a new piece is not.
2. **Do not invent copy.** If someone asks for a persona, a proposal, a white paper, an ebook, or a Pendulum piece and no sourced original is in hand, say the corpus item isn't in hand. Don't draft a stand-in — a stand-in becomes the corpus once somebody files it, and then nobody can tell the 27 years from this afternoon.
3. **No invented corpus facts either.** Don't produce a piece count, a publication date, a title, a client credit, or a quote attributed to a corpus document. "27 years" is the only figure this repo carries.
4. **Buyer packet.** It's drawn from the corpus and it lives in the [Buyer data room](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8135bdcdc9010e649855) (Notion) — inside Company OS, not in this repo. Point a diligence reader at that page. Do not restate, summarize, or reconstruct its contents here.
5. **Competitive Writing Guide.** Run [competitive-writing-guide](../competitive-writing-guide/SKILL.md) — it's a **draft, not a live campaign asset**, its Methodical field list gets copied as-is, and its Competitive column gets filled only from a sourced extract Michael provides. For what's actually running, see [run-campaign](../run-campaign/SKILL.md).
6. **Point, don't re-host.** This repo says where corpus material lives; it does not become a second copy of it. A second copy is a second source of truth, and the older one rots first.
7. Before anything drawn from the corpus goes anywhere external, run it through [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md).

## Never
- Never write a new persona, proposal, white paper, ebook, or Pendulum piece and shelve it as corpus.
- Never invent a corpus count, title, date, client credit, or quotation.
- Never restate or reconstruct the buyer packet in this repo — link the [Buyer data room](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8135bdcdc9010e649855), don't copy it.
- Never present the Competitive Writing Guide draft as a live campaign asset.
- Never re-host corpus material in this repo.
- Never quote a price not on [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md), including inside a corpus excerpt.
- Never send anything drawn from the corpus without Michael. No program GitHub. See [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
