# Support call prep

Reused from: [quivly/skills](https://github.com/quivly/skills), `customer-engineering/meeting-prep/SKILL.md` (the pre-call brief shape — since-last-touch, open items, watch-out-for, suggested agenda, "never walk in blind... flag gaps rather than guessing"), and [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins), `partner-built/common-room/skills/call-prep/SKILL.md` ("never invent deal context... a short honest output is always better than a long fabricated one").

## When to use this
Before any call with a client. Today that's Cornelia Choe.

## The gate
Everything in the brief traces to [handbook/clients.md](../../handbook/clients.md), the [If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f) FAQ page, or [PA handoff](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b8138a686fd8a52313333) notes. If a fact isn't in one of those, the brief says so — it doesn't fill the gap with a guess.

## Steps
1. Confirm who the call is with and what status they hold. Today: Cornelia Choe, Alumni, The Leaders Alliance — one of 1 Alumni against 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect (18 Aug 2026 extract, see [clients.md](../../handbook/clients.md)).
2. State the call type up front: **support, not a pitch.** Cornelia's Thu 20 Aug 2026, 11:00–11:30am America/Denver call is support. Don't reframe it as a sales opportunity anywhere in the brief.
3. Logistics: if two meeting links exist and are unresolved — as with Cornelia's two Zoom URLs — the brief says so and flags both to Michael. It does not pick one.
4. Offer / revenue: leave blank if not named. Do not invent a deal, a price, or a revenue figure for this call — see [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md) and [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
5. Check [PA handoff](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b8138a686fd8a52313333) and the [If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f) FAQ page for anything logged since the last touch.
6. Build the brief in this shape:
   - **Who / status** — name and client status, per `clients.md`.
   - **Call type** — support or pitch, stated explicitly.
   - **Logistics** — time, timezone, link(s) — flagged if unresolved.
   - **Open items** — anything sourced from the FAQ page, `clients.md`, or PA handoff; "none sourced" if there isn't anything.
   - **Watch out for** — known open questions (e.g., "two Zoom links, unresolved").
   - **Not in this brief** — offer, price, revenue: named missing, not guessed.
7. This is a draft. Hand it to whoever's taking the call. Never send it, and never send anything drafted from it, without Michael's sign-off. See [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md) and the [author-support-reply](../author-support-reply/SKILL.md) skill for any reply that goes back to the client.
8. Before the brief is used, run it through [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md).

## Example (Cornelia Choe, Thu 20 Aug 2026)
> **Who / status:** Cornelia Choe — Alumni, The Leaders Alliance.
> **Call type:** Support, not a pitch.
> **Logistics:** 11:00–11:30am America/Denver. Two Zoom URLs on file, unresolved — flagged to Michael, not picked.
> **Open items:** None sourced beyond the FAQ page — check [If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f) for anything client-specific before the call.
> **Watch out for:** Don't let this drift into a pitch.
> **Not in this brief:** Offer, price, revenue — named missing, not guessed.

## Never
- Never invent a client, an offer, a price, or a revenue figure for the call.
- Never turn an Alumni or Past-client support call into a pitch.
- Never pick between two unresolved meeting links.
- Never send the brief, or anything drafted from it, without Michael's sign-off.
