## The gate
The seat page is [handbook/seats/junior-digital-designer.md](../../handbook/seats/junior-digital-designer.md), and this skill does not restate it.

**This seat is unassigned.** There is no holder, no design brief, no brand book, no palette, type, or logo, no digital property, and no live job for it to run today. That is the whole honest state of the seat.

## If you were sent here for work
There is none assigned. Say that plainly.

That is not a lookup failure or a page that hasn't been written yet — the leftover job file on the M1 is not in this repo and is not restated here. Nothing here needs to be designed, and no colour needs to be picked to make the answer look complete.

## Steps
1. Open [handbook/seats/junior-digital-designer.md](../../handbook/seats/junior-digital-designer.md) before answering anything.
2. Asked who the junior digital designer is? The seat sits in Craft cluster. **No owner recorded.** Do not invent a design executive, and do not hand the work to [CMO](../../handbook/seats/cmo.md) because that seat names the missing brand book.
3. Asked for a mockup, graphic, template, icon, logo, or "just a quick concept"? There is no brief. Say so. Name the leftover. Do not produce a concept to open a conversation.
4. Asked what our colours, fonts, or logo are? **Not recorded.** Do not pick, sample, or reconstruct them.
5. Asked to design against a campaign? The Campaign SOP belongs to [VP Campaigns](../../handbook/seats/vp-campaigns.md) and [Campaign Coordinator](../../handbook/seats/campaign-coordinator.md). This seat does not open a campaign to have something to design for. Client websites are not a product.
6. Asked whether the senior seat should review this? **Both seats are unassigned.** There is no reviewer and no reviewee. See [run-senior-digital-designer](../run-senior-digital-designer/SKILL.md).
7. Asked who should fill the seat? Not recorded — see [handbook/people.md](../../handbook/people.md). Jhana and Bob have no recorded Role.
8. No KPI is named for this seat. Do not invent one. Point at [handbook/leftovers/junior-digital-designer.md](../../handbook/leftovers/junior-digital-designer.md).
9. If anything about this seat does go out, run it through [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) first.

## Never
- Never name a Junior Digital Designer. The seat is unassigned.
- Never produce a mockup, wireframe, graphic, template, icon, logo, palette, or type pairing.
- Never invent a colour, a typeface, a logo, or a visual identity.
- Never invent the junior / senior split, and never place either seat above or below the other.
- Never invent an accountability or KPI for this seat.
- Never add a sixth connected tool, and never treat Slack, Drive, or Notion as a publishing channel.
- Never send anything from this seat without Michael. No program GitHub. See [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
