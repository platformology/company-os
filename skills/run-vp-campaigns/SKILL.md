## The gate
Two pages define this seat; this skill restates neither:
- [handbook/seats/vp-campaigns.md](../../handbook/seats/vp-campaigns.md) — the seat: its purpose, what it owns, and who reports into it.
- [Campaign SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ea99c0dcf96db7cd2e) (Notion) — the live SOP this seat owns. This repo does not restate the SOP.

## This is the seat, not the day-to-day
This skill answers ownership questions about VP Campaigns. It does **not** walk a live campaign.

If the ask is "run it," "start it," or "where are we on the live campaign," stop and use [run-campaign](../run-campaign/SKILL.md) instead — that skill points at both the Campaign SOP and the [Campaign Coordinator do-this page](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b8181aaece1c83104102d) (Notion), which is the Coordinator's page, not this seat's.

## Who owns what
- **VP Campaigns is not Campaign Coordinator.** Two seats. VP Campaigns owns how a campaign runs start to finish and owns the Campaign SOP; [Campaign Coordinator](../../handbook/seats/campaign-coordinator.md) reports into this seat and runs the day-to-day steps off the do-this page. Delegating the day-to-day does not move ownership of the SOP off this seat.
- **CMO does not own day-to-day.** [CMO](../../handbook/seats/cmo.md) owns positioning and marketing, and owns the offer ladder in [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md) staying accurate against the Sales Menu. Running a campaign day-to-day is VP Campaigns / Campaign Coordinator. Don't route campaign execution to CMO.
- **Nobody is recorded in this seat.** Who fills VP Campaigns is named missing — see [handbook/people.md](../../handbook/people.md) and [`SOURCES.md`](../../SOURCES.md). The seat still owns what it owns while vacant.

## Steps
1. Open [handbook/seats/vp-campaigns.md](../../handbook/seats/vp-campaigns.md) first and answer the ownership question from that page — don't reason it out from memory.
2. Open the [Campaign SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ea99c0dcf96db7cd2e) in Notion for anything about how a campaign actually runs. This repo does not restate it.
3. Hand day-to-day campaign steps to [run-campaign](../run-campaign/SKILL.md), and keep the Coordinator's do-this page with the Coordinator.
4. Quote prices only from [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md). Where the ladder shows two numbers, both stay in the draft or neither does — ask Michael, per [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
5. **Do not record a seat holder for VP Campaigns** — it stays named missing until Michael says otherwise.
6. Before anything from this seat goes anywhere external, run it through [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md).

## Never
- Never restate the Campaign SOP in this repo — link it, don't copy it.
- Never treat VP Campaigns and Campaign Coordinator as one seat, and never fold either into CMO.
- Never hand day-to-day campaign work to CMO.
- Never name a holder for this seat, or hand this seat's ownership to a person the repo doesn't record.
- Never quote a price not on [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md), and never resolve a "both" into one number.
- Never send anything from a campaign without Michael. No program GitHub. See [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
