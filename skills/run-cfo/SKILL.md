# Run the CFO seat

Reused from: [narrative-io/narrative-skills-marketplace](https://github.com/narrative-io/narrative-skills-marketplace), `docs/authoring-skills.md` ("never duplicate" the reference in the skill body), and [dzhng/skills](https://github.com/dzhng/skills), `skills/authoring/write-skills/SKILL.md` ("keep a single source of truth").

## The gate
Three sources define this seat. This skill points at them and restates none of them:

- [handbook/seats/cfo.md](../../handbook/seats/cfo.md) — the seat itself: purpose, what it owns, who reports into it.
- [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81) (Notion) — the live process. The repo defers to this page; do not copy it here.
- [handbook/how-money-moves.md](../../handbook/how-money-moves.md) — the five jobs a coordinator runs: Attract, Diagnose, Place, Deliver, Expand alumni.

**The seat holder is not recorded** — see [handbook/people.md](../../handbook/people.md). Running this skill does not fill the seat, and does not let you name someone who fills it.

## Steps
1. Open all three sources above before answering anything.
2. Sort the question into one of the three things that land on this seat, then follow the row:

   **Unit economics.** The only sourced prices are in [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md), and the ladder shows two numbers in several places — quote both, pick neither. There is no cost, margin, or per-unit figure anywhere in this repo. If the answer requires one, that is a named missing, not a calculation you run.

   **Cash.** No cash balance, invoice, accounts-receivable figure, or savings total is sourced here. Bench is the Platformology general ledger and **there is no Bench connector** — see [handbook/seats/bookkeeper.md](../../handbook/seats/bookkeeper.md) and the [run-bookkeeper](../run-bookkeeper/SKILL.md) skill. Go to [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81) in Notion, or say the number is missing.

   **Buyer-ready books.** The buyer-facing surface is the [Buyer data room](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8135bdcdc9010e649855) (Notion) plus this repo. The books are buyer-ready to exactly the degree the gaps stay named — a dressed-up number is worse than a blank, because diligence finds it.
3. **Blue Sky stays off the LLC books.** See [handbook/company-facts.md](../../handbook/company-facts.md) and [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
4. **Named missing stay named.** Carry them forward as written, do not resolve them to make an answer look finished:
   - June and July 2026 are **not closed** — see [handbook/seats/controller.md](../../handbook/seats/controller.md) and the [run-controller](../run-controller/SKILL.md) skill.
   - KPI "Current" stays empty. Nothing is written in that field — no figure, and not a 0 standing in for one.
   - The client counts are a separate fact: 0 Current and 0 Prospect on the 18 Aug 2026 extract in [handbook/clients.md](../../handbook/clients.md). Quote those counts with the extract date attached, and do not carry them over into the KPI.
   - The track record is uncollapsed — 124 (Dec 2022), 128 (Book Retreat era), later 130–131. Quote a number with its date attached; never average or pick one. See [handbook/company-facts.md](../../handbook/company-facts.md).
   - Who fills this seat, and the seats reporting into it.
5. Two seats report into this one: [Controller](../../handbook/seats/controller.md) for the monthly close ([run-controller](../run-controller/SKILL.md)) and [Bookkeeper](../../handbook/seats/bookkeeper.md) for bills and the general ledger ([run-bookkeeper](../run-bookkeeper/SKILL.md)). Hand the question down rather than answering it from this seat.
6. Before anything from this seat goes anywhere external, run it through [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) — or the [last-gate-then-stop](../last-gate-then-stop/SKILL.md) skill — and stop.

## Never
- Never restate the [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81) Notion page in this repo — link it, don't copy it.
- Never invent an invoice, an accounts-receivable figure, a cash balance, a savings total, a margin, or a unit-economics number. None of those is sourced here.
- Never state a close for June or July 2026. They are not closed.
- Never fill KPI "Current" with a number.
- Never let Blue Sky touch the LLC books.
- Never quote a price that is not in [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md), and never pick one number where the ladder shows two.
- Never name a person into this seat. Not recorded means not recorded.
- Never send anything from this seat without Michael. No program GitHub. See [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
