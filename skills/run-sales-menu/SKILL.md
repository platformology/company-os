# Run the Sales Menu

Reused from: [narrative-io/narrative-skills-marketplace](https://github.com/narrative-io/narrative-skills-marketplace), `docs/authoring-skills.md` ("never duplicate" the reference in the skill body), [dzhng/skills](https://github.com/dzhng/skills), `skills/authoring/write-skills/SKILL.md` ("keep a single source of truth"), and [dswh/company-os](https://github.com/dswh/company-os), `brain/offers.md` ("never quote a price not written here").

## The gate
Two sources run a placement, and this skill restates neither:
- [Sales Menu](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b817dae2eeb55c5902ad7) (Notion) — the live line items. **Rungs come from here.**
- [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md) — the public price ladder. **Numbers come from here.**

## Steps
1. Open the [Sales Menu](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b817dae2eeb55c5902ad7) in Notion before doing anything. It is the live list of what can be sold; this repo does not copy it.
2. **Place from the Sales Menu.** The job is matching the client's situation to a rung already on that menu — see "offer fit" in [handbook/seats/cso.md](../../handbook/seats/cso.md). If nothing on the menu fits, the answer is "check with Michael," not a new rung.
3. Take the number from [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md) — not from the menu's phrasing, not from memory, not from a deck. **Public prices only.**
4. **Where a rung is stated two ways, quote both, in the order written.** Multi-author, Platform in a Box, Book Marketing Uncovery, and Digital each carry two public figures. Bestseller Uncovery carries one figure told two ways — credited toward Platinum in one telling, an additional charge in another — so give both tellings. Never average, and never pick the lower one to look cheap or the higher one to look premium; picking is Michael's call, per [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
5. **Platinum Major List has no public price.** Say exactly that. It is named missing in [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md) — do not back into a figure from the rung below it.
6. The **lead magnet** has no Offers row and the **membership price** is not published. Say "not recorded — check with Michael"; don't borrow the nearest rung's number.
7. Quoting is not sending. Before a placement or a number goes to a client, run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md).

## Where this sits
Placing is job 3 of the five in [handbook/how-money-moves.md](../../handbook/how-money-moves.md), and it follows Diagnose. For the mechanics of reading a single ladder row correctly, run [place-on-offer-ladder](../place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md) — this skill is the Sales-Menu side of that same job. Head of Demand, Head of Sales, and SDR are all unassigned; whoever runs a placement today does it without a filled seat under them, and that is not a reason to invent one.

## Never
- Never restate the Sales Menu in this repo — link it, don't copy it.
- Never invent a rung, tier, bundle, discount, or "starting at" figure that isn't on the Sales Menu and priced in [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md).
- Never collapse a rung stated two ways into one number or one telling.
- Never publish a price for Platinum Major List, the lead magnet, or membership.
- Never send a placement or a quote without Michael. No program GitHub. See [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
