## CCO means Chief Customer Officer
Nothing else. Not Chief Content Officer — the corpus is [Head of Content](../../handbook/seats/head-of-content.md), which reports into [CMO](../../handbook/seats/cmo.md). Not Chief Compliance Officer and not Chief Creative Officer — neither seat exists in [handbook/seats/](../../handbook/seats/README.md). Not [COO](../../handbook/seats/coo.md) either: Anthony C. Garcia's recorded public title is COO / Chief Persona Architect, a different seat. If a request says "CCO" and means content, compliance, or creative, say so and stop rather than answering from this seat.

## The gate
- [handbook/seats/cco.md](../../handbook/seats/cco.md) — the seat: purpose, what it owns, who reports in, who holds it. This skill does not restate it.
- [If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f) (Notion) — the FAQ, and the only source anything client-facing from this seat or from the seats under it can come from.
- [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) — before anything drawn from this seat goes anywhere external.

## Who owns what
This seat owns **campaign QA** — the quality gate on a live campaign. It does not own the campaign. Day-to-day is [Campaign Coordinator](../../handbook/seats/campaign-coordinator.md), reporting into [VP Campaigns](../../handbook/seats/vp-campaigns.md), who owns the Campaign SOP; **Campaign Coordinator is not VP Campaigns**, and **CMO does not own campaign day-to-day** either. Use [run-campaign](../run-campaign/SKILL.md) for the campaign itself and keep the QA pass separate from running the work.

Reporting into this seat: [Head of Author Success](../../handbook/seats/head-of-author-success.md) — run it with [run-author-success](../run-author-success/SKILL.md) — and [Client Success](../../handbook/seats/client-success.md) underneath that, run with [run-client-success](../run-client-success/SKILL.md).

## Steps
1. Open [handbook/seats/cco.md](../../handbook/seats/cco.md) and confirm the request actually belongs to this seat — author success, campaign QA, or retreat experience. If it belongs to a seat under it, hand it down; if it belongs to a marketing, sales, or campaign seat, hand it across.
2. For anything client-facing, open the [If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f) FAQ page first. Drafts come from [author-support-reply](../author-support-reply/SKILL.md); pre-call briefs come from [support-call-prep](../support-call-prep/SKILL.md). This skill writes neither.
3. Check status against [handbook/clients.md](../../handbook/clients.md), never a folder name and never HubSpot (HubSpot is closed). As of the 18 Aug 2026 extract: 1 Alumni, 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect.
4. **Cornelia Choe is our 1 Alumni.** Her Thu 20 Aug 2026, 11:00–11:30am America/Denver meeting is **support, not a pitch.** Two Zoom URLs are on file and unresolved — flag both to Michael; do not pick one.
5. Own it or escalate it, and say which: **own it** when the answer is pointing at the right seat page, running the QA pass, or naming a gap as missing. **Escalate to Michael** when the answer needs a send, a number the ladder leaves open, a client's status changed, or a Zoom URL chosen — see [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
6. Where a fact isn't sourced, write it down as missing — "not recorded" — the way [handbook/seats/cco.md](../../handbook/seats/cco.md) already names its own vacancy. Named missing stays named.
7. Run the result through [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), or the [last-gate-then-stop](../last-gate-then-stop/SKILL.md) skill, and then stop.

## No scores, no numbers
The public CCO and customer-success templates this skill borrows its shape from ship health scores, churn-risk tiers, gross- and net-retention thresholds, ARR-per-CSM ratios, and expansion plays. **None of that is imported.** Platformology has no health score, no churn tier, and no retention metric written down anywhere in this repo, and the KPI "Current" stays empty by design — see [handbook/clients.md](../../handbook/clients.md). If asked for an author-health number, the honest answer is that there isn't one. Prices come only from [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md), both numbers when the ladder shows two.
