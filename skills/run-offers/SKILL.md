# Run offers

Reused from: [narrative-io/narrative-skills-marketplace](https://github.com/narrative-io/narrative-skills-marketplace), `docs/authoring-skills.md` ("never duplicate" the reference in the skill body), [dzhng/skills](https://github.com/dzhng/skills), `skills/authoring/write-skills/SKILL.md` ("keep a single source of truth"), and [dswh/company-os](https://github.com/dswh/company-os), `brain/offers.md` ("never quote a price not written here") — the same point-and-stop shape already used by [run-sales-menu](../run-sales-menu/SKILL.md) and [run-money-moves](../run-money-moves/SKILL.md).

## The gate

One page holds the public prices, and this skill restates none of it:

- [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md) — the public price ladder. **Every number comes from there, in the words it is written in.**

What can be sold is a separate question with a separate source: the live line items live on the Sales Menu in Notion, reached through [run-sales-menu](../run-sales-menu/SKILL.md). This skill does not carry a list of offers either.

## Steps

1. Open [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md) before answering. Not memory, not a deck, not last quarter's proposal.
2. Find the row for the offer being asked about and quote it exactly as written. **Public prices only** — if it is not on that ladder, you do not have a price.
3. **Where a rung is stated two ways, quote both, in the order the ladder writes them.** Multi-author, Platform in a Box, Book Marketing Uncovery, and Digital each carry two public figures. Bestseller Uncovery carries one figure told two ways — quote both tellings. Never average the two, and never pick one to look cheap or to look premium; picking is Michael's call, per [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
4. **Platinum Major List has no public price.** Say exactly that. Do not back into a figure from the rung below it, from a bonus, or from what a client "probably" paid.
5. **The lead magnet has no Offers row, and the membership price is not published.** Both are named missing in [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md). The answer is "not recorded — check with Michael," not the nearest rung's number.
6. If the ask is for a rung that isn't there — a bundle, a tier, a discount, a payment plan, a "starting at" — the answer is **"check with Michael,"** not a new rung. Nobody invents an offer here.
7. If the ask is really "which rung does this client belong on," that is a placement: open the Sales Menu through [run-sales-menu](../run-sales-menu/SKILL.md), and read the single row correctly with [place-on-offer-ladder](../place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md).
8. Quoting is not sending. Before a price or an offer answer goes to a client, run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md).

## Where this sits

Three skills share this job and none of them duplicates the others: [run-sales-menu](../run-sales-menu/SKILL.md) is the menu side (which rungs exist), [place-on-offer-ladder](../place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md) is the mechanics of reading one row, and this skill is the handbook side — the pointer to where the numbers live and what to say when there isn't one. Placing is job 3 of the five in [handbook/how-money-moves.md](../../handbook/how-money-moves.md). Head of Demand, Head of Sales, and SDR are leftover-file assigned — who fills each is not recorded, leftover already written; whoever answers a price question today does it without a live job under them, and that is not a reason to invent a pipeline, a holder, or a job.

## Never

- Never restate the price ladder in this file or in any new page — link [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md), don't copy it.
- Never quote a price that isn't on that ladder.
- Never collapse a rung stated two ways into one number or one telling.
- Never publish a price for Platinum Major List, the lead magnet, or membership.
- Never invent a rung, tier, bundle, discount, payment plan, or "starting at" figure.
- Never invent a client, a deal, or a person to make an offer answer land.
- Never send a price without Michael. See [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
