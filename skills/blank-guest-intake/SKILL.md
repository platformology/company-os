# Blank guest intake

Reused from: [neurawork-git/n8n-autopilot](https://github.com/neurawork-git/n8n-autopilot), `skills/stack-intake/SKILL.md` ("never invent requirements they didn't state — mark genuine gaps as `OPEN:`"), and [lavidrori0702/tableau-dashboard-creator-skill](https://github.com/lavidrori0702/tableau-dashboard-creator-skill), `skills/tableau-intake/SKILL.md` (required-vs-optional field table; don't complicate a form with sections nobody asked for).

## The gate
The form lives in Notion: [Blank guest intake draft](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b812486c8eac90777658c). This repo does not copy its fields, because the fields are not sourced here — open the Notion page to see what's actually on it.

## What this is not
**Sales applications are not this form.** Don't route a prospect's application through the guest intake draft, and don't treat it as a stand-in for the Sales Menu or the offer ladder — see [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md).

## Steps
1. Open the [Blank guest intake draft](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b812486c8eac90777658c) in Notion before doing anything — this repo does not restate its contents.
2. Named missing, until the Notion page says otherwise: dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, and incident fields. Treat these as genuinely unanswered — not blanks to fill from a guess or from a comparable retreat's form.
3. If asked to add a field, don't invent one. Say the field isn't on the form yet and flag it to [VP Retreats](../../handbook/seats/vp-retreats.md) / Michael.
4. If asked what a guest's dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, or incident info is, and it isn't already recorded on that guest's completed intake, say so — don't guess based on a "typical" guest.
5. Before anything drawn from this form goes anywhere external, run it through [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md).

## Never
- Never invent a field this form doesn't have.
- Never fill a dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, or incident field from a guess.
- Never treat a sales application as this form, or this form as a sales application.
- Never send anything drawn from this form without Michael. See [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
