## The gate
The seat page is [handbook/seats/social-media-strategist.md](../../handbook/seats/social-media-strategist.md), and this skill does not restate it.

**This seat is unassigned.** There is no holder, no handle, no channel, no brand book, and no live job for it to run today. That is the whole honest state of the seat. It reports to [CMO](../../handbook/seats/cmo.md) on paper. [Social Account Expert](../../handbook/seats/social-account-expert.md) reports in on paper and is unassigned too.

This is not Social Account Expert, CMO, Head of Content, or VP Campaigns. The line against Account Expert is not recorded. Do not draw it.

## If you were sent here for work
There is none assigned. Say that plainly.

That is not a lookup failure or a page that hasn't been written yet — the seat has no accountabilities recorded because no source records any, and this repo does not write them in ahead of a source. Nothing here needs to be filled in, and no calendar needs to be produced to make the answer look complete.

## Steps
1. Open [handbook/seats/social-media-strategist.md](../../handbook/seats/social-media-strategist.md) before answering anything.
2. Asked who owns social strategy? The seat reports into [CMO](../../handbook/seats/cmo.md) and is unassigned — see [run-cmo](../run-cmo/SKILL.md). Don't hand the work to the CMO, to Head of Content, to VP Campaigns, or to Account Expert.
3. Asked for a content calendar, channel mix, cadence, pillar set, or theme set? There is no handle, no channel, and no brand book. Say so. Do not sketch a first version.
4. Asked which platforms we should be on? Not recorded, and not this seat's to decide. No sixth tool gets added. Slack is connected and no seat's job sources it — not a social channel.
5. Asked for a measurement plan, a growth target, or a KPI? No KPI is named for this seat. Naming the metric names the plan. Point at [handbook/leftovers/social-media-strategist.md](../../handbook/leftovers/social-media-strategist.md).
6. Asked whether this seat or Account Expert owns something? The line is not recorded. Say that instead of picking.
7. Asked who should fill the seat? Not recorded, and not this skill's guess — see [handbook/people.md](../../handbook/people.md). Jhana and Bob have no recorded Role and don't get assigned one here.
8. If anything about this seat does go out, run it through [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) first.

## Never
- Never name a Social Media Strategist. The seat is unassigned.
- Never write a content calendar, channel mix, cadence, pillar set, theme set, or campaign concept.
- Never invent a handle, a channel, an audience, a brand book, or a KPI.
- Never draw the line against Social Account Expert.
- Never add a sixth tool, and never treat Slack as a social channel.
- Never quietly reassign this seat's work to the CMO, Head of Content, VP Campaigns, or Account Expert.
- Never send anything from this seat without Michael. No program GitHub. See [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
