## If you were sent here for work
There is no live job. Open the leftover already written. Say that plainly.

That is not a lookup failure or a page that hasn't been written yet — the seat has no accountabilities recorded because no source records any, and this repo does not write them in ahead of a source. Nothing here needs to be filled in, and no number needs to be produced to make the answer look complete.

## Steps
1. Open [handbook/leftovers/head-of-demand.md](../../handbook/leftovers/head-of-demand.md), then [handbook/seats/head-of-demand.md](../../handbook/seats/head-of-demand.md), before answering anything.
2. Asked who owns demand? The seat reports into [CMO](../../handbook/seats/cmo.md) and is leftover-file assigned — who fills it is not recorded, there is no live job — see [run-cmo](../run-cmo/SKILL.md). Don't hand the work to the CMO, to Sales, or to whoever is nearest.
3. Asked for inbound volume, lead count, conversion rate, pipeline, or a channel breakdown? There is no number. Say so; don't estimate, benchmark, or carry a figure over from another seat's page.
4. Asked to score, rank, or qualify a lead? Run [campaign-lead-scoring](../campaign-lead-scoring/SKILL.md) — the corpus has no scoring model either.
5. Asked to build a demand plan, funnel, channel mix, or lead magnet offer? That's a decision for Michael, not a draft this skill produces. Flag the request. The lead magnet in particular has **no Offers row** — see [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md) — and does not get a price.
6. Asked who should fill the seat? Not recorded, and not this skill's guess — see [handbook/people.md](../../handbook/people.md). Jhana and Bob have no recorded Role and don't get assigned one here.
7. If anything about this seat does go out, run it through [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) first.

## Never
- Never name a Head of Demand. Who fills is not recorded. The seat is leftover-file assigned.
- Never invent an inbound volume, lead count, MQL/SQL number, conversion rate, pipeline figure, or channel.
- Never invent an accountability, KPI, or target for this seat, and never copy one in from a public demand-gen template.
- Never invent a persona, an ICP, a campaign, or a line of copy to give this seat something to do.
- Never price the lead magnet or add a row to [handbook/offers.md](../../handbook/offers.md).
- Never quietly reassign this seat's work to the CMO, Head of Content, or Head of Sales — [Head of Sales](../../handbook/seats/head-of-sales.md) and [SDR](../../handbook/seats/sdr.md) are leftover-file assigned too, who fills each not recorded, no live job.
- Never send anything from this seat without Michael. No program GitHub. See [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
