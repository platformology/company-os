# Run the Controller seat

Reused from: [narrative-io/narrative-skills-marketplace](https://github.com/narrative-io/narrative-skills-marketplace), `docs/authoring-skills.md` ("never duplicate" the reference in the skill body), and [dzhng/skills](https://github.com/dzhng/skills), `skills/authoring/write-skills/SKILL.md` ("keep a single source of truth").

## The gate
[handbook/seats/controller.md](../../handbook/seats/controller.md) is the seat. This skill points at it and does not restate it. The seat owns one thing: **the monthly close.** It reports to [CFO](../../handbook/seats/cfo.md) — see the [run-cfo](../run-cfo/SKILL.md) skill.

**The seat holder is not recorded** — see [handbook/people.md](../../handbook/people.md).

## The fact you carry forward
**June and July 2026 are not closed.**

That is the sourced status, and it is the answer. It is not a gap to paper over with an estimate, a projection, a "substantially complete," or a date by which it will be done. If someone needs more than "not closed" — a partial close, a reason, a target date — that is a named missing to raise with Michael, not something you supply.

## Steps
1. Open [handbook/seats/controller.md](../../handbook/seats/controller.md) before answering anything about the close.
2. Asked whether a month is closed? For June and July 2026 the answer is **not closed**, stated plainly. For any other month, this repo does not record a status — say so rather than inferring one from the months that are recorded.
3. **Do not invent a close.** No close date, no closing entries, no reconciliation, no trial balance, no month-end figure. None of that is sourced in this repo, and there is no system of record here to pull it from.
4. **KPI "Current" stays empty.** That field has no value in it, and 0 is not the value. The close does not invent a Current number to reconcile against, and neither do you. The client counts are a different fact and stay where they belong: 0 Current and 0 Prospect on the 18 Aug 2026 extract in [handbook/clients.md](../../handbook/clients.md), quoted with that date.
5. **Blue Sky stays off the LLC books.** Platformology LLC is the legal entity; Promote a Book and Book Retreat are DBAs of it, not separate entities. Blue Sky sits outside that boundary. See [handbook/company-facts.md](../../handbook/company-facts.md) and [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
6. Ledger questions go down to [Bookkeeper](../../handbook/seats/bookkeeper.md) ([run-bookkeeper](../run-bookkeeper/SKILL.md)); process questions go up to [CFO](../../handbook/seats/cfo.md) and the [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81) Notion page ([run-cfo](../run-cfo/SKILL.md)).
7. Before anything from this seat goes anywhere external, run it through [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) — or the [last-gate-then-stop](../last-gate-then-stop/SKILL.md) skill — and stop.

## Never
- Never report June or July 2026 as closed, closing, or nearly closed. Not closed is the fact.
- Never invent a close date, a closing entry, a reconciliation, a trial balance, or a month-end number.
- Never invent an invoice, an accounts-receivable figure, or a savings total to make a month tie out.
- Never fill KPI "Current" with a number.
- Never let Blue Sky touch the LLC books.
- Never restate the seat page or the [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81) Notion page here — link them, don't copy them.
- Never name a person into this seat. Not recorded means not recorded.
- Never send anything from this seat without Michael. No program GitHub. See [handbook/locks.md](../../handbook/locks.md).
