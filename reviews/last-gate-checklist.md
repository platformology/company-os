# Last-gate checklist

Run this before anything — an email, a Sales Menu quote, a Notion update, a repo change — leaves your hands. Every line is pass/fail. No partial credit, no "close enough."

- [ ] **Michael signed off.** Nothing external ships without Michael. (See [handbook/locks.md](../handbook/locks.md).)
- [ ] **Every price traces to [handbook/offers.md](../handbook/offers.md).** If the ladder shows two numbers, both are in your draft, or neither is — you did not pick one.
- [ ] **Every client fact traces to [handbook/clients.md](../handbook/clients.md).** Hoffman is not Current. HubSpot is not a source. Folder names are not clients.
- [ ] **No invented person, role, or title.** Jhana and Bob have no Role in this repo — if your draft gives them one, delete it.
- [ ] **No invented Current number, revenue figure, or offer** for Cornelia Choe or any other account.
- [ ] **Named missing stays named.** If a fact is a gap listed in [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md) — live Uncovery script, membership price, PIAB cycle time, lead magnet row, Jhana/Bob Role, production stack, studio address, the unresolved Zoom URLs — your draft says "not recorded," not a guess.
- [ ] **No program repo touched.** Nothing here references, links to, or was drafted from `smarter-voice`, `academic-research-platform`, `book-campaign-platform`, `styleguide-os`, `persona-standalone-programs`, or `tlpF-b01-research-engine`.
- [ ] **One clear next step.** The reader knows what happens after this lands.
- [ ] **You'd hand this to Anthony, Jhana, or Bob and stand behind it out loud.** If you wouldn't say it to their face, don't ship it.

If any box is unchecked, it doesn't ship. Fix the draft or ask Michael — do not soften the checklist to make the draft pass.
