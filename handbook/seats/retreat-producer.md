Shape: Holacracy Role — [holacracyone/Holacracy-Constitution](https://github.com/holacracyone/Holacracy-Constitution), `Holacracy-Constitution.md`. Seat tracked separately from seat holder — [JGalego/TeamAPI](https://github.com/JGalego/TeamAPI), `docs/spec/teamapi-extended-v1.md`. **Retreat Producer is not VP Retreats — they are two seats.** [VP Retreats](vp-retreats.md) owns [Retreat SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8185b9a9f19dd56389e5) and [PIAB SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81d5beecda81a111e6b3) (Notion). Retreat Producer reports into that seat and produces each retreat.

**Purpose:** Produce each Book Retreat, start to finish.

**Owns:**
- Running the "A retreat is running" step of the [weekday loop](../weekday-loop.md), per [Retreat SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8185b9a9f19dd56389e5) (Notion) — this repo does not restate the SOP.
- The uncollapsed track-record numbers tied to the Book Retreat era, in [company-facts.md](../company-facts.md): 128 (Book Retreat era), later 130–131 — quoted as-is, never averaged.

**Does not invent:** a retreat date, a guest list, or a venue. None of those is sourced in this repo — check [Retreat SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8185b9a9f19dd56389e5) and the live guest records in Notion, don't fill in a plausible one.

**Guest details:** Dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, and incident fields stay named missing — see the [blank-guest-intake](../../skills/blank-guest-intake/SKILL.md) skill and the [Blank guest intake draft](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b812486c8eac90777658c) (Notion).

**Reports to:** [VP Retreats](vp-retreats.md).

**Seat holder:** Not recorded — see [people.md](../people.md).
