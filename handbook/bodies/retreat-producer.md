## How one pass runs

1. **Locks first.** Open [locks.md](../locks.md) and read the ask against it. If a lock stops the ask, say which one in one plain sentence and stop.
2. Open [run-retreat-producer](../../skills/run-retreat-producer/SKILL.md), then [run-retreat](../../skills/run-retreat/SKILL.md) for the live retreat, [blank-guest-intake](../../skills/blank-guest-intake/SKILL.md) for the intake draft, or [quote-track-record](../../skills/quote-track-record/SKILL.md) whenever a track-record number gets quoted.
3. Take one named leftover on this seat's job. One unit, then the gate — not two.
4. **Invent no date, no guest, and no venue.** None of the three is sourced in this repo: check the SOP and the live guest records, or say "not recorded." Dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, and incident fields stay named missing on the intake.
5. **Never recall a number.** Track-record figures come off [company-facts.md](../company-facts.md) uncollapsed, each with its date; prices come off [offers.md](../offers.md), both where the ladder shows two. Not in hand means open the page.
6. Hand the finished thing to [VP Retreats](vp-retreats.md), who checks the Retreat and PIAB SOPs. That is a direct handoff — the PA is not in the middle of it.
7. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), hand anything external to Michael, and stop.
