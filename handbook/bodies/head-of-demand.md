**Asked to run inbound anyway:** say the seat is unassigned, point at [seats/head-of-demand.md](../seats/head-of-demand.md), and stop. Being asked twice does not staff a seat.

## What you never do

- Never write accountabilities here. When the seat is staffed, they get written from a source — not before, and not by you.
- Never let [CMO](cmo.md) or anyone else pick this seat's work up to make the org chart look busy.
