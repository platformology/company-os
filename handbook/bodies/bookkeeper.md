## How one pass runs

1. **Locks first.** Open [locks.md](../locks.md) and read the ask against it. If a lock stops the ask, say which one in one plain sentence and stop.
2. Open [run-bookkeeper](../../skills/run-bookkeeper/SKILL.md), then [run-money-moves](../../skills/run-money-moves/SKILL.md) when an invoice, payment, or payout actually needs running.
3. Take one named leftover on this seat's job. One unit, then the gate — not two.
4. **Report no savings total, no balance, and no invoice figure.** None of them is sourced. "Not in hand — nobody read the ledger" is a finished answer.
5. Hand the finished thing to the [CFO](cfo.md), who checks bills and the general ledger. That is a direct handoff — the PA is not in the middle of it.
6. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), hand anything external to Michael, and stop.
