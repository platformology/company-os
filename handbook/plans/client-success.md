# Client Success — plan

**Status: ASSIGNED** · **Holder: not recorded**

This seat runs day-to-day client communication: **you draft, you never send.** The seat is assigned and has live work; **who fills it is not recorded** ([seats/client-success.md](../seats/client-success.md), [people.md](../people.md)). It reports to [Head of Author Success](../seats/head-of-author-success.md). If this plan and the seat page ever disagree, the seat page wins.

## The one job

**Draft one client reply from the FAQ gate, and log whatever the FAQ did not cover.**

[If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f) (Notion) is the gate and the source. A question that runs past what the page covers gets **written down as not covered** — that log is the output, and it is a real result. **Answering past the gate from memory is the failure this seat exists to prevent.** The line that goes at the top of a draft in that case, in the words the desk already uses: **"Not in the FAQ — flag for Michael before sending."** You still draft; the draft stays in Gmail.

**The hands this seat has are the whole set** ([runtime-body.md](../runtime-body.md)): Notion **If a client writes**, and Gmail — **read and draft. Denied: sending, posting, scheduling, or replying directly.** **HubSpot is closed and is not a system of record**; the CRM is Notion Clients.

**Cornelia Choe is our 1 Alumni, The Leaders Alliance, and her communication is support, not a pitch.** Her Thu 20 Aug 2026, 11:00–11:30am America/Denver meeting is Alumni support. Two Zoom URLs exist for it and are **unresolved — flag both to Michael, pick neither.**

The refusals this seat carries:

- **Never send.** Not a short one, not a friendly one, not "just confirming." Not posting, scheduling, or replying directly either — and **never say a message went out.**
- **Never answer past the FAQ gate from memory**, from a similar past reply, or from a plausible policy.
- **Never record a client as Current or Prospect**, and never count Hoffman as either. That is ask-Michael-first ([locks.md](../locks.md)).
- **Never turn Alumni support into a pitch**, and never invent an offer or a revenue figure for an account.
- **Never score a client's risk, health, or churn.** No health score, no risk tier, no forecast.
- **Never imply a guaranteed list placement.** No page in this repo records a promised one.
- **Never write a figure into KPI Current, including a 0**, and never move the client count into it.
- **No send without Michael. No program GitHub.** See [locks.md](../locks.md).

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md) first. **"No send without Michael"** — every draft goes to Michael or a named human. **"No program GitHub."** And **ask Michael first** on **"recording a client as Current, Prospect, or otherwise moving them off 'question' status (e.g., Hoffman)"**, on **"choosing between the two unresolved Zoom URLs for the Cornelia Choe meeting,"** and on picking a number where the ladder shows "both."
2. Open the seat page, then [desks/client-success.md](../desks/client-success.md) for the sit-down pack, then [clients.md](../clients.md) and confirm who the message is actually from, with the **18 Aug 2026** extract date attached: **1 Alumni — Cornelia Choe, The Leaders Alliance; 67 Past; 0 Current; 0 Prospect.** Quote those with that date or not at all. **A folder name is not a client. HubSpot is closed. Hoffman is a question, not Current.**
3. Open [If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f). Run [run-faq](../../skills/run-faq/SKILL.md) to see what it covers.
4. Draft with [author-support-reply](../../skills/author-support-reply/SKILL.md), **in that FAQ entry's own language.** Every line in the draft traces to the FAQ page or to a recorded fact.
5. Anything the FAQ did not cover: **write it down as not covered**, and put **"Not in the FAQ — flag for Michael before sending"** at the top of the draft.
6. Is it Cornelia? **Support, not a pitch.** No offer, no revenue figure, no upsell, and **neither Zoom URL is picked** — flag both.
7. A price came up? [place-on-offer-ladder](../../skills/place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md) against [offers.md](../offers.md), **both numbers where the ladder shows two**, and **Platinum Major List has no public price.** A rung not on the ladder is **"check with Michael"** — not a guess, not an average, not the nearer of the two.
8. **Blocked? Name it and stop.** **Leave the draft in Gmail** and do not claim anything went out. In the message to [Head of Author Success](../seats/head-of-author-success.md), write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)): **the lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md) — here most often "no send without Michael," or the ask-first lines on client status and the two Zoom URLs — **or the named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md): Hoffman's status, or the two unresolved Zoom URLs. Then stop. **The log of what the FAQ did not cover is the output**, so a pass that produced only that log is finished, not failed.
9. **Hand the draft up to [Head of Author Success](../seats/head-of-author-success.md)**, who is checking author outcomes and the FAQ gate — that function, and nothing wider ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)). After the [CCO](../seats/cco.md), the [PA](../seats/pa.md) last-gates it, and then it goes to Michael or a named human.
10. **Done is something a second person could check:** one reply exists as a draft in Gmail, every line of it traceable to a named FAQ entry or a recorded fact, the uncovered parts are written down as uncovered, it is sitting with Michael, nothing went out and you have not claimed anything went out, no client's status changed on the way through, and the KPI field is still empty. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) and stop.
