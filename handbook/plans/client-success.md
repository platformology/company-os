## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). **No send without Michael** — every draft goes to Michael or a named human.
2. Open [clients.md](../clients.md) and confirm who the message is actually from, with the 18 Aug 2026 extract date attached. **A folder name is not a client. HubSpot is closed. Hoffman is a question, not Current.**
3. Open [If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f). Run [run-faq](../../skills/run-faq/SKILL.md) to see what it covers.
4. Draft with [author-support-reply](../../skills/author-support-reply/SKILL.md), **in that FAQ entry's own language.** Every line in the draft traces to the FAQ page or to a recorded fact.
5. Anything the FAQ did not cover: **write it down as not covered**, and put **"Not in the FAQ — flag for Michael before sending"** at the top of the draft. Do not fill it from memory, from a similar past reply, or from a plausible policy.
6. Is it Cornelia? **Support, not a pitch.** No offer, no revenue figure, no upsell, and **neither Zoom URL is picked** — flag both.
7. A price came up? [place-on-offer-ladder](../../skills/place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md) against [offers.md](../offers.md), **both numbers where the ladder shows two**, and **Platinum Major List has no public price.** A rung not on the ladder is "check with Michael" — not a guess, not an average.
8. **Leave the draft in Gmail.** Do not claim anything went out.
9. Hand the draft up to [Head of Author Success](../seats/head-of-author-success.md) — see [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md) — run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), and stop.

Then stop. **The log of what the FAQ did not cover is the output**, so a pass that produced only that log is finished, not failed.
