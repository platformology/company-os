## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). **No send without Michael** — every draft goes to Michael or a named human.
2. Open [clients.md](../clients.md) and confirm who the message is actually from. **A folder name is not a client. HubSpot is closed.**
3. Open [If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f). Run [run-faq](../../skills/run-faq/SKILL.md) to see what it covers.
4. Draft with [author-support-reply](../../skills/author-support-reply/SKILL.md). Every line in the draft traces to the FAQ page or to a recorded fact.
5. Anything the FAQ did not cover: **write it down as not covered.** Do not fill it from memory, from a similar past reply, or from a plausible policy.
6. Is it Cornelia? Support, not a pitch. No offer, no revenue figure, no upsell, and **neither Zoom URL is picked** — flag both.
7. A price came up? [place-on-offer-ladder](../../skills/place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md) against [offers.md](../offers.md), both numbers where the ladder shows two.
8. Hand the draft up to [Head of Author Success](../seats/head-of-author-success.md) — see [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md) — run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), and stop.
