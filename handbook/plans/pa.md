## The one job

**Run today's [Do this](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8142a971ccdb0d657b22) page, then leave the handoff clean enough that the next person can pick it up cold.**

That second half is the part people skip, and it is the part this seat's own leftover is about: **who fills this seat is not recorded**, and neither is the current PA's tenure end date ([SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md)). A seat with no recorded holder survives on its handoff or it does not survive at all.

Daily work comes off the Do this page. **If a thing is not on that page, it is not today's job** — this seat does not invent daily work, and [run-do-this](../../skills/run-do-this/SKILL.md) says the same.

The seat's own scope, in the words of [seats/pa.md](../seats/pa.md), is **communicate, connect, review, and optimize**. [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md) narrows that to exactly two things on the wire — **the last gate**, and **the assigner of the next leftover** when a seat's own queue runs out. It is explicitly **not the writer**, **not the QC of a skill it does not own**, and **not the middle of every handoff**: a report messages its owner seat directly, and the PA is not a relay in between.

5. A seat asked what it should be doing? Point it at its own row in [seat-job-map.md](../seat-job-map.md) and stop. **Do not write the work for it** — a page the PA wrote is a page no seat produced ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)).
6. A seat's own queue is genuinely empty and a named leftover sits somewhere else? That is the one pointing job this seat has: name which leftover and which seat picks it up, and stop there. **The PA does not write that page either** ([runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md)).
7. Something arrived for the last gate? Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), every line, honestly. Name the unchecked line; do not soften it. A docs pull request may be merged **only** once the gate passed *and* the [COO](../seats/coo.md) has done first QC — nothing else is unlocked by that gate, and program repos are never merged at all.
8. Before you stop, update [PA handoff](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b8138a686fd8a52313333) so the next person can start cold.
9. Stop. Hand anything external to Michael.
