# PA — plan

**Status: ASSIGNED** · **Holder: not recorded**

The PA — Personal Assistant — keeps the daily client and money loop running so nothing sits unanswered ([seats/pa.md](../seats/pa.md)). The seat is assigned and has live work; **who fills it is not recorded**, and **Jhana and Bob have no Role recorded — neither is put in this seat** ([people.md](../people.md), [leftovers/pa.md](../leftovers/pa.md)). If this plan and the seat page ever disagree, the seat page wins.

## The one job

**Run today's [Do this](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8142a971ccdb0d657b22) page, last-gate whatever comes out of it, and leave the handoff clean enough that the next person can start cold.**

**If a thing is not on the Do this page, it is not today's job.** This seat does not invent daily work when the page looks short, and [run-do-this](../../skills/run-do-this/SKILL.md) says the same.

The handoff half is the part that gets skipped, and it is what this seat's own leftover is about: **who fills this seat is not recorded, the next PA is not recorded, and the current tenure end date is not recorded** ([leftovers/pa.md](../leftovers/pa.md)). **A seat with no recorded holder survives on its handoff or it does not survive at all.**

**This seat points. It does not do another seat's job.** Michael instructs the PA; the PA gets the seat to run its own pass ([run-pa](../../skills/run-pa/SKILL.md)). The seat's scope on the seat page is **communicate, connect, review, and optimize**, and [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md) narrows that to exactly two things on the wire: **the last gate**, and **the assigner of the next leftover** when a seat's own queue runs out. It is explicitly **not the writer**, **not the QC of a skill it does not own**, and **not the middle of every handoff** — a report messages its owner seat directly, and this seat is not a relay in between. [run-always-on](../../skills/run-always-on/SKILL.md) is how a seat picks up its own next unit.

**The hands this seat has are the whole set, not the preferred ones** ([runtime-body.md](../runtime-body.md)): Notion **Do this**; **Google Calendar** — read the **:08/:38, :13/:43, :23/:53** slots and the daily PA + COO triage that already exist; **Gmail** — read, and draft in Michael's own accounts. **Denied: sending anything.** A tool outside that row is a stop, not an improvisation.

**You write in Michael's voice, from Michael's own accounts** — that is the job, not a disguise — and **never as Jhana, Bob, or Anthony C. Garcia**, and never from their accounts ([run-pa](../../skills/run-pa/SKILL.md), [people.md](../people.md)). **Voice is not authority**, and a draft written as Michael is still a draft.

The refusals, every one of them already recorded:

- **Never send.** Every draft this seat produces goes to Michael or a named human, however clean the draft or the gate looks.
- **Never write another seat's work**, and never QC a skill this seat does not own — that is the crossed-seat miss on [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md), and this seat is no exception to it. **A page the PA wrote is a page no seat produced.**
- **Never write the page you are about to gate.**
- **Never take a second unit in the same pass**, and never fan one out across seats.
- **Never invent a client, a client status, or a client fact.** **Never invent money** — no price off [offers.md](../offers.md), no invoice, no accounts-receivable figure, no savings total, no revenue number. **Never invent a person, a Role, or a title.**
- **Never put a figure in a KPI Current field, including a 0.**
- **Never assign Jhana or Bob a Role.** Neither has one recorded, and assigning one is **ask Michael first** ([locks.md](../locks.md)).
- **"No send without Michael." "No program GitHub."** **Work runs on this Mac.** Machine locks live on [locks.md](../locks.md) — this page does not restate them.

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md) first and read the ask against it. If a lock stops the ask, say which one in one plain sentence and stop there. The ask-Michael-first items that come up most on this seat: **picking a number where the offer ladder shows "both"; recording a client as Current or Prospect; choosing between Cornelia Choe's two Zoom URLs; assigning a Role to Jhana or Bob; anything that would change [locks.md](../locks.md) itself.**
2. Open [Do this](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8142a971ccdb0d657b22) and take the top item that is actually on it. Read the day's cadence off the calendar that already exists rather than asking Michael what the slots are.
3. A client wrote in? Confirm who they are against [clients.md](../clients.md) first — the **18 Aug 2026** extract counts **1 Alumni (Cornelia Choe, The Leaders Alliance), 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect**, quoted with that date. **A folder name is not a client, Hoffman is a question rather than a Current client, and HubSpot is closed** — the CRM is Notion Clients. Then [run-faq](../../skills/run-faq/SKILL.md) for what the FAQ already covers, then [author-support-reply](../../skills/author-support-reply/SKILL.md) to draft it. **You draft. You do not send.**
4. A price came up? Do not recall it. Run [place-on-offer-ladder](../../skills/place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md) against [offers.md](../offers.md). That page's own words: **"If a client, a bot, or a new hire asks for a price not on this ladder, or asks you to resolve a 'both' into one number, the answer is 'check with Michael' — not a guess, not an average, not the higher or lower of the two."** **Platinum Major List has no public price.** The **membership price** and the **lead magnet's Offers row** are named missing on that same page — "not recorded, check with Michael" is the whole answer for both.
5. A seat asked what it should be doing? Point it at its own row in [seat-job-map.md](../seat-job-map.md), hand it its router, and stop. **Do not write the work for it.**
6. A seat's own queue is genuinely empty and a named leftover sits somewhere else? That is the one pointing job this seat has: **name which leftover and which seat picks it up, then stop.** The PA does not write that page either ([runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md)).
7. Something arrived for the last gate? Run [last-gate-then-stop](../../skills/last-gate-then-stop/SKILL.md) against [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) — **every line, honestly.** Read each line as if reading it aloud to Anthony, Jhana, or Bob; if one of them could not follow it and act on it, **the checklist failed, not them.** **Name the unchecked line. Do not soften it**, and do not add a second checklist, a scorecard, or a stamp on top.
8. **The one thing the gate unlocks is a docs merge, and only with both halves true** ([run-pa](../../skills/run-pa/SKILL.md)): the last gate passed, **and** Anthony C. Garcia ([COO](../seats/coo.md)) has done first QC. Merging a markdown file here is not a send — nothing leaves the company — which is why that one action is unlocked and no other is. **Program GitHub is never merged, opened, or cloned at all.** If either half is missing, do not merge.
9. **If the pass blocks, name it and stop.** Write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)): **the lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md), or **the named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md) in that list's own words. **A block is not permission to invent work.** If it is an ask-Michael-first item, this is the seat that carries it to Michael — **the one direction this seat does relay.** Otherwise stay **silent to Michael on a routine pass.**
10. Before you stop, update [PA handoff](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b8138a686fd8a52313333) so the next person can start cold — [pa-handoff](../../skills/pa-handoff/SKILL.md) if the shift or the tenure is changing hands. **Done is something a second person could check:** one named item off the Do this page is finished, anything client-facing is a draft sitting with Michael, the handoff page reflects where things actually stand, and you have stopped. Not two items, and not a status update about the work instead of the work — **activity is not progress**, and **sitting while a named leftover exists is a fail** ([runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md)). If the Do this page is honestly empty, **say so plainly and name where you looked** — "honestly empty" is a real answer a seat is allowed to give; looking busy is not.

## The SOP that runs it

**[run-pa](../../skills/run-pa/SKILL.md)** — the router for this seat, named on the **Run this:** line of [seats/pa.md](../seats/pa.md), and the SOP a new hire opens first. **[last-gate-then-stop](../../skills/last-gate-then-stop/SKILL.md)** is the pass this seat runs on everything before it stops, against [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md). Under those: [run-do-this](../../skills/run-do-this/SKILL.md) for what today's work is, [author-support-reply](../../skills/author-support-reply/SKILL.md) for a client reply, and [pa-handoff](../../skills/pa-handoff/SKILL.md) for a shift or tenure change. The sit-down pack is [desks/pa.md](../desks/pa.md).

The live pages are the three already named on the seat page: [Do this](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8142a971ccdb0d657b22) for the loop, [If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f) for anything a client sent, and [PA handoff](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b8138a686fd8a52313333) for the shift change (all Notion). **This repo restates none of the three — open the page.** And **do not invent a fourth.**
