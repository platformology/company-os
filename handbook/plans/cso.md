# CSO — plan

**Status: ASSIGNED** · **Holder: not recorded**

CSO is **Chief Sales Officer**, not Chief Strategy Officer. The seat owns pipeline, offer fit, and close ([seats/cso.md](../seats/cso.md)), and **who fills it is not recorded.** One pass places a real buyer on a rung that already exists. If this plan and the seat page ever disagree, the seat page wins.

## The one job

**Take one real, sourced buyer question and answer it off the public ladder — the rung, and the price as the ladder states it.**

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md) first.
2. Open [seats/cso.md](../seats/cso.md), then the sit-down pack [desks/cso.md](../desks/cso.md).
3. Open [offers.md](../offers.md). **Every price this seat quotes comes off that page and nowhere else.**

Offer fit is matching a situation to a rung that exists. It is not building a new rung, and it is not resolving a "both" into one number. The rule on [offers.md](../offers.md), in its own words: **"If a client, a bot, or a new hire asks for a price not on this ladder, or asks you to resolve a 'both' into one number, the answer is 'check with Michael' — not a guess, not an average, not the higher or lower of the two."**

The rungs that show two numbers, so you do not have to go and look: **Multi-author $1,000 or $20,000** · **PIAB Consult, or Drive $40,000** · **Book Marketing Uncovery Consult, or Drive wire $20,000** · **Digital $35,000 or $35,000 + $40,000 bonus** · **Bestseller Uncovery $20,000**, credited toward Platinum in one telling and an additional charge in another. **Platinum Major List has no public price.** Single-number rungs: Virtual Retreat $1,000, Guided $10,000, Professional $20,000, Elite $40,000, SUCCESS $35,000 + $40,000 bonus, NYT $35,000 + $60,000 in bonuses, Book Writing Uncovery Consult.

**In hand, and that is the whole set** ([runtime-body.md](../runtime-body.md)): Notion **Sales Menu**, Notion **Clients** for status, plus [offers.md](../offers.md) and [clients.md](../clients.md) in this repo. **Denied: any pipeline tool.** Notion **Clients** is the CRM; **HubSpot is closed and is not a system of record.**

The refusals, and not one of them is a matter of taste:

- **No list guarantee.** No page in this repo records a promised list placement. A rung is a price, not a promise about an outcome, and nothing drafted off this plan implies one.
- **Both reports under this seat are leftover-file assigned**: [Head of Sales](../seats/head-of-sales.md) and [SDR](../seats/sdr.md) — who fills each is not recorded, there is no live job, leftover already written. Assigned on file is not staffed: the file exists, nobody fills the chair. **No work gets handed down to either one** to make a chart look busy.
- **No pipeline, forecast, close rate, lead count, consult count, or score gets invented** — not for this seat and not for the two under it. [runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md) is explicit that none of it gets invented so a seat has something to digest.
- **Never resolve a "both" into one number**, never quote a price that is not on [offers.md](../offers.md), and never give Platinum Major List a price.
- **Never record a client as Current or Prospect without Michael**, never move Hoffman off "question," never treat HubSpot as a source, and never read a folder name as a client.
- **Never put a figure in a KPI Current field, including a 0**, and never turn the 0 Current client count into a KPI value, a target, or a trend.
- **Never send without Michael. No program GitHub.** The full list is [locks.md](../locks.md), and a plan is not that list.

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md) and read the ask against it. The ones that bite here, in their own words: **"No send without Michael."** **"No program GitHub."** And two ask-Michael-first lines — **"picking a number where the offer ladder shows 'both'"**, and **"recording a client as Current, Prospect, or otherwise moving them off 'question' status (e.g., Hoffman)."**
2. Read the ask again. If it is really a diagnosis question — "what do I actually need?" — that is **Uncovery, job 2 on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md)**, and it comes before any placement. **Diagnose comes before Place**, and the live script for it is named missing.
3. Place the buyer on a rung that already exists: [place-on-offer-ladder](../../skills/place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md), then [run-sales-menu](../../skills/run-sales-menu/SKILL.md). Open the [Sales Menu](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b817dae2eeb55c5902ad7) — do not place from memory of it.
4. Quote the rung the way the ladder states it. **Two numbers stay two numbers. Platinum Major List has no public price** — say exactly that.
5. Asked for a rung that is not on the ladder? The answer is **"check with Michael."** Not a guess, not an average, not the nearer of two. The **membership price** has none recorded and the **lead magnet has no Offers row** at all — both are named missing on [offers.md](../offers.md) itself.
6. Asked for pipeline, a forecast, a consult count, a qualification bar, or a close rate? **None is recorded.** Say so plainly. Do not build one out of the client count, and **do not hand the ask down to [Head of Sales](../seats/head-of-sales.md) or [SDR](../seats/sdr.md)** — both are leftover-file assigned, who fills each not recorded, no live job, and neither has hands on [runtime-body.md](../runtime-body.md).
7. Anything about a client's status goes through [run-clients](../../skills/run-clients/SKILL.md), against the 18 Aug 2026 extract in [clients.md](../clients.md): **1 Alumni (Cornelia Choe, The Leaders Alliance), 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect.** Counts travel with that date or not at all. **Hoffman is a question, not a Current client. A folder name is not a client. HubSpot is closed.**
8. **Blocked? Name it and stop.** In the message to whoever is waiting, write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)): **the lock, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md)** — here, most often "no send without Michael," the ask-first line on resolving a "both," or the ask-first line on moving a client off "question" status — or **the named-missing hole, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md)** — the live Uncovery script, how an Uncovery is sold, the membership price, or Hoffman's status. **Do not route around it, and do not invent a pipeline to have something to report.** An ask-Michael-first item travels up the wire through the [PA](../seats/pa.md); you do not message Michael from this seat.
9. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) honestly, then hand the finished thing on. It goes to the [PA](../seats/pa.md), whose gate is the last one before Michael — or to the [COO](../seats/coo.md) first for QC if what you produced is a Company OS / docs change. **You do not last-gate your own work and you do not send.** No seat reports into this one today, so nothing arrives from below to check.
10. **Stop.** What a second person can check: **one buyer question answered against a named rung that exists on the ladder, priced exactly as the ladder states it** — both numbers where it shows two, "no public price" where it says so — with no seat below this one handed work it cannot take, and nothing sent. If the honest answer was "not recorded, check with Michael," that is a finished pass too.
