2. Open [clients.md](../clients.md) and establish who you are actually talking about. **A folder name is not a client.** **Hoffman is a question, not a Current client** — recording anyone as Current is ask-Michael-first.

6. **Cornelia Choe is Alumni. Her communication is support, not a pitch**, and her two Zoom URLs are unresolved — flag both to Michael, pick neither.
