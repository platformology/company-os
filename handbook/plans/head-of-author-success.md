## The one job

**Take one author's engagement and say how it is actually going, using only facts that are recorded — then hold the FAQ gate on anything that gets drafted back to them.**

Two rules make this seat honest, and both are on its seat page. **Author outcomes are checked against [clients.md](../clients.md)** — never against a folder name, and never against HubSpot, which is closed. And **[If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f) (Notion) is the only source** anything drafted from this seat, or from [Client Success](../seats/client-success.md) underneath it, can come from.

The recorded picture today, with its date attached: **1 Alumni (Cornelia Choe, The Leaders Alliance), 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect — 18 Aug 2026 extract.** That is a count, and it goes out with the date on it. **Hoffman is a question, not a Current client.**

The line to use when the FAQ runs out, in the words the desks already use: **"Not in the FAQ — flag for Michael before sending."** You still draft. You do not answer past that gate.

**No list guarantee.** No page in this repo records a promised list placement, and nothing drafted off this plan implies one.

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). **Every draft from this seat goes to Michael or a named human. Nothing sends.**
2. Open [clients.md](../clients.md) and establish who you are actually talking about, with the 18 Aug 2026 extract date attached. **A folder name is not a client. HubSpot is closed. Hoffman is a question, not a Current client** — recording anyone as Current is ask-Michael-first.
3. Asked how an engagement is going? **Answer from what is recorded, and where nothing is recorded, say nothing is recorded.** Do not reconstruct a status from a folder, a filename, or a HubSpot record.
4. A client wrote in? [run-faq](../../skills/run-faq/SKILL.md) says what the FAQ covers; [author-support-reply](../../skills/author-support-reply/SKILL.md) drafts the reply. **Log what the FAQ does not cover** rather than answering past it, and put **"Not in the FAQ — flag for Michael before sending"** at the top of the draft.
5. A call is coming? [support-call-prep](../../skills/support-call-prep/SKILL.md), off the FAQ and the calendar as they already stand. **Flag gaps rather than filling them in the brief.**
6. **Cornelia Choe is Alumni. Her communication is support, not a pitch.** Her Thu 20 Aug 2026, 11:00–11:30am America/Denver meeting is Alumni support, and her **two Zoom URLs are unresolved — flag both to Michael, pick neither.**
7. A price came up? Off [offers.md](../offers.md) only, **both numbers where the ladder shows two**, and **Platinum Major List has no public price.** Never invent an offer or a revenue figure for an account.
8. [Client Success](../seats/client-success.md) reports into this seat and hands its finished drafts here — check the function you own, author outcomes and the FAQ gate, and nothing wider. Hand your own finished work up to the [CCO](../seats/cco.md) ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)).
9. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) and stop.

## If the pass blocks

Name it and stop. In the message to the [CCO](../seats/cco.md), write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)):

- **The lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md) — here, most often "no send without Michael," or the ask-first lines on client status and the two Zoom URLs.
- **The named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md) — Hoffman's status, the two unresolved Zoom URLs, or the absent author-success SOP.

Then stop. **"Not in the FAQ" is a finished answer** once it is written down; it is not a reason to answer from memory.

- Never answer past what the FAQ covers; log the gap instead.
- Never turn Alumni support into a pitch, and never invent an offer or a revenue figure for an account.
- Never imply a guaranteed list placement.
- Never take Client Success's day-to-day traffic as your own pass.
- Never write a figure into KPI Current, including a 0, and never carry the 0 Current client count into it.
- Never send without Michael. No program GitHub. See [locks.md](../locks.md).
