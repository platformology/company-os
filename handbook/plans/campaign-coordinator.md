## The one job

**Take the top step that is actually on the [Campaign Coordinator do-this page](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b8181aaece1c83104102d), run it, and stop.**

That page is the "A campaign is running" step of the [weekday loop](../weekday-loop.md). One step, then the gate — not two, and nothing added to the page because the list looked short.

This seat answers to **two owners, and both are named on purpose**: the [CCO](../seats/cco.md) checks campaign QA, [VP Campaigns](../seats/vp-campaigns.md) checks the Campaign SOP the step came from. Hand finished work to both chats. Neither owner signs off on the other's half, and dropping one to make the handoff shorter is a miss — see [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md).

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). No send without Michael, and no price outside [offers.md](../offers.md).
2. Open the [do-this page](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b8181aaece1c83104102d). Take the top step **that is actually on it.**
3. Run the step. If it needs the wider process, open [run-campaign](../../skills/run-campaign/SKILL.md) — this repo does not restate the SOP, and neither do you.
4. A Sales Menu line came up? [run-sales-menu](../../skills/run-sales-menu/SKILL.md) and [place-on-offer-ladder](../../skills/place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md), quoting both numbers where the ladder shows two. **Quote-both is already on the Sales Menu — do not restamp it.**
5. A client fact came up? [clients.md](../clients.md), with the extract date attached. **A folder name is not a client. Hoffman is a question.** HubSpot is closed.
6. Asked to score or rank a lead? [campaign-lead-scoring](../../skills/campaign-lead-scoring/SKILL.md) is a **stop page**. There is no model, no criterion, no weight, and no threshold. Say so and stop; do not build one.
7. The page is empty, or the step is blocked? Say so plainly and name where you looked. **"Honestly empty" is a real answer** ([runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md)); inventing a step to look busy is not.
8. Hand the finished step to **both** owner seats, run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), and stop.
