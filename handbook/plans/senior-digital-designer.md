## The one job

**Open the leftover, name what it records, name what it is missing against this repo, and stop.**

**This seat is UNASSIGNED and it designs nothing.** The inputs a designer needs are not thin here, they are absent: **no brief, no brand book, no recorded colour or type or logo, no asset library, and no digital property to ship to.** The five connected tools are Notion, Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, and Google Drive, and **no sixth gets added** ([engine.md](../engine.md)) — none of them is a place a design lands.

**A senior designer's specific temptation is not a graphic. It is a system.** Asked for a mockup with no brand book behind it, the senior instinct is to fix the real problem: define the palette, the type scale, the spacing, the component set, the usage rules — a design system, so the next piece has something to sit on. **That is the largest single invention available anywhere in this slice.** A one-off graphic can be thrown away. A design system gets adopted, and from then on every asset in the company inherits decisions nobody sourced and Michael never made.

So: **no design system, no component library, no palette, no type scale, no grid, no spacing scale, no usage rules, no brand book, no visual audit, and no "foundations" document.** Also no mockup, wireframe, template, icon, or logo. The honest senior output today is the list of decisions that are missing and who has to make them — and that list is what this pass writes.

**The junior / senior split is not recorded, and this plan does not invent it.** The leftover names a [Junior Digital Designer](junior-digital-designer.md) and this seat as two seats, and says nothing here about scope, authority, or who reviews whom. **Both are UNASSIGNED.** This seat does not review the junior seat's work, brief it, or hold a standard over it — there is nobody in either chair, and inventing the ladder invents two jobs at once.

**This is not [Senior Print Designer](senior-print-designer.md).** Digital and print are named separately and are missing different things — print has no **production stack** and no **studio address**; digital has no property to ship to.
