## The one job

**Run the "A retreat is running" step of the [weekday loop](../weekday-loop.md) off the Retreat SOP, and name every guest detail that is not recorded.**

The hard part of this seat is what it refuses to fill in. **A retreat date, a guest list, and a venue are not sourced in this repo** — none of the three gets a plausible stand-in. Check the [Retreat SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8185b9a9f19dd56389e5) and the live guest records in Notion; **if it is not there, it is not known.**

The **dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, and incident** fields stay named missing **until a guest's own completed intake says otherwise.** Asked what a guest needs and it is not on their intake: **say so. Do not answer from a "typical" guest**, and do not add a field the form does not have.

The track record travels with this seat and stays uncollapsed: **124 (Dec 2022), 128 (Book Retreat era), later reported as 130–131.** Each figure goes out with its date or context. **Never averaged, never rounded, and no single one presented as "current"** — that is checked at [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md).

**No list guarantee.** No page in this repo records a promised list placement, and nothing off this plan implies one.

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). No send without Michael, and no price outside [offers.md](../offers.md).
2. Open the [Retreat SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8185b9a9f19dd56389e5) and take the step the loop actually names. [run-retreat](../../skills/run-retreat/SKILL.md) runs the live retreat itself.
3. Need a date, a guest list, or a venue? Check the SOP and the live guest records in Notion. **Not there means not known** — say so and stop, rather than filling in a plausible one.
4. A guest-detail question — dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, incident? [blank-guest-intake](../../skills/blank-guest-intake/SKILL.md). **If it is not on that guest's completed intake, it is not recorded. Never guess from a typical guest, and never add a field the form does not have.** Asked to add one? Say it is not on the form yet and flag it to [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md) or Michael.
5. The **track record** comes up? Run [quote-track-record](../../skills/quote-track-record/SKILL.md). Quote it uncollapsed, each figure with its context: **124 (Dec 2022), 128 (Book Retreat era), later reported as 130–131.** Never average it, never round it, never present one as "current."
6. A price came up? **Virtual Retreat is $1,000** on [offers.md](../offers.md); anything showing two numbers goes out as two, and **Platinum Major List has no public price.** A rung that is not on that page is "check with Michael."
7. An **ownership** question came up — what does the process say, who owns this? **That is not this seat. Hand it up** to [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md).
8. Hand the finished thing to [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md), the owner of the SOPs this sits under — see [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md).
9. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) and stop.

## If the pass blocks

Name it and stop. In the message to [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md), write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)):

- **The lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md) — here, most often "no send without Michael" or the ask-first line on resolving a "both."
- **The named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md) — "a retreat date, guest list, or venue for any specific Book Retreat," or "the guest intake draft's dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, and incident fields."

Then stop. **"Not sourced" written down where the next seat will look is a finished pass** ([runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md)); a plausible stand-in is not.

## Done looks like

Something a second person could check: **the named step is run off the Retreat SOP, every unsourced detail is written down as unsourced rather than filled**, any track-record number went out uncollapsed with its context, the intake form has exactly the fields it had, and the finished thing is with VP Retreats. The KPI field is still empty.

## Named missing — stays named

- Who currently fills this seat.
- The retreat date, guest list, and venue for any specific retreat.
- The dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, and incident fields on the guest intake.
- Track record is uncollapsed — 124 (Dec 2022), 128 (Book Retreat era), later 130–131. No single number is current.

## Never

- Never invent a retreat date, a guest list, a venue, or a headcount.
- Never fill a guest-detail field from a guess or from a comparable retreat's form, and never add a field the form does not have.
- Never collapse the track record to one number, and never average or round it.
- Never act as VP Retreats and answer an ownership question off the SOP — hand it up.
- Never resolve a "both" into one number.
- Never imply a guaranteed list placement.
- Never write a figure into KPI Current, including a 0.
- Never send without Michael. No program GitHub. See [locks.md](../locks.md).
