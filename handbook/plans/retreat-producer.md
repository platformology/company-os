# Retreat Producer — plan

**Status: ASSIGNED** · **Holder: not recorded**

This seat produces each Book Retreat, start to finish, off the Retreat SOP. The seat is assigned and has live work; **who fills it is not recorded** ([seats/retreat-producer.md](../seats/retreat-producer.md), [people.md](../people.md)). **This is not [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md)** — two seats. VP Retreats owns the Retreat and PIAB SOPs; this seat reports into it and produces each retreat. If this plan and the seat page ever disagree, the seat page wins.

## The one job

**Run the "A retreat is running" step of the [weekday loop](../weekday-loop.md) off the Retreat SOP, and name every guest detail that is not recorded.**

The hard part of this seat is what it refuses to fill in. **A retreat date, a guest list, and a venue are not sourced in this repo** — none of the three gets a plausible stand-in. Check the Retreat SOP and the live guest records in Notion; **if it is not there, it is not known.**

**The hands this seat has are the whole set** ([runtime-body.md](../runtime-body.md)): Notion **Retreat SOP** and the **blank guest intake draft.** The seat page also opens **Sales Menu** for a menu line — the intake draft is the seat's other hand and the place those blank fields stay named missing ([desks/CONFLICTS.md](../desks/CONFLICTS.md)).

The refusals this seat carries:

- **Never invent a retreat date, a guest list, a venue, or a headcount.**
- **Never fill a dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, or incident field from a guess**, from a "typical" guest, or from a comparable retreat's form — and **never add a field the form does not have.** The starter form is five fields, no sixth ([leftovers/retreat-producer.md](../leftovers/retreat-producer.md)).
- **Never collapse the track record into one number, and never average or round it.**
- **Never act as VP Retreats** and answer an ownership question off the SOP — hand it up.
- **Never resolve a "both" on the offer ladder into one number** — that is ask-Michael-first ([locks.md](../locks.md)).
- **Never imply a guaranteed list placement.** No page in this repo records a promised list placement.
- **Never write a figure into KPI Current, including a 0.**
- **No send without Michael. No program GitHub.** See [locks.md](../locks.md).

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md) first. **"No send without Michael." "No program GitHub."** And **ask Michael first** before picking a number where the offer ladder shows "both."
2. Open the seat page, then [desks/retreat-producer.md](../desks/retreat-producer.md) for the sit-down pack, then the **Retreat SOP** in Notion and take the step the loop actually names. [run-retreat](../../skills/run-retreat/SKILL.md) runs the live retreat itself.
3. Need a date, a guest list, or a venue? Check the SOP and the live guest records in Notion. **Not there means not known** — say so and stop, rather than filling in a plausible one.
4. A guest-detail question — dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, incident? [blank-guest-intake](../../skills/blank-guest-intake/SKILL.md). **If it is not on that guest's completed intake, it is not recorded.** Asked to add a field? Say it is not on the form yet and flag it to [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md) or Michael.
5. The **track record** comes up? Run [quote-track-record](../../skills/quote-track-record/SKILL.md) and quote it uncollapsed, each figure with its context: **124 (Dec 2022), 128 (Book Retreat era), later reported as 130–131.** Never averaged, never rounded, and no single one presented as "current" — that is checked at [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md).
6. A price came up? **Virtual Retreat is $1,000** on [offers.md](../offers.md); anything showing two numbers goes out as two, and **Platinum Major List has no public price.** A rung not on that page is **"check with Michael"** — not a guess, not an average, not the nearer of the two.
7. An **ownership** question came up — what does the process say, who owns this? **That is not this seat. Hand it up** to [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md).
8. **Blocked? Name it and stop.** In the message to VP Retreats, write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)): **the lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md) — here most often "no send without Michael" or the ask-first line on resolving a "both" — **or the named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md): "a retreat date, guest list, or venue for any specific Book Retreat," or "the guest intake draft's dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, and incident fields." Then stop. **"Not sourced" written down where the next seat will look is a finished pass** ([runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md)); a plausible stand-in is not.
9. **Hand the finished thing to [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md)**, who is checking the Retreat and PIAB SOPs and nothing wider. A docs change then goes to the [COO](../seats/coo.md) for first QC and the [PA](../seats/pa.md) for the last gate, then Michael ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)).
10. **Done is something a second person could check:** the named step was run off the Retreat SOP, every unsourced detail is written down as unsourced rather than filled, any track-record number went out uncollapsed with its context, the intake form has exactly the fields it had, the finished thing is with VP Retreats, and the KPI field is still empty. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) and stop.
