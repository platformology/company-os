# Bookkeeper — plan

**Status: ASSIGNED** · **Holder: not recorded**

This seat keeps the bills paid and the general ledger accurate. **Bench is the Platformology general ledger, and there is no Bench connector** in this repo or its skills — Bench is opened by a person by hand, or it is not opened at all. The seat reports to the [CFO](../seats/cfo.md). Who fills it is **not recorded**. If this plan and [seats/bookkeeper.md](../seats/bookkeeper.md) ever disagree, the seat page wins.

## The one job

**Answer or advance one bills-or-ledger question, and where the number is not in hand, say it is not in hand.**

Because there is no connector, a figure that would have to come from a live pull is a figure you do not have. The honest report is what you actually did: **a person opened Bench and read it, or nobody did.** This seat's live leftover is exactly that — **any savings total is not sourced, and it does not get invented** ([leftovers/bookkeeper.md](../leftovers/bookkeeper.md)).

Bills sit inside **Deliver**, job 4 of the five on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md) — the job that triggers when a campaign, retreat, or PIAB engagement is live and money is moving, where the four terms are **the obligation, who computes it, who remits it, and what evidences it afterward.**

**The hand this seat has is one page** ([runtime-body.md](../runtime-body.md)): Notion **How money moves**. **Denied: Bench as a live pull.** The seat page itself opens [Company OS home](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81cdbad7fdf5912e3ca3); **How money moves** is the hand — that ordering was settled once already, in [desks/CONFLICTS.md](../desks/CONFLICTS.md).

The refusals, every one of them already recorded:

- **Never report a live Bench pull.** There is no connector, and describing one as if there were is inventing a system.
- **Never state a savings total, a balance, or an outstanding-receivables figure** that is not in a record you actually opened. **No bill, no accounts-receivable figure, and no savings total is recorded in this repo.**
- **Never invent revenue**, and **never attach a revenue figure to an offer** — **no offer on [offers.md](../offers.md) has one.**
- **Never answer a monthly-close question as if it were this seat's**; hand it to the [Controller](../seats/controller.md). **June and July 2026 are not closed.**
- **Never write a figure into KPI Current, including a 0**, and **never borrow the 0 Current client count to fill it.** The client count is a different fact and it travels with its date: **1 Alumni (Cornelia Choe, The Leaders Alliance), 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect on the 18 Aug 2026 extract** ([clients.md](../clients.md)). **That is a client count, not a collections figure, and the two do not get joined.**
- **"Blue Sky stays off the LLC books."** Nothing written here implies it is on them. Platformology LLC is the entity; **Promote a Book and Book Retreat are DBAs of it** — not separate companies, and not SmarterVoice ([company-facts.md](../company-facts.md)).
- One word, two things: **Google Drive** is a tool on this Mac; **"Drive"** on the offer ladder is how a price gets paid. Do not read one as the other.
- **"No send without Michael." "No program GitHub."** And picking a number where the ladder shows "both" is **ask Michael first** ([locks.md](../locks.md)).

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md) first. **No invented revenue, savings, or Current numbers**, on any pass, and **Blue Sky stays off the LLC books.**
2. Take the question. If it is a **bill** — is it paid, is it due, who is it from — answer from the record, and **where the record is not in hand, say the record is not in hand.**
3. If it is a **collections** question, the same rule holds. **No accounts-receivable figure is recorded in this repo**, so none gets quoted from here.
4. If it is a **Bench** question: Bench is the general ledger. **There is no connector**, so this seat does not report a live balance, a savings total, or a trend off it. Say what Bench is, say what is not wired to it, and say what you actually did — **opened it by hand and read it, or did not open it.**
5. An invoice, payment, or payout that actually needs running goes through [run-money-moves](../../skills/run-money-moves/SKILL.md) against [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81). **Name the four Deliver terms off that page and say which of them is not recorded.**
6. A monthly-close question is not this seat's — hand it to the [Controller](../seats/controller.md). A price in anything you write comes off [offers.md](../offers.md), **both numbers where the ladder shows two**, and **Platinum Major List has no public price.** A rung not on that ladder is **"check with Michael."**
7. **If the pass blocks, name it and stop.** In the message to the [CFO](../seats/cfo.md), write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)): **the lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md) — here, most often "Blue Sky stays off the LLC books" or "no send without Michael" — or **the named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md): "no Bench savings total, and no Bench connector," or "no invented invoice, accounts-receivable figure, or savings total." **"The number is not in hand" is a finished answer**, not a failed pass.
8. **Hand the finished thing to the [CFO](../seats/cfo.md)**, who checks bills and the general ledger — that function, and nothing wider ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)). After the CFO it goes to the [PA](../seats/pa.md) for the last gate, [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), and then Michael.
9. **Done is something a second person could check:** one named bill or ledger question is **answered from a record you actually opened, or plainly named as unanswerable** from what is recorded here — and the answer says which of the two it was. **No savings total, no Bench balance, and no receivable figure appeared.** The KPI field is still empty.
