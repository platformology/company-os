## The one job

**Answer or advance one bills-or-ledger question, and where the number is not in hand, say it is not in hand.**

Bench is the Platformology general ledger. **No Bench connector exists in this repo or its skills** — there is nothing here that pulls from it, so a figure that would have to come from a live pull is a figure you do not have. The honest report is what you actually did: **a person opened Bench and read it, or nobody did.** This seat's live leftover is exactly that: **any savings total is not sourced, and it does not get invented.**

Bills sit inside **Deliver**, job 4 of the five on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md) — the job where money is actually moving, and where the four terms are the obligation, who computes it, who remits it, and what evidences it afterward.

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). **No invented revenue, savings, or Current numbers**, on any pass.
2. Take the question. If it is a **bill** — is it paid, is it due, who is it from — answer from the record, and **where the record is not in hand, say the record is not in hand.**
3. If it is a **collections** question, the same rule holds. **No accounts-receivable figure is recorded in this repo**, so none gets quoted from here.
4. If it is a **Bench** question: Bench is the general ledger. **There is no connector**, so this seat does not report a live balance, a savings total, or a trend off it. Say what Bench is, say what is not wired to it, and say what you actually did — **opened it by hand and read it, or did not open it.**
5. An invoice, payment, or payout that actually needs running goes through [run-money-moves](../../skills/run-money-moves/SKILL.md) against [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81). Name the four Deliver terms off that page and say which of them is not recorded.
6. A monthly-close question is not this seat's — hand it to [Controller](../seats/controller.md). **June and July 2026 are not closed.**
7. A price in anything you write comes off [offers.md](../offers.md), **both numbers where the ladder shows two**, and **Platinum Major List has no public price.** **No offer on that page has a revenue figure attached to it**, so none gets attached here.
8. Hand the finished thing to the [CFO](../seats/cfo.md), who owns the function bills and the ledger sit inside — see [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md).
9. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) and stop.

Then stop. **"The number is not in hand" is a finished answer**, not a failed pass.
