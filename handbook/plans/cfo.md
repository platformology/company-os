That is job 4 — Deliver — on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md), and it is the shape this seat works in. The answers live in [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81) (Notion). This repo does not restate them, and neither do you.

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). Blue Sky stays off the LLC books, and nothing external goes out without Michael.
2. Identify which of the five jobs on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md) the ask actually sits in. Attract, Diagnose, Place, Deliver, or Expand alumni — one of them, not a blend.
3. Open [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81) and answer from it. Run [run-money-moves](../../skills/run-money-moves/SKILL.md) when an invoice, payment, or payout actually needs running.
4. A price in the answer comes off [offers.md](../offers.md) — both numbers where the ladder shows two. Never recalled.
5. A monthly-close question is not this pass: hand it to [Controller](../seats/controller.md). **June and July 2026 are not closed**, and that fact travels as-is rather than being estimated around.
6. A Bench, bills, or savings question is not this pass either: hand it to [Bookkeeper](../seats/bookkeeper.md). **There is no Bench connector and no sourced savings total.**
7. Asked for revenue — company-wide or per offer? None is recorded in this repo. Say so. No offer on [offers.md](../offers.md) has a revenue figure attached to it.
8. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) and stop.
