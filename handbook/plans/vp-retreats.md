## The one job

**Answer one ownership question off the Retreat SOP or the PIAB SOP, and leave the guest intake draft's blank fields blank.**

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). No send without Michael, and no price outside [offers.md](../offers.md).
2. Read the ask and pick the right SOP: **Retreat, or PIAB. One of them, not a blend.**
3. Answer off that page. **Open it; do not answer from memory of it.** [run-retreat](../../skills/run-retreat/SKILL.md) and [run-piab](../../skills/run-piab/SKILL.md) run a live engagement; this seat's own router handles the ownership question.
4. Asked how long PIAB takes? **Not recorded.** That is the whole answer. No estimate, no "typically," no range.
5. A price came up? PIAB is **Consult, or Drive $40,000** on [offers.md](../offers.md) — **both, because the ladder shows two. Never one.** A Book Retreat price question runs to the same page: **Virtual Retreat $1,000**, and **Platinum Major List has no public price.** Resolving a "both" is ask-Michael-first.
6. Guest intake question? [blank-guest-intake](../../skills/blank-guest-intake/SKILL.md). **The starter form keeps its five fields; do not invent a sixth**, and do not fill dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, or incident from a guess or from a comparable retreat's form. Asked to add a field? Say it is not on the form yet and flag it to Michael.
7. A retreat date, guest list, or venue? **None is sourced.** Hand production down to the [Retreat Producer](../seats/retreat-producer.md) rather than filling in a plausible one.
8. Hand the finished thing to the [COO](../seats/coo.md) — the seat this one reports to — run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), and stop.

## If the pass blocks

Name it and stop. In the message to the [COO](../seats/coo.md), or back to the Producer below, write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)):

- **The lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md) — here, most often the ask-first line on resolving a "both," which is what PIAB's two prices are.
- **The named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md) — the cycle time, the blank guest intake fields, or "a retreat date, guest list, or venue for any specific Book Retreat."

Then stop. **"Not recorded" is the finished answer for all three.**

## Never

- **Never estimate a cycle time**, and never back one out of a price or a retreat length.
- Never add a field to the guest intake starter form, and never fill a blank one from a guess.
- Never treat a sales application as the guest intake form, or the reverse.
- Never invent a retreat date, guest list, or venue.
- Never resolve PIAB's two prices into one.
- Never produce the retreat yourself — that is the Retreat Producer's seat.
- Never write a figure into KPI Current, including a 0.
- Never send without Michael. No program GitHub. See [locks.md](../locks.md).
