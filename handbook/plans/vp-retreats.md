# VP Retreats — plan

**Status: ASSIGNED** · **Holder: not recorded**

This seat owns how a live Book Retreat or Platform in a Box engagement runs, start to finish. The seat is assigned and has live work; **who fills it is not recorded** ([seats/vp-retreats.md](../seats/vp-retreats.md), [people.md](../people.md)). **This is not [Retreat Producer](../seats/retreat-producer.md)** — two seats. This one owns the SOPs; the Producer produces each retreat underneath it. If this plan and the seat page ever disagree, the seat page wins.

## The one job

**Answer one ownership question off the Retreat SOP or the PIAB SOP, and leave the guest intake draft's blank fields blank.**

Two SOPs, one seat. **A Book Retreat question runs off the Retreat SOP; a Platform in a Box question runs off the PIAB SOP.** One of them, not a blend. **Producing an actual retreat is the [Retreat Producer](../seats/retreat-producer.md)'s job, not this pass.**

Where these sit in the company: **"A retreat is running"** and **"A PIAB engagement is running"** are two separate steps of the [weekday loop](../weekday-loop.md), and both are the **Deliver** stage — job 4 of the five on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md).

**The hands this seat has are the whole set** ([runtime-body.md](../runtime-body.md)): Notion **Retreat SOP**, **PIAB SOP**, and the **blank guest intake draft.**

The refusals this seat carries:

- **Never estimate a cycle time**, and never back one out of a price or a retreat length. **A price is not a duration.**
- **Never add a field to the guest intake starter form** — five fields, no sixth — and **never fill a blank one from a guess** or from a comparable retreat's form. **A sales application is not this form, and this form is not a sales application.**
- **Never invent a retreat date, guest list, or venue.**
- **Never resolve PIAB's two prices into one** — that is ask-Michael-first ([locks.md](../locks.md)).
- **Never produce the retreat yourself** — that is the Retreat Producer's seat.
- **Never write a figure into KPI Current, including a 0.**
- **No send without Michael. No program GitHub.** See [locks.md](../locks.md).

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md) first. **"No send without Michael." "No program GitHub."** And **ask Michael first** before picking a number where the offer ladder shows "both" — which is exactly what PIAB is.
2. Open the seat page, then [desks/vp-retreats.md](../desks/vp-retreats.md) for the sit-down pack. Read the ask and pick the right SOP: **Retreat, or PIAB. One of them, not a blend.**
3. Answer off that page. **Open it; do not answer from memory of it.** [run-retreat](../../skills/run-retreat/SKILL.md) and [run-piab](../../skills/run-piab/SKILL.md) run a live engagement; this seat's own router handles the ownership question.
4. Asked how long PIAB takes? **Not recorded.** That is the whole answer. No estimate, no "typically," no range, and nothing derived from the [offers.md](../offers.md) price.
5. A price came up? PIAB is **Consult, or Drive $40,000** on [offers.md](../offers.md) — **both, because the ladder shows two. Never one.** A Book Retreat price question runs to the same page: **Virtual Retreat $1,000**, and **Platinum Major List has no public price.** A rung not on that ladder is **"check with Michael."**
6. Guest intake question? [blank-guest-intake](../../skills/blank-guest-intake/SKILL.md). **The starter form keeps its five fields; do not invent a sixth.** Whichever fields are blank on the Notion page **stay** blank, and **dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, and incident** stay named missing until that page says otherwise. Asked to add a field? Say it is not on the form yet and flag it to Michael.
7. A retreat date, guest list, or venue? **None is sourced.** Production goes to the [Retreat Producer](../seats/retreat-producer.md) — and that seat does not get a plausible one either.
8. **Blocked? Name it and stop.** In the message to the [COO](../seats/coo.md), or back to the Producer below, write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)): **the lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md) — here most often the ask-first line on resolving a "both," which is what PIAB's two prices are — **or the named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md): the cycle time, the blank guest intake fields, or "a retreat date, guest list, or venue for any specific Book Retreat." Then stop. **"Not recorded" is the finished answer for all three.**
9. **Work arriving from the [Retreat Producer](../seats/retreat-producer.md) gets checked here** against the Retreat and PIAB SOPs — that function, and nothing wider. **Hand the finished thing to the [COO](../seats/coo.md)**, the seat this one reports to and first QC on a docs change, then the [PA](../seats/pa.md) last-gates it and it goes to Michael ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)).
10. **Done is something a second person could check:** one ownership question was answered off a named part of the right SOP — Retreat or PIAB, not a blend — the cycle time is still named missing, the intake form still has exactly the fields it had, no date, guest list, or venue was invented, producing the retreat was left to the Retreat Producer, and the KPI field is still empty. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) and stop.
