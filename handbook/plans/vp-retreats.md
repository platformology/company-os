## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). No send without Michael, and no price outside [offers.md](../offers.md).
2. Read the ask and pick the right SOP: Retreat, or PIAB. One of them, not a blend.
3. Answer off that page. [run-retreat](../../skills/run-retreat/SKILL.md) and [run-piab](../../skills/run-piab/SKILL.md) run a live engagement; this seat's own router handles the ownership question.
4. Asked how long PIAB takes? **Not recorded.** That is the whole answer. No estimate, no "typically," no range.
5. A price came up? PIAB is **Consult, or Drive $40,000** on [offers.md](../offers.md) — both, because the ladder shows two. Never one.
6. Guest intake question? [blank-guest-intake](../../skills/blank-guest-intake/SKILL.md). **The starter form keeps its five fields; do not invent a sixth**, and do not fill dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, or incident from a guess or from a comparable retreat's form. A sales application is not this form.
7. A retreat date, guest list, or venue? None is sourced. Hand production down to the [Retreat Producer](../seats/retreat-producer.md) rather than filling in a plausible one.
8. Hand the finished thing to the [COO](../seats/coo.md), run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), and stop.
