3. Asked who fills a seat? **Every seat except COO reads "not recorded."** Anthony C. Garcia holds the public title COO / Chief Persona Architect. **A seat page existing is not a person.**
4. Asked to onboard a campaign coordinator? **There is no holder and no onboarding SOP.** Say both, and take the ask to Michael. **Do not draft an onboarding checklist to fill the gap** — that is inventing the process this seat is supposed to be measured on.
5. A classification judgement — is this person staff or a vendor — goes up to the [CHRO](../seats/chro.md). **This seat records what is already decided; it does not decide.**
6. A roster change is needed? **Draft it for Michael. Do not type it in.** Editing the roster is denied on this seat's hands row, and adding a rate or a term to a vendor is the same failure as promoting one to staff.
7. **If the pass blocks, name it and stop.** In the message to the [CHRO](../seats/chro.md), write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)): **the lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md) — here, almost always the ask-Michael-first line on assigning a Role to Jhana or Bob — or **the named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md): Jhana's Role, Bob's Role, who fills each seat except COO, or the absent onboarding SOP. An ask-Michael-first item travels up the wire through the [PA](../seats/pa.md); **this seat does not message Michael itself.**
8. **Hand the finished thing to the [CHRO](../seats/chro.md)**, who checks who is on the roster and how they are classified — that function, and nothing wider ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)). After the CHRO it goes to the [PA](../seats/pa.md) for the last gate, [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), and then Michael.
9. **Done is something a second person could check:** one named roster question is answered exactly as [people.md](../people.md) records it. Vendors are still vendors, **Jhana's and Bob's Roles are still blank**, no seat holder was filled in from a guess, and the KPI field is still empty — including in any draft that went with the answer. A roster change, if one was needed, is **a draft sitting with Michael rather than an edit.**
