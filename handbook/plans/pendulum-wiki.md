## The one job

**Open the leftover, name what it records, name what it is missing against this repo, and stop.**

**This seat is UNASSIGNED, and the thing it is named after does not exist.** **No Pendulum wiki exists** — not in this repo, not linked from it, not referenced by any seat page or skill. So the honest first question is not "what goes in it" but "is there one," and the answer today is no.

**What is recorded is the corpus, and it belongs to somebody else.** [Head of Content](../seats/head-of-content.md) is the **librarian** for the 27-year body of **personas, proposals, white papers, ebooks, and Pendulum material** Platformology has produced. **Pendulum is one part of that body, not a synonym for it** — [run-buyer-packet](../../skills/run-buyer-packet/SKILL.md) says so directly, and a packet that shows only Pendulum is not the buyer packet.

So this seat produces **no wiki, no page tree, no index, no glossary, no concept page, no entity page, no summary, no synthesis, and no explanation of what Pendulum is.**

**Three rules govern this seat the day it is staffed, and they are worth writing down now because a wiki is where they get broken quietly:**

1. **The corpus is read-only.** A wiki is a derived layer. The 27 years of material underneath it are never edited, moved, renamed, or overwritten to make a page tidier, and nothing written for the wiki gets filed back as corpus. [Head of Content](head-of-content.md) is already barred from drafting a stand-in and shelving it, because **once it is filed nobody can tell the 27 years from this afternoon.**
2. **Never conflate speculation with fact.** A wiki page that reads confidently is indistinguishable from a wiki page that is sourced. Every claim carries where it came from, and a claim with no source says so instead of being phrased around.
3. **Contradictions get recorded, not resolved.** When two sources disagree, the page records **which source says what** rather than silently picking. This repo already has the worked example: the track record reads **124 (Dec 2022), 128 (Book Retreat era), later 130–131**, and the correct entry is all three readings with their dates — never one number ([company-facts.md](../company-facts.md), [quote-track-record](../../skills/quote-track-record/SKILL.md)).

**A wiki is also the exact thing [runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md) bars.** **No new tracker. No new board, no queue file, no second place to look for work.** A leftover lives where it was already named — a seat page, a router, or [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md). If a "Pendulum wiki" turns into a place where work gets listed, it has become the tracker that page rules out. Whether it is ever built at all is Michael's call, and **nothing in this repo records that he has made it.**
