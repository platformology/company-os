So this seat produces **no wiki, no page tree, no index, no glossary, no concept page, no entity page, no summary, no synthesis, no stub, no "structure to fill in later," and no explanation of what Pendulum is.** A page tree is the wiki. Asked what Pendulum is, say what this repo records and no more — it is part of the 27-year corpus — because an explanation written here becomes the definition.

The sourced refusals this seat carries:

- **The corpus is read-only.** The 27 years of material are never edited, moved, renamed, or overwritten to make a page tidier, and nothing written for a wiki gets filed back as corpus. [Head of Content](head-of-content.md) is already barred from drafting a stand-in and shelving it, because **once it is filed nobody can tell the 27 years from this afternoon.** Asked for a Pendulum piece that is not in hand, say it is not in hand — **never draft a stand-in.**
- **Never conflate speculation with fact.** A page that reads confidently is indistinguishable from a page that is sourced. Every claim carries where it came from, and a claim with no source says so instead of being phrased around.
- **Contradictions get recorded, not resolved.** When two sources disagree, record **which source says what** rather than silently picking. The worked example is the track record: **124 (Dec 2022), 128 (Book Retreat era), later 130–131** — all three readings with their dates, never one number ([company-facts.md](../company-facts.md), [quote-track-record](../../skills/quote-track-record/SKILL.md)).

- **Never name a holder for this seat.** **Jhana and Bob have no recorded Role**, and assigning one is ask-Michael-first.
- **No send without Michael. No program GitHub. No VMs, no GKE, no local LLM until Michael says go** ([locks.md](../locks.md)). Nothing here installs or names a wiki platform.
