## The one job

**Answer one ownership question off the Campaign SOP, and hand the day-to-day step down to the Coordinator.**

This seat owns how a live Promote a Book campaign runs, start to finish — **the SOP, not the daily steps.** The [Campaign Coordinator](../seats/campaign-coordinator.md) runs the do-this page underneath. **Do not collapse the two seats to make an answer shorter**, and do not run the Coordinator's page as if it were yours.

The Coordinator has **two owners on purpose**: this seat checks the Campaign SOP it ran, the [CCO](../seats/cco.md) checks campaign QA. **Neither signs off on the other's half**, and dropping one to make a row shorter is a miss ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)). That page's own words for the rule: **"crossed-seat sign-off is a miss, not a pass."**

Where a campaign sits in the company: it is the **"A campaign is running"** step of the [weekday loop](../weekday-loop.md), and it is the **Deliver** stage — job 4 of the five on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md), the one that triggers when an engagement is live and money is moving.

**No list guarantee.** No page in this repo records a promised list placement. A rung on [offers.md](../offers.md) is a price, not a promise about an outcome, and nothing off this plan implies one.

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). No send without Michael, and no price outside [offers.md](../offers.md).
2. Read the ask. Is it **ownership** — who owns this, what does the process say — or is it **day-to-day**? Ownership is this pass. **Day-to-day goes down to the Coordinator.**
3. Answer off the [Campaign SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ea99c0dcf96db7cd2e). **Open it; do not answer from memory of it.**
4. The ask is to actually run or check on a live campaign? Run [run-campaign](../../skills/run-campaign/SKILL.md) — and remember the 18 Aug 2026 extract in [clients.md](../clients.md) records **0 Current clients**, so **a "live campaign" needs a sourced client behind it, not an assumed one.** A folder name is not a client; Hoffman is a question; HubSpot is closed.
5. A price came up? [place-on-offer-ladder](../../skills/place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md) against [offers.md](../offers.md), **both numbers where the ladder shows two** — Multi-author **$1,000 or $20,000**, Digital **$35,000 or $35,000 + $40,000 bonus**, and so on — and **Platinum Major List has no public price. Quote-both is already settled on the Sales Menu — do not restamp it.**
6. **Lead scoring stays a stop page.** [campaign-lead-scoring](../../skills/campaign-lead-scoring/SKILL.md) refuses to invent criteria, weights, or thresholds, and the owner said no. **Do not build a model, a point table, a tier system, or an MQL/SQL cutoff**, and do not fill one of the five named-missing fields on that draft. Those five are not named here on purpose — **naming them would itself be inventing structure the Notion page owns.**
7. **Leave campaign QA to the [CCO](../seats/cco.md).** Check the Campaign SOP half only. Hand your finished thing on and stop.
8. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md).

## If the pass blocks

Name it and stop. In the message to whoever is waiting — the Coordinator below, or the [COO](../seats/coo.md) above — write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)):

- **The lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md) — here, most often "no send without Michael" or the ask-first line on resolving a "both."
- **The named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md) — "five unnamed fields on the campaign lead scoring draft, and no scoring model in the corpus," or the absence of any milestone schedule.

Then stop. **A block is not permission to invent a campaign, a milestone, or a client to hang one on.**
