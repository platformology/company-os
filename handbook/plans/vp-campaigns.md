# VP Campaigns — plan

**Status: ASSIGNED** · **Holder: not recorded**

This seat owns how a live Promote a Book campaign runs, start to finish — **the SOP, not the daily steps.** The seat is assigned and has live work; **who fills it is not recorded** ([seats/vp-campaigns.md](../seats/vp-campaigns.md), [people.md](../people.md)). **Not the [CMO](cmo.md). Not "also the [Campaign Coordinator](../seats/campaign-coordinator.md)."** Three separate seats. If this plan and the seat page ever disagree, the seat page wins.

## The one job

**Answer one ownership question off the Campaign SOP, and hand the day-to-day step down to the Coordinator.**

The [Campaign Coordinator](../seats/campaign-coordinator.md) runs the do-this page underneath. **Do not collapse the two seats to make an answer shorter**, and do not run the Coordinator's page as if it were yours. **The hand on this seat's row in [runtime-body.md](../runtime-body.md) is one page: Notion Campaign SOP.** The Coordinator's do-this page belongs to the seat underneath — **read it, do not run it.**

The Coordinator has **two owners on purpose**: this seat checks the Campaign SOP it ran, the [CCO](../seats/cco.md) checks campaign QA. **Neither signs off on the other's half**, and dropping one to make a row shorter is a miss — [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)'s own words: **"crossed-seat sign-off is a miss, not a pass."**

Where a campaign sits in the company: it is the **"A campaign is running"** step of the [weekday loop](../weekday-loop.md), and it is the **Deliver** stage — job 4 of the five on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md), the one that triggers when an engagement is live and money is moving.

The refusals this seat carries:

- **Never fold this seat into the CMO**, and never run the Coordinator's do-this page as if it were yours.
- **Never build a lead-scoring model, and never assign a lead a number.** The owner said no; the stop page stays a stop page.
- **Never invent a campaign, a milestone date, or a client to hang one on.**
- **Never stamp campaign QA** — that half belongs to the CCO.
- **Never quote a price outside [offers.md](../offers.md), and never resolve a "both" into one number.**
- **Never imply a guaranteed list placement.** No page in this repo records a promised one, and **a rung on the ladder is a price, not a promise about an outcome.**
- **Never write a figure into KPI Current, including a 0.**
- **No send without Michael. No program GitHub.** See [locks.md](../locks.md).

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md) first. **"No send without Michael." "No program GitHub."** And **ask Michael first** before picking a number where the offer ladder shows "both," or before recording a client as Current or Prospect.
2. Open the seat page, then [desks/vp-campaigns.md](../desks/vp-campaigns.md) for the sit-down pack. Read the ask: is it **ownership** — who owns this, what does the process say — or is it **day-to-day**? Ownership is this pass. **Day-to-day goes down to the Coordinator.**
3. Answer off the [Campaign SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ea99c0dcf96db7cd2e). **Open it; do not answer from memory of it.**
4. The ask is to actually run or check on a live campaign? Run [run-campaign](../../skills/run-campaign/SKILL.md) — and remember the **18 Aug 2026** extract in [clients.md](../clients.md) records **0 Current and 0 Prospect** clients, quoted with that date or not at all. **A "live campaign" needs a sourced client behind it, not an assumed one.** A folder name is not a client; **Hoffman is a question, not a Current client**; **HubSpot is closed** and the CRM is Notion Clients.
5. A price came up? [place-on-offer-ladder](../../skills/place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md) against [offers.md](../offers.md), **both numbers where the ladder shows two** — Multi-author **$1,000 or $20,000**, Digital **$35,000 or $35,000 + $40,000 bonus**, and so on — and **Platinum Major List has no public price.** A rung not on that ladder is **"check with Michael."** **Quote-both is already settled on the Sales Menu — do not restamp it.**
6. **Lead scoring stays a stop page.** [campaign-lead-scoring](../../skills/campaign-lead-scoring/SKILL.md) refuses to invent criteria, weights, or thresholds, and the owner said no. **Do not build a model, a point table, a tier system, or an MQL/SQL cutoff**, and do not fill one of the five named-missing fields on that draft. Those five are not named here on purpose — **naming them would itself be inventing structure the Notion page owns.**
7. **Leave campaign QA to the [CCO](../seats/cco.md).** Check the Campaign SOP half only.
8. **Blocked? Name it and stop.** In the message to whoever is waiting — the Coordinator below, or the [COO](../seats/coo.md) above — write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)): **the lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md) — here most often "no send without Michael" or the ask-first line on resolving a "both" — **or the named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md): "five unnamed fields on the campaign lead scoring draft, and no scoring model in the corpus," or the absence of any milestone schedule. Then stop. **A block is not permission to invent a campaign, a milestone, or a client to hang one on.**
9. **Work arriving from the [Campaign Coordinator](../seats/campaign-coordinator.md) gets checked here against the Campaign SOP the coordinator ran — that half only**, with campaign QA going to the [CCO](../seats/cco.md) in parallel. **Hand the finished thing to the [COO](../seats/coo.md)**, the seat this one reports to and first QC on a docs change, then the [PA](../seats/pa.md) last-gates it and it goes to Michael.
10. **Done is something a second person could check:** the ownership question is answered off a named part of the SOP, the day-to-day step sits with the Coordinator rather than with you, campaign QA was left to the CCO, no milestone, campaign, or client was invented on the way through, and the KPI field is still empty. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) and stop.
