## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). No send without Michael, and no price outside [offers.md](../offers.md).
2. Read the ask. Is it **ownership** — who owns this, what does the process say — or is it **day-to-day**? Ownership is this pass. Day-to-day goes down to the Coordinator.
3. Answer off the [Campaign SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ea99c0dcf96db7cd2e). Open it; do not answer from memory of it.
4. The ask is to actually run or check on a live campaign? Run [run-campaign](../../skills/run-campaign/SKILL.md) — and remember the 18 Aug 2026 extract in [clients.md](../clients.md) records **0 Current clients**, so a "live campaign" needs a sourced client behind it, not an assumed one.
5. A price came up? [place-on-offer-ladder](../../skills/place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md) against [offers.md](../offers.md), both numbers where the ladder shows two. **Quote-both is already settled on the Sales Menu — do not restamp it.**
6. **Lead scoring stays a stop page.** [campaign-lead-scoring](../../skills/campaign-lead-scoring/SKILL.md) refuses to invent criteria, weights, or thresholds, and the owner said no. Do not build a model, a point table, a tier system, or an MQL/SQL cutoff, and do not fill one of the five named-missing fields on that draft.
7. Leave campaign QA to the [CCO](../seats/cco.md). Hand your finished thing on and stop.
8. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md).
