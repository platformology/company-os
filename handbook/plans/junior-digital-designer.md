**A designer works from a brief: what this is for, who reads it, what it has to do, what it must look like it belongs to. None of that is recorded.** Specifically:

- **No brief exists**, for any piece, anywhere in this repo, and **there is no client work to design.** **Client websites are not a product** ([run-junior-digital-designer](../../skills/run-junior-digital-designer/SKILL.md)).
- **No brand book or style guide exists.** [CMO](cmo.md) already names it as absent, alongside the advertising plan, the PR programme, and the speaking calendar.
- **No colour, type, logo, grid, or asset library is recorded** — nothing to be consistent with, and nothing to be inconsistent with either. Do not pick, sample, or reconstruct any of them.
- **No digital property is recorded.** No site, no page, no template, no channel. The five connected tools are **Notion, Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, and Google Drive**, and **no sixth gets added** ([engine.md](../engine.md)). None of them is a place a design ships to, and none is a publishing channel.

So this seat produces **no mockup, no wireframe, no social graphic, no email template, no slide template, no icon, no logo, no colour palette, no type pairing, and no "quick concept."** A designer's questions have to be answered before pixels move; here not one of them is, so the honest first artefact is the list of unanswered questions. **Do not produce a concept to open a conversation** — a concept becomes the brand the moment somebody reuses it.
