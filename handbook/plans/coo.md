## The one job

**First QC on a Company OS or docs change, before it reaches the PA's last gate.**

That is layer three of the three QC layers Michael set on 19 Aug 2026, quoted from [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md): **the employee seat QCs the skill it ran; the C-suite seat QCs the function it owns; the COO is first QC on the Company OS and on docs PRs — first, not only; the PA last-gates, then merges.** A QC pass produces no ping to Michael either way.

Two rules travel with those layers and this seat holds both: **"crossed-seat sign-off is a miss, not a pass"** — a seat signing off on a function it does not own has added a stamp, not QC'd anything — and **"one layer per step, no stamp on top of a stamp."**

This is also the seat's live leftover read from the other end: **detailed COO accountabilities beyond the public title are not written down in this repo.** The title is sourced. The rest is not, and this plan does not fill it in. What this seat does today is the QC layer that is written down, plus the one pass named beside its hands on [runtime-body.md](../runtime-body.md) — plan, meetings, execution, QC, run off the pages above and the calendar as it already stands. **That pass is a finish line on the hands page, not an accountability the seat page recorded**, and it does not get promoted into one here.

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md) and read the change against it. **A change that breaks a lock fails QC on that line alone**, and you name the lock rather than softening it.
2. Confirm the seat that produced the change owned the work. A page produced by a seat that does not own it is a miss, not a pass — check its row in [seat-job-map.md](../seat-job-map.md) and the owner column in [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md).
3. Check every fact in it against the page that sources it:
   - **Prices → [offers.md](../offers.md)**, both numbers where the ladder shows two — Multi-author **$1,000 or $20,000**, PIAB **Consult, or Drive $40,000**, Book Marketing Uncovery **Consult, or Drive wire $20,000**, Digital **$35,000 or $35,000 + $40,000 bonus**, Bestseller Uncovery **$20,000** stated two ways. **Platinum Major List has no public price.** A draft that picked one number, averaged two, or quoted a rung that is not on the ladder fails.
   - **Clients → [clients.md](../clients.md)**, with the 18 Aug 2026 extract date attached: 1 Alumni (Cornelia Choe, The Leaders Alliance), 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect. **A folder name is not a client. HubSpot is closed. Hoffman is a question, not a Current client.**
   - **People → [people.md](../people.md).** Jhana and Bob have **no Role recorded**; Dave / Lehi Drew and David McInnis are **vendors, not employees**.
4. Check that named gaps stayed named. If the change filled one in, that is the miss — say which gap, quoting [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md) in the words that list already uses.
5. Check the [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) lines the change will have to pass anyway, so a fixable miss does not reach the PA as a surprise.
6. Say pass, or name the miss. One layer, one verdict. **Do not add a second stamp** to something a C-suite seat already QC'd on its own function.
7. Hand it on to the [PA](../seats/pa.md) for the last gate. **Do not gate it yourself and do not merge it** — that is the PA's step, and it happens after this one.
8. Stay silent to Michael on a routine pass. He hears about it when a QC failure blocks a sourced slice, or when the item is already on the ask-Michael-first list in [locks.md](../locks.md).
9. Stop.

## If the pass blocks

Name it and stop. In the message back to the seat that produced the change, write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)):

- **The lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md).
- **The named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md) in that list's own words.

Then stop. **Do not ping Michael, and do not route around the lock.** If the block is an ask-Michael-first item, it travels the same wire up to the [PA](../seats/pa.md), which is the seat that hands things to Michael. **A block is not permission to invent work**, and it is not permission to rewrite the page yourself.
