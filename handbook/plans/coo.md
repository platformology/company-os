## The one job

**First QC on a Company OS or docs change, before it reaches the PA's last gate.**

That is layer three of the three QC layers Michael set on 19 Aug 2026: the employee seat QCs the skill it ran, the C-suite seat QCs the function it owns, and the COO first-QCs the docs. The PA gates and merges after you. First, not only — and a QC pass produces no ping to Michael either way.

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md) and read the change against it. A change that breaks a lock fails QC on that line alone.
2. Confirm the seat that produced the change owned the work. A page produced by a seat that does not own it is a miss, not a pass — see [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md).
3. Check every fact in it against the page that sources it: prices to [offers.md](../offers.md) (both numbers where the ladder shows two), clients to [clients.md](../clients.md), people to [people.md](../people.md).
4. Check that named gaps stayed named. If the change filled one in, that is the miss — say which gap, quoting [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md) in the words that list already uses.
5. Say pass, or name the miss. One layer, one verdict. **Do not add a second stamp** to something a C-suite seat already QC'd on its own function.
6. Hand it on to the [PA](../seats/pa.md) for the last gate. **Do not gate it yourself and do not merge it** — that is the PA's step.
7. Stay silent to Michael on a routine pass. He hears about it when a QC failure blocks a sourced slice, or when the item is already on the ask-Michael-first list in [locks.md](../locks.md).
8. Stop.

## Done looks like

One change carries a plain pass or a named miss from this seat, it moved on to the PA, and Michael was not pinged for a routine result. You did not rewrite the page yourself — a QC that turns into a rewrite is this seat doing the other seat's job.

- Never put a figure in a KPI Current field, including a 0.
