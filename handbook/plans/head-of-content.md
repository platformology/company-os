## The one job

**Take the next named unit on the Competitive Writing Guide draft: the Competitive buying-style answer for one field on the Methodical list, written only from a sourced extract Michael provides.**

The five fields are already sourced off the page — **Characteristics, Buying needs, Likes and dislikes, Use, Avoid.** That list is settled; this seat does not reword it, reorder it, or look for a sixth. What is still open is the Competitive-style answer against those same five, and that is a named gap in [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md).

**"Competitive" names the reader, not a rival company.** This is a buying style. It is not a competitor teardown, and no rival, feature, price, or claim gets invented to fill a field.

**No extract for a field? Leave it blank and write "not sourced."** Not a guess, not a paraphrase of the field above it, not a line borrowed from another style. A later writer reads invented language as observed speech and puts it into public copy — that is the failure this rule exists to stop.

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). Nothing off this draft goes to a client or the public without Michael.
2. Open the [Competitive Writing Guide draft](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81239c5fc767bddc0823). Read the Methodical field names and their order **off the page**, not from memory.
3. Pick **one** field. One unit per pass, then the gate — not all five "while I'm here."
4. Do you have a sourced extract from Michael for it — a persona quote, a piece of sourced writing, a document or link he handed you? If yes, write the Competitive answer and **keep the source next to the words**, the way the page already does.
5. If not: leave it blank and write **"not sourced."** That is a finished pass. Naming the hole is a real result, not a failed one — see [runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md).
6. **One style per page.** Do not produce Spontaneous or Humanistic copy off this draft. Training names in the old persona decks are not clients, and none of them moves onto a client list from here.
7. Corpus question rather than guide question? Catalogue what exists; do not write new material to fill a hole in the catalogue. The buyer packet drawn from the corpus runs through [run-buyer-packet](../../skills/run-buyer-packet/SKILL.md).
8. Hand the finished thing to the [CMO](../seats/cmo.md), who owns the function this sits inside — see [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md). Then run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) and stop.
