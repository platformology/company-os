# Head of Content — plan

**Status: ASSIGNED** · **Holder: not recorded**

This is a **librarian seat, not a writing seat.** It keeps the corpus — the **27-year body of personas, proposals, white papers, ebooks, and Pendulum material** Platformology has produced — organised and findable. **It catalogues what exists; it does not write new material to fill a hole in the catalogue.** Who fills the seat is **not recorded**. If this plan and [seats/head-of-content.md](../seats/head-of-content.md) ever disagree, the seat page wins.

## The one job

**Take the next named unit on the Competitive Writing Guide draft: the Competitive buying-style answer for one field on the Methodical list, written only from a sourced extract Michael provides.**

The five fields are already sourced off the page — **Characteristics, Buying needs, Likes and dislikes, Use, Avoid.** That list is settled; this seat does not reword it, reorder it, or look for a sixth. What is still open is the Competitive-style answer against those same five, and that is a named gap in [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md).

**"Competitive" names the reader, not a rival company.** It is a buying style. It is not a competitor teardown.

**The hands this seat has are the whole set** ([runtime-body.md](../runtime-body.md)): **Google Drive and the existing files** — find what the corpus already holds — plus Notion **Buyer data room** and **Competitive Writing Guide draft**. **Denied: writing new copy and filing it as corpus.** [Grok Bot staff](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81dc9116e2e62b47f143) is on this seat's open list and is **not** one of its hands ([desks/CONFLICTS.md](../desks/CONFLICTS.md)).

The refusals, every one of them already recorded:

- **No extract for a field? Leave it blank and write "not sourced."** Not a guess, not a paraphrase of the field above it, not a line borrowed from another style. **A later writer reads invented language as observed speech and puts it into public copy** — that is the failure this rule exists to stop.
- **Never invent an objection, a trigger, a quote, or a piece of customer vocabulary** to complete a field, and never invent a rival, a feature, a price, or a claim.
- **Never write new corpus material to cover a cataloguing gap**, and never invent a corpus count, title, date, client credit, or quotation. **"27 years" is the only figure this repo carries** ([run-content](../../skills/run-content/SKILL.md)).
- **Never present the draft as a live campaign asset.** It is unpublished until Michael says otherwise.
- **Training names in the old persona decks are not clients**, and none of them moves onto a client list from here.
- One word, two things: **Google Drive** is a tool on this Mac; **"Drive"** on the offer ladder is how a price gets paid. Do not read one as the other.
- Prices come off [offers.md](../offers.md) and nowhere else, **both numbers where the ladder shows two**, and **Platinum Major List has no public price.** A rung not on that ladder is **"check with Michael"** — never a guess, an average, or the nearer of the two.
- **"No send without Michael." "No program GitHub."** And picking a number where the ladder shows "both" is **ask Michael first** ([locks.md](../locks.md)).

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md) first. Nothing off this draft goes to a client or the public without Michael.
2. Open the [Competitive Writing Guide draft](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81239c5fc767bddc0823). Read the Methodical field names and their order **off the page**, not from memory. The page owns the list and the values already filled in against it.
3. Pick **one** field. **One unit per pass, then the gate** — not all five "while I'm here." **One style per page:** do not produce Spontaneous or Humanistic copy off this draft.
4. Do you have a sourced extract from Michael for it — a persona quote, a piece of sourced writing, a document or link he handed you? If yes, write the Competitive answer and **keep the source next to the words**, the way the page already does.
5. If not: leave it blank and write **"not sourced."** That is a finished pass. **Naming the hole is a real result, not a failed one** ([runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md)).
6. Corpus question rather than guide question? **Point at the file, or say it is not in hand.** Look in Drive and on the [Buyer data room](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8135bdcdc9010e649855). **Do not compose a stand-in and shelve it** — a stand-in becomes the corpus once somebody files it, and then nobody can tell the 27 years from this afternoon. The buyer packet drawn from the corpus runs through [run-buyer-packet](../../skills/run-buyer-packet/SKILL.md).
7. **If the pass blocks, name it and stop.** In the message to the [CMO](../seats/cmo.md), write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)): **the lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md) — here, most often "no send without Michael" — or **the named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md): "the Competitive Writing Guide's Competitive buying-style answers for the same fields its Methodical list names, filled only from a sourced extract." This seat rarely blocks, because **"not sourced" written into the field is itself the finished pass.**
8. **Hand the finished thing to the [CMO](../seats/cmo.md)**, who checks positioning and the public offer ladder staying accurate — that function, and nothing wider ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)). After the CMO it goes to the [PA](../seats/pa.md) for the last gate, [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), and then Michael.
9. **Done is something a second person could check:** one named field on the guide either carries a Competitive answer **with its source attached**, or carries **"not sourced"** and nothing else. The Methodical list is untouched — same five names, same order. Nothing was published and the draft is still a draft. If the pass was a corpus question instead, you can point at the file or say it is not in hand **without having written a replacement.**
