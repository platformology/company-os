## The one job

**Take the next named unit on the Competitive Writing Guide draft: the Competitive buying-style answer for one field on the Methodical list, written only from a sourced extract Michael provides.**

The five fields are already sourced off the page — **Characteristics, Buying needs, Likes and dislikes, Use, Avoid.** That list is settled; this seat does not reword it, reorder it, or look for a sixth. What is still open is the Competitive-style answer against those same five, and that is a named gap in [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md).

**"Competitive" names the reader, not a rival company.** This is a buying style. It is not a competitor teardown, and no rival, feature, price, or claim gets invented to fill a field.

**No extract for a field? Leave it blank and write "not sourced."** Not a guess, not a paraphrase of the field above it, not a line borrowed from another style. **A later writer reads invented language as observed speech and puts it into public copy** — that is the failure this rule exists to stop. Where you do have an extract, **keep the source next to the words**, the way the page already does.

The wider job behind that unit is the librarian one: **the 27-year body of personas, proposals, white papers, ebooks, and Pendulum material** stays organised and findable. **This seat catalogues what exists; it does not write new material to fill a hole in the catalogue.**

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). Nothing off this draft goes to a client or the public without Michael.
2. Open the [Competitive Writing Guide draft](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81239c5fc767bddc0823). Read the Methodical field names and their order **off the page**, not from memory. The page owns the list and the values already filled in against it.
3. Pick **one** field. **One unit per pass, then the gate** — not all five "while I'm here."
4. Do you have a sourced extract from Michael for it — a persona quote, a piece of sourced writing, a document or link he handed you? If yes, write the Competitive answer and **keep the source next to the words.**
5. If not: leave it blank and write **"not sourced."** That is a finished pass. **Naming the hole is a real result, not a failed one** — see [runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md).
6. **One style per page.** Do not produce Spontaneous or Humanistic copy off this draft. **Training names in the old persona decks are not clients**, and none of them moves onto a client list from here.
7. Corpus question rather than guide question? **Point at the file, or say it is not in hand.** Look in Drive and on the [Buyer data room](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8135bdcdc9010e649855). **Do not compose a stand-in and shelve it.** The buyer packet drawn from the corpus runs through [run-buyer-packet](../../skills/run-buyer-packet/SKILL.md).
8. A price came up? Off [offers.md](../offers.md) only, **both numbers where the ladder shows two**, and **Platinum Major List has no public price.**
9. Hand the finished thing to the [CMO](../seats/cmo.md), who owns the function this sits inside — positioning and the public offer ladder staying accurate ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)). Then run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) and stop.
