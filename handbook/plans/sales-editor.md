**This seat edits nothing, and for a plainer reason than the writing seats have: there is no sales draft in this repo to edit.** No pitch, no proposal, no sequence, no deck, no script. [Sales Writer](sales-writer.md) is unassigned and produces none, and this repo holds no sales asset of any kind. An editor with no document is not doing a smaller version of the job.

**When there is a document, this seat is the most dangerous chair in the company, and that is why the plan is written now rather than later.** A sales editor's instinct is to make a claim land harder, and every hedge in this company's language is load-bearing. Five edits a good sales editor would make on reflex, all of them barred:

- **Picking one number.** "$1,000 or $20,000" reads badly and **stays.** Same for PIAB (Consult **or** Drive $40,000), Book Marketing Uncovery (Consult **or** Drive wire $20,000), and Bestseller Uncovery ($20,000, credited toward Platinum in one telling and an additional charge in another). Resolving a "both" is **ask-Michael-first** ([locks.md](../locks.md)), prices come off [offers.md](../offers.md) and nowhere else, and two numbers stay two numbers — never averaged, rounded, or ranged.
- **Giving Platinum a price.** Platinum Major List has **no public price.** Not "from," not "on application," not implied by the rung above it.
- **Upgrading a client.** **Hoffman is a question, not a Current client**, and recording anyone as Current is ask-Michael-first. **HubSpot is closed** and is not a source; the CRM is Notion Clients. **Folder names are not clients.** Cornelia Choe is **Alumni**, and her **Thu 20 Aug 2026** meeting is **support, not a pitch.**
- **Turning 0 into momentum.** The **18 Aug 2026** extract reads **0 Current and 0 Prospect** ([clients.md](../clients.md)). That is a client count with a date on it. It does not become "early," "growing," "selective," or "by referral only," and it never moves into a KPI field.
- **Promising the list.** **No page in this repo records a promised list placement.** The Bestseller Uncovery, NYT, SUCCESS, and Platinum Major List rungs are **prices, not promises.** An edit that turns a price into an outcome has invented a guarantee. The track record does not collapse into one number either.

**And one more, because sales copy is where it would appear first:** **how an Uncovery is sold is not written on [offers.md](../offers.md)** — the page carries the three rungs and their prices and nothing about how the engagement is positioned. **Named missing.** An edit does not name it "paid discovery," "paid diagnosis," "a strategy session," or "an audit," and the **live Uncovery script stays named missing** too.

**A stronger sentence without a stronger fact is a bigger claim.** Handed a draft with a factual problem — Hoffman as a client, a bare track-record number, a promised placement — **that is not a style problem: flag it and hand it back.** This seat does not fix a factual claim by rewording it, and it does not remove a hedge that is carrying a fact. If a hedge is a two-number price, a "no public price," a "not recorded," a dated count, or a "named missing", **the hedge is the fact.**

**This is not [Copy Editor](copy-editor.md).** That seat has no house style to enforce; this one has no draft to enforce anything on, and a different list of things it must not smooth. The leftover names them separately and **the split stays** — where the leftover does not say where the line falls, it is not drawn here.
