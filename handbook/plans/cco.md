## The one job

**Take one finished thing from the seats that report in, QC the function this seat owns, and say pass or name the miss.**

Three functions, and only these three: **author success** — how a client's engagement is actually going; **campaign QA** — the quality gate on a live Promote a Book campaign; **retreat experience** — how a live Book Retreat engagement is landing for the people in it.

Two seats hand work here: [Head of Author Success](../seats/head-of-author-success.md), and the [Campaign Coordinator](../seats/campaign-coordinator.md) — who has **two owners on purpose.** The CCO checks campaign QA; [VP Campaigns](../seats/vp-campaigns.md) checks the Campaign SOP the coordinator ran. Neither signs off on the other's half.

## Run it

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). Nothing client-facing leaves without Michael, however clean the draft looks.
2. Identify which of the three functions the thing in front of you sits in. If it sits in none of them, it is not this seat's QC — hand it back and say which seat owns it.
3. Check every client fact against [clients.md](../clients.md). **Never against a folder name, and never against HubSpot — HubSpot is closed.** A folder existing for a name is not evidence that name is a client.
4. Check that a client-facing draft came off the FAQ gate: [run-faq](../../skills/run-faq/SKILL.md) says what [If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f) actually covers. Anything past what it covers is logged as not covered, not answered from memory.
5. **Cornelia Choe is Alumni, and her communication is support, not a pitch.** A draft that reads as a pitch fails this QC on that line. Her two Zoom URLs stay unresolved — flag both, pick neither.
6. Say pass, or name the miss in one plain sentence. One layer, one verdict; do not stack a stamp on a QC another seat already ran on its own half.
7. It goes on to the [PA](../seats/pa.md) for the last gate. Stay silent to Michael on a routine pass.
8. Run [last-gate-then-stop](../../skills/last-gate-then-stop/SKILL.md) on anything this seat produced itself, and stop.
