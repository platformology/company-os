3. Asked when they will close, or by how much they are off? **Neither is recorded.** That is named missing, and **a projected date is a guess wearing a number.**

5. Asked for a ledger figure? **There is no Bench connector**, so nothing here reads a balance. Say who opened Bench by hand, or say the number is not in hand. **Do not describe a live pull that does not exist.**
6. A bills, Bench, or savings question is not this seat's — hand it to the [Bookkeeper](../seats/bookkeeper.md). A price in anything you write comes off [offers.md](../offers.md), **both numbers where the ladder shows two** (Multi-author **$1,000 or $20,000**, PIAB **Consult or Drive $40,000**, Book Marketing Uncovery **Consult or Drive wire $20,000**, Digital **$35,000 or $35,000 + $40,000 bonus**, Bestseller Uncovery **$20,000** stated two ways). **Platinum Major List has no public price**, and a rung not on that ladder is **"check with Michael."**
7. **If the pass blocks, name it and stop.** In the message to the [CFO](../seats/cfo.md), write one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)): **the lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md) — here, most often "Blue Sky stays off the LLC books" or "no send without Michael" — or **the named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md): "June and July 2026 are not closed," "no Bench savings total, and no Bench connector," or "no invented invoice, accounts-receivable figure, or savings total." **An open month is a fact to report, not a problem to solve with an estimate.**
8. **Hand the finished thing to the [CFO](../seats/cfo.md)**, who checks the monthly close inside how money moves — that function, and nothing wider ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)). After the CFO it goes to the [PA](../seats/pa.md) for the last gate, [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), and then Michael.
