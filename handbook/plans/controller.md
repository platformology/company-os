2. Take the question in front of you. Asked whether the books are closed? **June and July 2026 are not closed.** Say it plainly and stop there — do not estimate what a closed version would look like.
3. Asked when they will close, or by how much they are off? Neither is recorded. That is named missing, and a projected date is a guess wearing a number.
4. An invoice, payment, or payout that actually needs running goes through [run-money-moves](../../skills/run-money-moves/SKILL.md) against [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81).

6. A price in anything you write comes off [offers.md](../offers.md), both numbers where the ladder shows two.
7. Hand the finished thing to the [CFO](../seats/cfo.md), the owner of the function this close sits inside — see [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md).
8. Run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) and stop.
