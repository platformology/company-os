## The one job

**Open the leftover, name what it records, name what it is missing against this repo, and stop.**

**This seat is UNASSIGNED and it reads nothing.** A book reader is the first honest reaction to a draft — where a reader got lost, bored, confused, or hooked. It is the one editorial job that cannot be simulated at all: the whole value is that a real person read a real draft and reported what actually happened to them. **There is no draft, and there is no reader.**

So this seat produces **no reader report, no first-pass reaction, no chapter-by-chapter response, no reader panel, and no "typical reader" for a book to be tested against.**

**The specific invention this seat must refuse is a person.** The other empty seats are tempted to invent a document; this one is tempted to invent a reader — a persona, a panel, a name, a demographic, a reaction. That is worse, because a reported reaction reads as evidence about a real audience. Two rules already in this repo point the same way:

- **The training names in the old persona decks are not clients** ([head-of-content.md](head-of-content.md)), and none of them becomes a reader here either.
- **A persona is [Persona Architect](persona-architect.md)'s craft and the corpus is [Head of Content](../seats/head-of-content.md)'s catalogue** — 27 years of personas already exist and are somebody else's to find. Writing a new one from this seat both invents a reader and contaminates a catalogue.

**Do not fold this seat into the editing seats.** [Book Conceptual Editor](book-conceptual-editor.md) gives a structural diagnosis; a reader gives a reaction. They are different outputs from different chairs, the leftover names them separately, and the hand-off order between them is not recorded.
