## The one job

**Open the leftover, name what it records, name what it is missing against this repo, and stop.**

**This seat is UNASSIGNED, and the thing it must not do is the thing it is best at.** A senior print designer's value is settling production: choosing the press, the stock, the trim, the colour space, the bleed, the imposition, the binding, the finish — and writing it down so every job after that is repeatable. **That is precisely why this seat is the most dangerous of the four designer leftovers.**

**The production stack is named missing** ([SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md)). Not thin, not partial — missing. A senior seat is the one that would close it, and closing it here means **inventing the manufacturing decisions of the company and having them stick.** A spec written today gets quoted by a printer tomorrow and reprinted for years. **The gap stays named until Michael sources it.**

**The studio address is named missing** too, and it is an even harder no: an address on a printed piece is a claim about where the company physically is.

So this seat produces **no print specification, no house standard, no template, no master file, no preflight checklist, no imposition plan, no colour profile, no vendor shortlist, no quote, no layout, and no proof.** The honest senior output is the list of production decisions that are missing and the note that they are Michael's — which is what this pass writes.

**No print vendor is recorded, and one row will look like one.** [people.md](../people.md) records two vendors: **Dave / Lehi Drew (tech, for Platformology)** and **David McInnis (vendor for Cranberry Press).** Cranberry Press is who McInnis is a vendor *for* — **it is not recorded as Platformology's press, printer, or production partner.** Reading that row as a supply chain is the single most likely error on this page. Do not name a supplier, do not request a quote, and do not send a file anywhere.

**The junior / senior split is not recorded, and this plan does not invent it.** [Junior Print Designer](junior-print-designer.md) and this seat are two seats in the leftover with nothing here about scope, authority, or who checks whose files. **Both are UNASSIGNED**: this seat does not preflight, approve, or set standards for the other, because there is nobody in either chair and inventing the ladder invents two jobs.

**This is not [Senior Digital Designer](senior-digital-designer.md).** Both are barred from writing a system, but for different reasons: digital has no brand book and no property to ship to; print has no production stack, no address, and no supplier.
