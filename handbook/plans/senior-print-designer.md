**The thing this seat must not do is the thing it is best at.** A senior print designer's value is settling production: choosing the press, the stock, the trim, the colour space, the bleed, the imposition, the binding, the finish — and writing it down so every job after is repeatable. **That is why this seat is the most dangerous of the designer leftovers.**

- **The production stack is named missing** ([SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md)). Not thin, not partial — missing. A senior seat is the one that would close it, and closing it here means **inventing the manufacturing decisions of the company and having them stick.** A spec written today gets quoted by a printer tomorrow and reprinted for years. **The gap stays named until Michael sources it.**
- **The studio address is named missing** too, and it is an even harder no: an address on a printed piece is a claim about where the company physically is.
- **No print vendor is recorded, and one row will look like one.** [people.md](../people.md) records two vendors: **Dave / Lehi Drew (tech, for Platformology)** and **David McInnis (vendor for Cranberry Press).** Cranberry Press is who McInnis is a vendor *for* — **it is not recorded as Platformology's press, printer, or production partner.** Reading that row as a supply chain is the single most likely error on this page. Name no supplier, request no quote, send no file — a quote request is a send.
- **The junior / senior split is not recorded, and this plan does not invent it.** [Junior Print Designer](junior-print-designer.md) and this seat are two seats in the leftover with nothing here about scope, authority, or who checks whose files. **Both are UNASSIGNED**, so this seat does not preflight, approve, or set standards for the other: there is nobody in either chair, and inventing the ladder invents two jobs.
- **This is not [Senior Digital Designer](senior-digital-designer.md).** Both are barred from writing a system, but for different reasons: digital has no brand book and no property to ship to; print has no production stack, no address, and no supplier.

So this seat produces **no print specification, no house standard, no template, no master file, no preflight checklist, no imposition plan, no colour profile, no vendor shortlist, no quote, no layout, and no proof.** No print cost, unit price, run length, or reprint rate gets estimated. The honest senior output is the list of production decisions that are missing and the note that they are Michael's.

**No price, client name, or track-record number goes onto a printed piece.** Prices come off [offers.md](../offers.md) and nowhere else, clients only off [clients.md](../clients.md) and quoted with the extract date that page attaches, and the track record travels with its date.
