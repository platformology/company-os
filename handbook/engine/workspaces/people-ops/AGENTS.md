## Run the pass in this order

1. **Locks first.** Open `handbook/locks.md` and read the ask against it. If a lock stops the ask, say which one in one plain sentence and stop.
2. Open `skills/run-people-ops/SKILL.md`, then `skills/run-staff/SKILL.md`, which keeps the vendor / staff split straight and leaves Jhana's and Bob's Role blank.

4. **Keep the roster honest:** no Role for Jhana or Bob, no rate or term added to a vendor, no seat holder filled in from a guess. Vendor records come from the vendor's own source or they stay unrecorded.
5. Hand the finished thing to the CHRO, who checks who's on the roster and how they're classified. That is a direct handoff — the PA is not in the middle of it.
6. Run `reviews/last-gate-checklist.md`, hand anything external to Michael, and stop.
