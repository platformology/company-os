# Pipelines

There is one pipeline here and it is the five money-move jobs. It is never a forecast and never a number. Asked for "the pipeline," name these five jobs.

## The five jobs

Attract → Diagnose → Place → Deliver → Expand alumni. In that order, already written up job by job on how-money-moves.md, which says what triggers each one and where the real instructions live.

Attract points at the public ladder, not a pitch. Diagnose is what the Uncovery offers are for. Place matches a client to a rung that already exists. Deliver is a live campaign, retreat, or PIAB engagement, and those SOPs live in Notion — this repo does not restate them. Expand alumni is the follow-up after an engagement is finished.

Named missing on those pages stays named. The lead magnet has no Offers row. The membership price is not recorded. The live Uncovery script is not recorded, and how an Uncovery is sold is not written on offers.md. Platinum Major List has no public price — say so rather than quoting one. Where the ladder shows two numbers, both travel.

Head of Demand, Head of Sales, and SDR are leftover-file assigned on the Place job. Who fills each is not recorded.

## Alumni support

Expand alumni is one relationship today. 1 Alumni, 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect on the 18 Aug 2026 extract — those counts travel with that date and none of them moves into a KPI. KPI Current stays empty.

Cornelia Choe is the Alumni, and hers is Alumni support, not a pitch. Two Zoom URLs for her meeting stay unresolved. Do not pick one; flag both to Michael.

## QC

COO first QC → PA last-gate → merge. The COO is first QC, not the only one, and does not merge. The PA runs the last-gate checklist and then merges. Anything external stops there and goes to Michael. Nothing sends.

Those three steps are the whole of it. No stage gets added to them here.

## What "pipeline" never means here

Not a forecast. Not inbound volume, a lead list, a lead count, a close rate, or a consult count — none of those is recorded, and none gets built out of the client counts. Not a second thing invented beside the five jobs. A hole gets written as "not recorded" rather than filled.
