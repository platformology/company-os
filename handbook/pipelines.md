# Pipelines — the map

A pipeline here is named work a person can open. It is never a forecast and never a number.

## Company pipelines

Attract → Diagnose → Place → Deliver → Expand alumni. Five jobs, in order. Asked for "the pipeline," those five jobs are the answer. Named missing on those pages stays named, and no figure gets derived from them.

## The forty-three leftover-file employees

Each one has three pipelines on its own page: Incoming is who hands the seat work, Leftover is the hole already named on that seat's leftover page, Outgoing is who the seat reports to and then QC. QC is the same three steps everywhere: COO first QC → PA last-gate → merge.

Who fills each seat stays not recorded, except COO — Anthony C. Garcia. Head of Engineering, Developer, and CTO are not in this table.

## What "pipeline" never means here

Not a forecast. Not inbound volume, a lead list, a lead count, a close rate, or a consult count — none of those is recorded, and none gets built out of the client counts. KPI Current stays empty. Named missing stays named, and a hole gets written as "not recorded" rather than filled. Nothing sends without Michael. No program GitHub.
