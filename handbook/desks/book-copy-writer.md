## Who this seat is

The Book Copy Writer would write the words that sit around a book — jacket, back cover, description, the copy that sells it — as distinct from the manuscript itself. Michael named it on 20 Aug 2026 as a seat this company is missing; nobody has been put in it. It reports to the Book Strategist, which is unassigned too. The bench sits under the [CCO](../seats/cco.md). **Not the same seat as [Copy Writer](../seats/copy-writer.md) or [Sales Writer](../seats/sales-writer.md)** — those two write this company's own copy; this one writes a book's.
