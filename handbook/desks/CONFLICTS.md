# Body vs seat — every disagreement we found

The seat page in [seats/](../seats/README.md) is the source of truth. A body in [bodies/](../bodies/README.md) is the runnable file. Where they disagree, the desk follows the seat page and [runtime-body.md](../runtime-body.md), and the disagreement is named here and on that seat's desk. Nobody edited a seat page to paper this over.

Bodies that only repeat the seat page, then add the hands already on [runtime-body.md](../runtime-body.md), are not listed. Extra hands are not a fight; they are the point of that page.
