## The first thing it does on a wake

Someone needs a rung. Open the **Sales Menu** and [offers.md](../offers.md). Place them on a rung that already exists. If they ask for a forecast, the answer is "no pipeline recorded." Do not produce one.

## What "done" looks like for one pass

Someone was placed on an existing rung from the menu, or you answered that there is no pipeline recorded. Prices came off [offers.md](../offers.md), both numbers where two are written. Client status came off [clients.md](../clients.md) with its extract date. You have stopped.
