## Outgoing — no reports-to recorded, and this seat is the last gate

No reports-to line is recorded on the seat page. This is the seat that last-gates a docs change and then merges it, after the COO's first QC.

Then QC, the same three steps for every seat: COO first QC → PA last-gate → merge. The COO is first QC, not the only one, and does not merge. Anything external stops with Michael. Nothing sends.
