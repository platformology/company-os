This seat reports to Book Strategist, under the CCO. This is not Book Writer, Copy Writer, or Sales Writer — the leftover names all four separately.

## Leftover — unassigned, which of two jobs unrecorded, no book either way

Three holes, all already named on the leftover page.

- Which of two jobs this name is is not recorded — the copy that sells a book, or copy written inside one.

Do not pick which job. Do not write jacket copy, a blurb, a bio, or in-book copy. Do not invent a book, an author, or KPI Current.

Reports to Book Strategist, under the CCO.
