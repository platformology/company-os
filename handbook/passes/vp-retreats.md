## What this seat records, and what it does not

**Two SOPs, one seat, and the ask belongs to exactly one of them.** A **Book Retreat** question runs off the [Retreat SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8185b9a9f19dd56389e5); a **Platform in a Box** question runs off the [PIAB SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81d5beecda81a111e6b3). **One of them, not a blend.** An answer stitched from both reads more complete than either and cannot be traced to a place on either page, which is the failure that matters: the next person cannot go and read the same lines. **Name which SOP, and which part of it, you answered off.**

**Where these sit in the company, so you know what you are being asked about.** **"A retreat is running"** and **"A PIAB engagement is running"** are **two separate steps** of the [weekday loop](../weekday-loop.md), and both land at **Deliver**, job 4 of the five on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md) — the one that fires when an engagement is live and money is moving. Both routes end on a SOP page, and neither of them is a description of a retreat or an engagement this repo holds.

**A "live engagement" needs a sourced client behind it.** The **18 Aug 2026 extract records 0 Current clients** ([clients.md](../clients.md)) — 1 Alumni (Cornelia Choe, The Leaders Alliance), 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect — and that count travels with its date. So an ask that begins "on the retreat we're running" is an ask about something this repo does not record, and saying so is the answer. **A folder name is not a client. Hoffman is a question, not a Current client. HubSpot is closed**, and recording anybody as Current or Prospect is ask-Michael-first ([locks.md](../locks.md)). **Cornelia Choe is Alumni, and her communication is support, not a pitch** — she is not a guest to seat at a retreat.

What this pass produces is **one ownership question answered off a named part of one SOP, with the blanks left blank and production sitting where it belongs.** What it does **not** produce, each for its own reason rather than as one shrug:

- **A retreat date, a guest list, or a venue.** **None is sourced in this repo.** Not as a placeholder, not as an example, and not as a "typical" shape — see below.
- **The retreat, produced.** That is the [Retreat Producer](../seats/retreat-producer.md)'s. Reading the SOP is yours; producing off it is not.
- **A sixth field on the guest intake, or a blank one filled.** See below.
- **A cycle time.** See below; the figure is not recorded and does not get worked out.

### Production goes down, and no plausible one goes in

**A retreat date, a guest list, and a venue are not recorded here.** That is the whole answer when one is asked for, and it is the answer that gets quietly filled in, because a plausible date is easier to write than an absence and reads afterwards exactly like a sourced one. **Hand production down rather than filling in a plausible one** — the Producer works off the [Retreat SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8185b9a9f19dd56389e5) and the live guest records in Notion, and this seat has neither in hand.

The reach here is a two-step one rather than a one-step one, and it is worth naming: the seat that owns how a retreat runs is the seat best placed to sketch the date, the room list, and the venue. **A sketch is indistinguishable afterwards from a plan somebody agreed**, and writing one would have made the engagement real by making up the thing it describes.

### The guest intake starter form stays a starter form

**The starter form stays as it is: five fields, and no sixth gets added here.** That question was already asked and answered on this seat — **the starter form stays** — and it is settled on [plans/vp-retreats.md](../plans/vp-retreats.md), so it does not get re-argued on this pass. **A pass that finds the form thin has found the form as agreed.**

**Which five are on it lives on the [blank guest intake draft](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b812486c8eac90777658c) in Notion. Open that page rather than restating it from here.** Whatever is blank there **stays blank**, and the **dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, and incident** fields **stay named missing** until that page says otherwise ([SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md)). **A blank left blank is the finished answer, not a gap in the pass.**

Two ways that goes wrong, and both look like helpfulness while you are doing it:

- **Filling a blank from a comparable retreat's form**, or from what a guest like this one usually needs. A value carried across from somewhere else reads afterwards exactly like one the guest gave you, and nobody downstream can tell which it was.
- **Treating a sales application as this form, or this form as a sales application.** They are different documents with different owners. **A sales application is not the guest intake**, and routing one through it puts buying answers where arrival answers go.

**Asked to add a field? Say it is not on the form yet, and flag it to Michael** ([blank-guest-intake](../../skills/blank-guest-intake/SKILL.md)). That is a finished pass.

## The note you leave

Where the work is — on the pull request, or in the message you hand to the COO. Four lines, and nothing more:

- **Where things stand.** One sentence somebody who was not here can act on.
- **The one unit I took**, and where it ended up: one ownership question answered off a named part of one SOP with production handed down, or a stop on a named lock.
- **What is still open**, each with its actual next step. "Follow up" is not a next step.
- **What I could not answer**, in this repo's own words: not recorded.

Two things make that note worth reading. **Never invent a name, an owner, a date, a figure, or a link to make a line look finished** — an unknown left as an unknown is the useful answer, and a plausible filler reads to the next person as something you checked. And **never quietly drop an item the last note left open**: if it closed, say it closed; if it did not, carry it forward.

That second rule bites in a particular way here, because the open items on this seat are absences that time makes feel resolved. **An unrecorded cycle time does not age into a recorded one** — the more passes that run without one, the more obviously a plausible number of days would fit, and that is the pressure rather than the permission. **A blank intake field does not age into a filled one**, however many guests have since been through something like it. **An absent retreat date does not age into a date.** **A seat with nobody recorded in it does not become filled by habit**, however many times it has been run from. **Hoffman being a question does not age into being a Current client**, and **0 Current clients on an extract does not age into a live engagement** because time has passed since the extract. Carry each one forward in the same words until somebody actually changes it.

Two things have to be in the note explicitly, because the whole pass is two moves that read as one. **Say which SOP you answered off — Retreat or PIAB — and which part of it.** And **say that production went down to the Retreat Producer.** A note reading "handled the retreat question" reads like a note where both halves happened, and the next person hands it on believing they did.

No stamps, no status tags, no run identifiers. A person reads this.
