**One unit, then the gate.** One author's engagement. Not two, and not a second one "while I'm here."

**The one job has two halves and they run in that order: say how one author's engagement is actually going, using only facts that are recorded — then hold the FAQ gate on anything that gets drafted back to them.** The first half is a reading. The second half is a refusal. A pass that does the reading and then answers past the gate has failed the half that was harder.

**The failure this pass exists to prevent is a status assembled out of things that are not the record.** A folder with an author's name on it. A filename with a date in it. An old HubSpot record. A calendar entry from last spring. Each one looks like evidence that an engagement exists and is moving, and **not one of them is a client fact** — those live in [clients.md](../clients.md) and nowhere else, and **HubSpot is closed** and is not a system of record. **Notion Clients is the CRM.**

**Silence is the same failure wearing better clothes.** An author nobody has heard from is not an author whose engagement is going well, and a quiet account is not a healthy one — **the absence of a complaint is not a reading.** Where nothing is recorded, the answer is that nothing is recorded, said in those words.

**And "going well" has nothing to be measured against here anyway.** **No milestone schedule for any author is recorded in this repo**, and the 18 Aug 2026 extract counts **0 Current** clients — so there is no live engagement to read a milestone off even if a schedule existed. That is the state, and it is the answer rather than the reason to construct one.

## The pass

**1. Read the ask against the locks, first, before you open anything else.** [locks.md](../locks.md), against the thing actually in front of you — this author, this message, this call — rather than the seat in general. The one that bites here more than anywhere: **"No send without Michael"** — and on this seat the hands page says it a second way, **Denied: sending**, so a reply that reads as finished is still a draft. With it, **"No program GitHub."** And two ask-Michael-first lines land here more than on any other seat: **"recording a client as Current, Prospect, or otherwise moving them off 'question' status (e.g., Hoffman)"** and **"choosing between the two unresolved Zoom URLs for the Cornelia Choe meeting."** The machine locks hold too: no VMs, no GKE, **no local model until Michael says go** and **Qwen stays stopped**, and the M1 is the office while the M5 is the shop and stays parked. If a lock stops the ask, quote it in one sentence and stop there.

**2. Work out which ask you have — an outcome question or a client-facing draft.** They have different finished shapes, and answering the one you are better at is how the other quietly goes unanswered. If a reply or a call brief is the job, jump to step 5.

**3. Open [clients.md](../clients.md) and establish who you are actually talking about, with the 18 Aug 2026 extract date attached.** One named author, read off that page. **A folder name is not a client, HubSpot is closed, and Hoffman is a question rather than a Current client.** If the name in front of you is not on that page, that is the answer — not a prompt to go looking for a record somewhere else that would make it one.

**4. Say how that engagement is going, using only what is recorded — and where nothing is recorded, say nothing is recorded.** In those words. **Do not reconstruct a status** from a folder, a filename, a HubSpot record, or a calendar entry, and **do not read silence as progress or as trouble.** Three near-misses count as "not recorded" too, and each one is easier to talk yourself into than a straight invention: **a Past client's old engagement is not a current one**, **a scheduled call is not an engagement status**, and **an author who has not complained has not reported anything.** With **0 Current clients on that extract, there is no live engagement recorded at all** — say that, rather than describing how one is presumably going.

**5. A client wrote in, or a call is coming.** [run-faq](../../skills/run-faq/SKILL.md) says what [If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f) actually covers; [author-support-reply](../../skills/author-support-reply/SKILL.md) drafts the reply; [support-call-prep](../../skills/support-call-prep/SKILL.md) builds the brief off the FAQ and the calendar as they already stand. **Open the FAQ. Do not check the message against your memory of it**, and do not write to a category template instead of to the entry on the page — a reply written to a template has skipped the gate altogether.

**6. Not covered by the FAQ? Draft it anyway, and put the line at the top: "Not in the FAQ — flag for Michael before sending."** In those words. **You still draft. You do not answer past that gate**, and **"Not in the FAQ" is a finished answer once it is written down** — it is not a preamble to working the answer out. **Log what the page failed to cover** so the page can be fixed by whoever owns it; **noticing the gap and closing it are two different jobs**, and rewriting the FAQ is not this pass. That line is an instruction to a human, not a sentence anybody sends — if it ever got stripped and the draft went out, that is the failure this step exists to prevent.

**7. Cornelia Choe is Alumni, and her communication is support, not a pitch.** Her **Thu 20 Aug 2026, 11:00–11:30am America/Denver** meeting is **Alumni support.** A draft that reads as a pitch — a new deal, a price, an upsell, a "while we're talking" — fails on that line however warmly it is written. **Her two Zoom URLs are unresolved: flag both to Michael, pick neither.** Choosing one is ask-Michael-first, and the choice travels up the wire rather than into the brief.

**8. A price came up? Off [offers.md](../offers.md) only.** **Both numbers where the ladder shows two** — never a guess, an average, or the higher or lower of the pair — and **Platinum Major List has no public price**, said in those words rather than implied out of a nearby rung. **How an Uncovery is sold is named missing**, so nothing off this pass calls it paid discovery, paid diagnosis, a strategy session, or an audit. **Never invent an offer or a revenue figure for an account**, and **never imply a guaranteed list placement** — no page here records one.

**9. [Client Success](../seats/client-success.md) hands its finished drafts here. Check the function you own and nothing wider.** **Author outcomes and the FAQ gate** — that the draft came off the FAQ page, and that anything the page did not cover is written down as not covered. **The day-to-day message traffic is that seat's, not yours**, and picking it up means nobody ran this pass. Naming a miss and handing it back is the finished check; **rewriting the draft yourself means nobody checked the result.**

**10. Nothing goes into KPI Current.** **On-time author milestones stays a name with an empty field under it** — not a percentage, not a count of milestones hit, not a 0, and **not the 0 borrowed off the client count**, which is a different fact and travels with its own extract date.

**11. You do not last-gate and you do not merge**, and nothing reaches an author because your own check passed.

The **track record stays uncollapsed** — 124, 128, later 130–131 — it travels with its dates, and it is not an outcome for any one author.

## The note you leave

Where the work is — on the pull request, in the draft, or in the message you hand to the CCO. Four lines, and nothing more:

- **Where things stand.** One sentence somebody who was not here can act on.
- **The one unit I took**, and where it ended up: one named author's status stated from the record, one named author's status honestly named as unrecorded, a draft sitting with Michael with the FAQ gap written into it, a call brief with its gaps flagged, or a stop on a named lock.
- **What is still open**, each with its actual next step. "Follow up" is not a next step.
- **What I could not answer**, in this repo's own words: not recorded.

Two things make that note worth reading. **Never invent a name, an owner, a date, a figure, or a link to make a line look finished** — an unknown left as an unknown is the useful answer, and a plausible filler reads to the next person as something you checked. And **never quietly drop an item the last note left open**: if it closed, say it closed; if it did not, carry it forward.

That second rule bites hardest on this seat, because the open items here are about people and people look settled after a while. **Hoffman being a question does not age into being a Current client. Two unpicked Zoom URLs do not age into one.** An absent author-success SOP does not age into a present one. **A question the FAQ did not cover last month is still uncovered** — the number of passes it has sat through is not evidence that the page now answers it. And **an author nobody has heard from for a long time has still reported nothing**: a long quiet stretch is not a milestone hit, and it is not a problem either. Carry each one forward in the same words until somebody actually changes it.

One thing on this seat has to be said in the note explicitly, because the pass so often ends in a plain sentence about an absence: **say that the blank is what the record holds, not what you ran out of time for.** A note reading "checked on the author" reads like a note where a status got established, and the next person picks it up believing one exists.

No stamps, no status tags, no run identifiers. A person reads this.

## Stops that count as finished

A pass that stops honestly is a finished pass, not a failed one. Six of them:

- **A lock bit.** Quote it from [locks.md](../locks.md), in its own words — most often here, no send without Michael, the ask-first line on moving a client off "question," or the ask-first line on the two Zoom URLs.
- **A named hole was what you were asked for.** Quote it from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md), in the words that list already uses: no author-success SOP, no author milestone schedule, Hoffman's status, the two unresolved Zoom URLs.
- **Nothing is recorded about the author.** Saying so is the answer, and it is the whole answer — not the preamble to a reconstruction from a folder or a filename.
- **The FAQ did not cover it.** **"Not in the FAQ — flag for Michael before sending," written into the draft, is itself the finished pass.** The draft exists; the answer does not.
- **You could not open the FAQ.** No access, page moved, Notion down — say which page you could not open and stop. **Do not reconstruct the FAQ from this repo, from the desks, or from what a support page usually says**, and do not answer the author's question anyway.
- **The ask was another seat's, or nothing arrived.** Campaign QA and retreat experience are the [CCO](../seats/cco.md)'s; a live campaign's day-to-day is the [Campaign Coordinator](../seats/campaign-coordinator.md)'s under [VP Campaigns](../seats/vp-campaigns.md); day-to-day client message traffic is [Client Success](../seats/client-success.md)'; a price on the ladder is the [CSO](../seats/cso.md)'s to quote. Pointing it, with the seat named, is a real answer — and so is an empty in-tray.

If the block is an ask-Michael-first item, carrying it up the wire — the CCO, then the PA — is part of the stop. Otherwise: name it, stop, and **do not invent a smaller version of the ask to stay busy.**
