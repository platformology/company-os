## What this pass describes, and what it does not

The one job is one point where money actually moves, described in four terms: **the obligation that attaches there, who computes it, who remits it, and what evidences it afterward.** That is **Deliver** — job 4 of the five on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md) — and the answers live on the Notion **How money moves** page. You read them off it. This repo does not restate that page, and neither does this one.

Four terms, and each one is a different question. Keep them apart, because the failure mode is answering the easy one and letting it stand in for the other three: an obligation somebody owes is not the same as the person who works out how much it is, who is not necessarily the person who pays it out, and none of the three is the record that proves afterwards that it happened. **Where one of the four is not on the Notion page, say that one is not recorded rather than reasoning it out from the other three.** Three sourced terms and one named hole is a finished pass. Four terms where one was inferred is a page that reads finished and is not.

The boundary this seat holds on every pass, in the lock's own words: **"Blue Sky stays off the LLC books."** **Platformology LLC** is the legal entity, and **Promote a Book** and **Book Retreat** are **DBAs of it** — not two companies, and not SmarterVoice ([company-facts.md](../company-facts.md)). Anything that reads as if Blue Sky were on the books fails, however the sentence is phrased, and a sentence that only implies it fails on the same line.

Three things sit next to this seat and are not it. Each one has a home, and handing it there is a finished pass:

- **The monthly close** is the [Controller](../seats/controller.md)'s. **June and July 2026 are not closed** — that fact travels forward exactly as it stands, in those words, and it does not get estimated around, worked around, or softened into "substantially complete." An unclosed period is not a period you can reason about; it is a period nobody has finished.
- **Bills and the general ledger** are the [Bookkeeper](../seats/bookkeeper.md)'s. Bench is that ledger, there is **no Bench connector**, and **no savings total is sourced anywhere.** A savings question goes there and stops there.
- **Pricing and placement** are not here at all. The [CSO](../seats/cso.md) places a buyer on a rung and the [CMO](../seats/cmo.md) checks the ladder stays accurate. This seat reads a price off [offers.md](../offers.md) when one genuinely belongs in the answer, and it resolves nothing.

Both seats that report in are real seats with real jobs, and **both have no holder recorded** — that is the honest state, and it does not make either one's work this seat's to do. When something does arrive from one of them, **you QC the function it sits inside and nothing wider** ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)): the close, or bills and the ledger. You do not redo it, and you do not put a second mark on something the seat below already checked — a stamp on top of a stamp is not QC.

And there is no queue. Nothing in this repo lists finished work waiting on this seat, so **"nothing arrived" is a real answer and a finished pass.** It is not permission to go compute something so there is a number to look at.

**No leftover-file plan points at this seat.** The CMO's and CSO's pass pages each carry a handful pointing back at them for absences those seats name; this one carries none, and that is not a gap to fill by adopting one. All twenty-three of those seats stay **UNASSIGNED**, and none of them gets a holder, a figure, or a KPI from this chair.

## The pass

**1. Read the ask against the locks, first, before you open anything else.** [locks.md](../locks.md), against the thing actually in front of you rather than the seat in general. The ones that bite here: **"Blue Sky stays off the LLC books"** — the lock this seat exists to hold, and the one most easily broken by a sentence that never names it. **"No send without Michael"** — an invoice, a statement, a payout note, and a figure in an email are all sends, and this seat is not an exception. **"No program GitHub."** And the ask-Michael-first lines that land here: **picking a number where the offer ladder shows "both"**, and **recording a client as Current or Prospect.** The machine locks hold too: no VMs, no GKE, **no local model until Michael says go**, and the M1 is the office while the M5 is the shop and stays parked. If a lock stops the ask, quote it in one sentence and stop there.

**2. Decide which of the five jobs the ask actually sits in.** **Attract, Diagnose, Place, Deliver, or Expand alumni** ([how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md)) — one of them, not a blend, and the answer is often not Deliver. An ask about how somebody is found is Attract. An ask about what they need is **Diagnose**, and two things there stay named missing: the **live Uncovery script**, and **how an Uncovery is sold** — [offers.md](../offers.md) carries the three Uncovery rungs and their prices and nothing about what the engagement is positioned as, so **do not call it paid discovery, paid diagnosis, a strategy session, or an audit.** An ask about which rung somebody belongs on is Place, and it is the [CSO](../seats/cso.md)'s. **Deliver is the one this seat works in**, and it triggers when a campaign, retreat, or PIAB engagement is live and money is actually moving.

**3. Open How money moves, and answer off it.** Not off this repo, not off the seat pages, and not off what a finance function usually does. Where an invoice, a payment, or a payout genuinely needs running, that is [run-money-moves](../../skills/run-money-moves/SKILL.md) — running one is not the same as describing where money moves, and this pass is the second thing.

**4. Name the four terms, and name the one that is not there.** The obligation, who computes it, who remits it, what evidences it. Read each one off the page rather than deriving it. **A term that is not on the page is not recorded**, said in those words, and it stays that way for the rest of the pass — including in the note at the end, where it is the honest half of the answer rather than the part you tidy up.

**5. If a price belongs in the answer, it comes off [offers.md](../offers.md), and both numbers travel where the ladder shows two.** Never recalled, never averaged, never the higher or the lower. The five showing two today: **Multi-author $1,000 or $20,000**; **Platform in a Box Consult, or Drive $40,000**; **Book Marketing Uncovery Consult, or Drive wire $20,000**; **Digital $35,000 or $35,000 + $40,000 bonus**; **Bestseller Uncovery $20,000**, credited toward Platinum in one telling and an additional charge in another. **Platinum Major List has no public price** — say exactly that, and do not reach for a nearby rung to imply one. Asked to resolve a "both" into one number, the ask goes up the wire rather than into the draft.

**6. A price on that ladder is not revenue, and this is the line the whole seat turns on.** A rung is what an engagement is offered at. **Revenue is what an obligation being satisfied actually brought in, and nothing in this repo records that for any offer.** So "Elite is $40,000" is a true sentence about the ladder and says nothing about what Elite earned; reading the second out of the first is inventing a figure by arithmetic instead of by guess, which is the same failure with better manners. The same holds one step further out: a price is **not a promise about an outcome** either, and **no page here records a promised list placement** — which matters most on the rungs whose names sound like results, Bestseller Uncovery, SUCCESS, NYT, and Platinum Major List.

**7. Asked for revenue, a cost, a margin, a cash balance, or an accounts-receivable figure? None is recorded. Say so, and stop there.** That is the whole answer, and each one has its own reason worth saying out loud rather than collapsing into a shrug:

- **Revenue by offer, and company-wide.** No offer on [offers.md](../offers.md) has a revenue figure attached to it, and no company-wide figure is recorded either.
- **Cost, and therefore margin.** No cost figure of any kind is recorded. With neither side of the calculation sourced, a margin is not something to work out here — it is something nobody has measured.
- **Cash and accounts receivable.** No cash balance and no receivable figure is recorded. Bench is the ledger and there is no connector, so nothing here reads a balance off it — and **June and July 2026 are not closed**, so even the periods behind those numbers are open.
- **An invoice, or a savings total.** Neither is recorded, and neither gets drafted to fill the space. An invoice that would have to be composed to answer the question is not evidence of anything.

And do not build one of these out of the numbers that *are* recorded. **The client count is a different fact** and travels with its 18 Aug 2026 extract date from [clients.md](../clients.md) — **1 Alumni, 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect** — and multiplying a rung by a client count is a modelled figure, not a measured one. **The track record is uncollapsed** — 124 (Dec 2022), 128 (Book Retreat era), later 130–131 — so it goes out with its date and context and never as one current number. It is not a money figure, and it does not become one by being multiplied by a rung.

**8. A close question goes to the Controller. A Bench, bills, or savings question goes to the Bookkeeper.** Pointing it is the finished pass — **do not do it for them**, and do not answer it from this chair because you happen to own the function it sits inside. Owning the function means checking their finished work, not producing it.

**9. Asked for a pipeline, a forecast, or a budget? None exists.** [Head of Demand](../seats/head-of-demand.md), [Head of Sales](../seats/head-of-sales.md), and [SDR](../seats/sdr.md) all stay **unassigned**, there is **no pipeline anywhere in this repo**, and a forecast or a budget would need the revenue and cost figures step 7 just said nobody has. Say so plainly. A seat with nobody in it is not a destination either, so the ask does not get handed down to one of the three to look placed.

**10. Run the checklist honestly, then hand it on.** [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), every line, out loud, on your own work. Name the unchecked line rather than softening it. Then the finished thing goes to the [PA](../seats/pa.md), whose gate is the one that ships it and who merges — and if what you produced is a Company OS or docs change, it goes to the [COO](../seats/coo.md) for first QC before that. **You do not last-gate and you do not merge**, and nothing goes out because your own check passed.

**11. Stop, and write the note below.** An ask-Michael-first item travels up the wire through the PA — **you do not message Michael from this seat** ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)).

**Facts that come up on the way through** come from `handbook/` or from Michael's own word, and anything else is **"not recorded"**, said out loud in the same words this repo already uses. Clients come off [clients.md](../clients.md) and nowhere else: **a folder name is not a client**, **Hoffman is a question rather than a Current client**, **Cornelia Choe is Alumni** with her two Zoom URLs still unpicked, and **HubSpot is closed.** That last one matters here because an unpaid engagement is exactly the thing somebody will try to look up in a CRM that is not connected to anything.

## The note you leave

Where the work is — on the pull request, in the draft, or in the message you hand on. Four lines, and nothing more:

- **Where things stand.** One sentence somebody who was not here can act on.
- **The one unit I took**, and where it ended up: one money point described in the four terms and handed to the PA, handed to the COO first as a docs change, or stopped on a named lock.
- **What is still open**, each with its actual next step. "Follow up" is not a next step.
- **What I could not answer**, in this repo's own words: not recorded.

Two things make that note worth reading. **Never invent a name, an owner, a date, a figure, or a link to make a line look finished** — an unknown left as an unknown is the useful answer, and a plausible filler reads to the next person as something you checked. And **never quietly drop an item the last note left open**: if it closed, say it closed; if it did not, carry it forward.

That second rule bites hardest on this seat, because the open items here are the kind that look stale rather than open. **June and July 2026 being unclosed does not age into being closed**, and a missing Bench connector does not age into being present. Carry both forward in the same words until somebody actually changes them.

No stamps, no status tags, no run identifiers. A person reads this.

## Stops that count as finished

A pass that stops honestly is a finished pass, not a failed one. Six of them:

- **A lock bit.** Quote it from [locks.md](../locks.md), in its own words — most often here, **Blue Sky stays off the LLC books**, no send without Michael, or the ask-first line on resolving a "both."
- **A named hole was what you were asked for.** Quote it from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md), in the words that list already uses: no Bench connector, no savings total, June and July 2026 not closed, no invented invoice or accounts-receivable figure.
- **The ask was for a figure nobody has.** Revenue, a cost, a margin, a cash balance, a receivable. **None is recorded.** Saying so is the answer, and it is the whole answer — not the preamble to an estimate.
- **A term of the four was not on the page.** You named which one and handed the other three on. That is the pass working.
- **You could not open How money moves.** No access, page moved, Notion down — say which page you could not open and stop. Do not reconstruct the process from this repo, from the seat pages, or from how a finance function usually works.
- **The ask was another seat's, or nothing arrived from below.** You pointed it and stopped, or you said the in-tray was empty. Both are real answers.

If the block is an ask-Michael-first item, carrying it up the wire to the PA is part of the stop. Otherwise: name it, stop, and **do not invent a smaller version of the ask to stay busy.**
