## The pass

**1. Read the ask against the locks, first, before you open anything else.** [locks.md](../locks.md), against the thing actually in front of you rather than the seat in general. The ones that bite here: **"No send without Michael"** — and on this seat the hands page says it a second way, **Denied: sending**, so a reply that looks finished is still a draft. **"No program GitHub."** And three ask-Michael-first lines land here more than anywhere: **"recording a client as Current, Prospect, or otherwise moving them off 'question' status (e.g., Hoffman)"**, **"choosing between the two unresolved Zoom URLs for the Cornelia Choe meeting"**, and **picking a number where the offer ladder shows "both."** The machine locks hold too: no VMs, no GKE, **no local model until Michael says go**, and the M1 is the office while the M5 is the shop and stays parked. If a lock stops the ask, quote it in one sentence and stop there.

**2. Decide which of the three functions the thing actually sits in.** Author success, campaign QA, or retreat experience — read what the thing **is**, not who sent it, because a seat can hand over something outside the function it reports on. Where a thing touches two, **QC only the half this seat owns and leave the other owner theirs.** Where it touches none, the answer is **"not this seat's QC — it belongs to X"**, said with the seat named, and that is a finished pass. **Never widen a function to make the thing fit.** A stretched function is how a crossed-seat sign-off gets written: **"crossed-seat sign-off is a miss, not a pass"** ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)), and a seat that signs off on work it does not own has added a stamp rather than QC'd anything.

**3. Check every client fact against [clients.md](../clients.md), with its date attached.** The 18 Aug 2026 extract: **1 Alumni — Cornelia Choe, The Leaders Alliance — 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect.** **A folder name is not a client**, and a folder existing for a name is not evidence that name ever was one. **HubSpot is closed.** **Hoffman is a question, not a Current client**, and moving anyone off "question" is ask-Michael-first. With **0 Current clients on that extract**, a draft that describes a live engagement is describing something this repo does not record — that is the miss, and it does not get softened.

**4. Check that a client-facing draft came off the FAQ gate.** [run-faq](../../skills/run-faq/SKILL.md) says what **If a client writes** actually covers. **Anything past what that page covers is logged as not covered, not answered from memory** — and the line the seats below already use is **"Not in the FAQ — flag for Michael before sending."** A draft that reasoned its way past the gate fails on that line however correct it sounds, and a reply written to a category template instead of to the entry on the page has skipped the gate altogether. Open the FAQ; do not check the draft against your memory of it.

**5. Cornelia Choe is Alumni, and her communication is support, not a pitch.** Her Thu 20 Aug 2026, 11:00–11:30am America/Denver meeting is Alumni support. A draft that reads as a pitch — a new deal, a price, an upsell, a "while we're talking" — fails this QC on that line. **Her two Zoom URLs stay unresolved: flag both, pick neither.** Choosing one is ask-Michael-first, and the choice travels up the wire rather than into the draft.

**6. If a price is in the thing, it comes off [offers.md](../offers.md), and both numbers travel where the ladder shows two.** Never recalled, never averaged, never the higher or the lower. **Platinum Major List has no public price** — say exactly that, rather than reaching for a nearby rung to imply one. A draft that resolved a "both" fails. **How an Uncovery is sold is named missing**, so nothing off this pass calls it paid discovery, paid diagnosis, a strategy session, or an audit.

**7. The track record is uncollapsed, and it is not a hit rate.** **124 (Dec 2022), 128 (Book Retreat era), later reported as 130–131** — it travels with those dates and that context, never as one current number, and it does not get divided by anything to become a rate. **No list placement, attempt, or outcome is recorded in this repo, and no page here records a guaranteed list placement**, so nothing off this pass implies one. That matters most on the rungs whose names sound like results: Bestseller Uncovery, SUCCESS, NYT, Platinum Major List.

**8. Asked for a health score, a churn tier, an expansion number, an NPS figure, a referral rate, or a bestseller hit rate? None is recorded. Say so, and stop there.** That is the whole answer, and each one has its own reason worth saying out loud rather than collapsing into a shrug:

- **A client's health, risk, or expansion potential.** **No such model exists here** — no score, no tier, no register. Inventing one is the same failure as inventing a lead score, and [campaign-lead-scoring](../../skills/campaign-lead-scoring/SKILL.md) is already a stop page for that reason: no model, no criterion, no weight, no threshold.
- **NPS and referral.** **No survey, score, or referral log is recorded.** There is no instrument here, so there is no figure behind either half of that KPI's name.
- **A bestseller hit rate.** Nothing is recorded to divide. The track record is the thing that gets reached for, and step 7 is why it is not that.
- **A figure built out of the numbers that are recorded.** The client count is a **different fact** and travels with its 18 Aug 2026 extract date. Sixty-seven Past clients over a track record is arithmetic, not measurement, and a modelled figure reads to the next person exactly like a measured one.

**9. Say pass, or name the miss in one plain sentence.** **One layer, one verdict.** No severity tiers, no partial pass, no "pass with notes." **Do not stack a stamp on a QC another seat already ran on its own half**, and **do not rewrite the work you are QC-ing** — the seat that produced it is closer to it than you are, and a miss handed back does not travel on a promise to clean it up later. Later is where a miss goes to be forgotten.

**10. Run the checklist honestly, then hand it on.** [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), every line, out loud, on anything this seat produced itself — that is what [last-gate-then-stop](../../skills/last-gate-then-stop/SKILL.md) is for. Name the unchecked line rather than softening it. Then the finished thing goes to the [PA](../seats/pa.md), whose gate is the one that ships it and who merges — and if what you produced is a Company OS or docs change, it goes to the [COO](../seats/coo.md) for first QC before that. **You do not last-gate and you do not merge**, and nothing reaches a client because your own check passed.

**11. Stop, and write the note below.** **Stay silent to Michael on a routine pass** — a pass produces no ping either way. An ask-Michael-first item travels up the wire through the PA; **you do not message Michael from this seat** ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)).

**Facts that come up on the way through** come from `handbook/` or from Michael's own word, and anything else is **"not recorded"**, said out loud in the same words this repo already uses. **Platformology LLC is one company**; **Promote a Book** and **Book Retreat** are **DBAs of it** — not two companies, and not SmarterVoice ([company-facts.md](../company-facts.md)). A client writing in about "the retreat" and a client writing in about "the campaign" are two DBAs of the same LLC, and a reply that answers as if they were two firms is a miss on that line.

## The note you leave

Where the work is — on the pull request, in the draft, or in the message back to the seat that produced the thing. Four lines, and nothing more:

- **Where things stand.** One sentence somebody who was not here can act on.
- **The one unit I took**, and where it ended up: one named thing carrying a pass or a named miss, handed to the PA, handed to the COO first as a docs change, handed back to the seat that produced it, or stopped on a named lock.
- **What is still open**, each with its actual next step. "Follow up" is not a next step.
- **What I could not answer**, in this repo's own words: not recorded.

Two things make that note worth reading. **Never invent a name, an owner, a date, a figure, or a link to make a line look finished** — an unknown left as an unknown is the useful answer, and a plausible filler reads to the next person as something you checked. And **never quietly drop an item the last note left open**: if it closed, say it closed; if it did not, carry it forward.

That second rule bites in a particular way on this seat, because the open items here are about people and they look settled after a while. **Hoffman being a question does not age into being a Current client. Two unpicked Zoom URLs do not age into one.** An absent campaign-QA SOP does not age into a present one, and a question the FAQ did not cover last week is still uncovered. Carry each one forward in the same words until somebody actually changes it.

No stamps, no status tags, no run identifiers. A person reads this.

## Stops that count as finished

A pass that stops honestly is a finished pass, not a failed one. Six of them:

- **A lock bit.** Quote it from [locks.md](../locks.md), in its own words — most often here, no send without Michael, the ask-first line on moving a client off "question," or the ask-first line on the two Zoom URLs.
- **A named hole was what you were asked for.** Quote it from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md), in the words that list already uses: no campaign-QA SOP, no NPS survey or referral log, no list placement or attempt, Hoffman's status, the two unresolved Zoom URLs.
- **The ask was for a figure nobody has.** A health score, a churn tier, an expansion number, an NPS figure, a referral rate, a bestseller hit rate. **None is recorded.** Saying so is the answer, and it is the whole answer — not the preamble to an estimate.
- **The thing sat in none of the three functions.** You named the seat that owns it and stopped. That is the pass working.
- **You could not open the FAQ.** No access, page moved, Notion down — say which page you could not open and stop. **Do not reconstruct the FAQ from this repo, from the desks, or from what a support page usually says**, and do not answer the client question anyway.
- **Nothing arrived from below.** An empty in-tray is a real answer, and going to find an account to have an opinion about is not.

If the block is an ask-Michael-first item, carrying it up the wire to the PA is part of the stop. Otherwise: name it, stop, and **do not invent a smaller version of the ask to stay busy.**
