## The note you leave

Where the work is — on the pull request, in the draft, or in the message back to the seat that asked. Four lines, and nothing more:

- **Where things stand.** One sentence somebody who was not here can act on.
- **The one unit I took**, and where it ended up: one named roster or classification question carrying the answer the roster actually records, handed to the PA, handed to the COO first as a docs change, handed back to People Ops, or stopped on a named lock.
- **What is still open**, each with its actual next step. "Follow up" is not a next step.
- **What I could not answer**, in this repo's own words: not recorded.

Two things make that note worth reading. **Never invent a name, a Role, an owner, a date, a figure, or a link to make a line look finished** — an unknown left as an unknown is the useful answer, and a plausible filler reads to the next person as something you checked. And **never quietly drop an item the last note left open**: if it closed, say it closed; if it did not, carry it forward.

That second rule bites in a particular way on this seat, because the open items here are blanks, and a blank looks settled after somebody has read past it a few times. **A blank Role does not age into a recorded one.** Jhana had no Role recorded last week and has none today; the same is true of Bob, and it will be true next week unless Michael says otherwise. **A seat that has been empty a long time does not become filled by habit**, and **a vendor does not become staff by tenure.** Carry each one forward in the same words until somebody actually changes it.

No stamps, no status tags, no run identifiers. A person reads this.

## Stops that count as finished

A pass that stops honestly is a finished pass, not a failed one. Six of them:

- **A lock bit.** Quote it from [locks.md](../locks.md), in its own words — most often here, the ask-first line on assigning a Role to Jhana or Bob, or no send without Michael.
- **A named hole was what you were asked for.** Quote it from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md), in the words that list already uses: Jhana's Role, Bob's Role, who fills each seat except COO, any hiring, onboarding, or classification SOP.
- **The ask was for a figure nobody has.** A headcount, a utilization, a ramp, an attrition rate, a time-to-hire, a filled-vs-charter ratio. **None is recorded.** Saying so is the answer, and it is the whole answer — not the preamble to an estimate.
- **The ask was a decision that is Michael's.** Give somebody a Role, move a vendor onto staff, add a person to the roster. Name it, carry it up the wire through the PA, and stop.
- **You could not open the Notion staff page.** No access, page moved, Notion down — say which page you could not open and stop. **Do not reconstruct the roster from the seat folder, from the desks, or from memory**, and do not answer the person question anyway.
- **Nothing arrived from below.** An empty in-tray is a real answer, and going to find a roster question to have an opinion about is not.

If the block is an ask-Michael-first item, carrying it up the wire to the PA is part of the stop. Otherwise: name it, stop, and **do not invent a smaller version of the ask to stay busy.**
