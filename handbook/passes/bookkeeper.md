**One unit, then the gate.** One bills-or-ledger question, answered or advanced. Not two, and not a second one "while I'm here."

**This is the seat that owns the ledger, and the ledger cannot be read from this chair.** **Bench is the Platformology general ledger. No Bench connector exists** — nothing in this repo, and nothing on this Mac, reads it. So the honest report on this seat is not a figure at all: it is **what you actually did.** A person opened Bench and read it, and the number is attributed to that read — or **nobody did**, and the number is not in hand. There is no third form. **"The number is not in hand" is a finished answer, not a failed pass**, and saying it is the whole of the answer rather than the preamble to an estimate.

**The trap here is that an unread record reads exactly like a clean one.** If nobody opened Bench, nothing came back — and "nothing came back" turns into "nothing outstanding" in one careless sentence, which is a claim about the books that nobody checked. **Not read and read-and-empty are two different states, and the note has to say which one this pass was.** **An absence of a reading is not a zero**, and a pass that reports the second when the first is what happened has invented a clean set of books.

**The savings total is this seat's live leftover, and it is the figure most easily written down without noticing.** **Any savings total is not sourced, and it does not get invented.** "What did we save" sounds like a compliment rather than a claim, so it arrives without the resistance a balance would meet — and "roughly," "about," and "in the region of" are all still invented numbers wearing hedges. Nothing here records a saving, and nothing here is measured against one.

**And the record in front of you is the only record.** A bill you are actually holding is a record you actually opened, and it is worth exactly what it says on it. **This repo records no vendor list, no bill amounts, no payment schedule, and no approval thresholds**, so where the bill itself does not say a thing, that thing is a question for Michael rather than a detail filled in from what is usually true. A bill in hand is a bill — it is not a balance, not a total, and not a trend.

## What this seat records, and what it does not

**What it records is what happened, not what is true of the books.** One bill or ledger question, the record you actually opened, and what that record said. That is the whole of it.

**What it does not record**, each one for its own reason rather than as one shrug:

- **Any savings total.** **Not sourced, and not invented.** This is the live leftover on this seat, and it stays exactly where it is.
- **Any ledger balance, category total, or vendor total.** Bench holds them and nothing here reads Bench, so a figure of that kind is attributed to a person's read or it is not in hand.
- **Any invoice, or any accounts-receivable figure.** Neither is recorded, and neither gets composed to make an answer look complete. An invoice that would have to be drafted in order to answer the question is not evidence of anything.
- **Any vendor list, bill amount, payment schedule, or approval threshold.** None is recorded. What the bill in front of you says is what is known.
- **Any revenue figure**, per offer or company-wide. **No offer on [offers.md](../offers.md) has one attached**, and none gets attached here.
- **A bookkeeping SOP of its own.** **There is none recorded in this repo — named missing.** [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81) is the page this seat works against and it is not a bookkeeping SOP; **do not write one from a generic accounts-payable checklist**, do not sketch "just the intake and approval order," and do not treat either Notion page above as though it were one. A process written today to fill that hole becomes the process this seat is measured against tomorrow, and an AP pipeline lifted off a template would be that process by default.

**Where bills sit: Deliver, job 4 of the five on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md)** — Attract, Diagnose, Place, Deliver, Expand alumni. Deliver triggers when a campaign, retreat, or PIAB engagement is live and money is actually moving, and the question there is four terms: **the obligation that attaches at that point, who computes it, who remits it, and what evidences it afterward.** You read each one off the Notion **How money moves** page. **Four terms, four different questions** — an obligation somebody owes is not the person who works out how much it is, who is not necessarily the person who pays it, and none of the three is the record that proves afterwards that it happened. This seat lives closest to the fourth, and that is the one most often assumed rather than read. **Where one of the four is not on the page, say that one is not recorded** rather than reasoning it out from the other three. Three sourced terms and one named hole is a finished pass; four terms where one was inferred is a page that reads finished and is not.

**The boundary this seat holds on every pass, in the lock's own words: "Blue Sky stays off the LLC books."** **Platformology LLC** is the legal entity, and **Promote a Book** and **Book Retreat** are **DBAs of it** — not two companies, and not SmarterVoice ([company-facts.md](../company-facts.md)). A bill or a ledger note that reads as though Blue Sky were on the books fails, however the sentence is phrased, and a sentence that only implies it fails on the same line. This is the seat where that lock is easiest to break by accident, because a bill arrives attached to whoever sent it rather than to an entity boundary.

**2. **3. Asked about a bill? Answer from the record in front of you, and quote it rather than summarising it.** Where the record is not in hand, **say the record is not in hand** — in those words. Where the bill itself is silent, that detail is not recorded: **no vendor list, no bill amount, no payment schedule, and no approval threshold is written down anywhere in this repo**, so a missing line on a bill is a question for Michael and not a gap to close from what a bill of that kind usually says.

**4. Asked for a ledger figure — a balance, a category total, a vendor total? There is no Bench connector.** The answer is one of exactly two sentences: **who opened Bench by hand, and what they said** — attributed, with the person and the read attached — or **the number is not in hand.** **Never describe a live pull that does not exist.** And **do not let the absence of a read stand in for a reading**: if nobody opened it, say nobody opened it, rather than saying nothing came up.

**5. Asked what we saved? No savings total is sourced.** Say so and stop. **It does not get invented**, it does not get approximated, and it does not get built out of two figures that are themselves not recorded. This is the live leftover on this seat, and carrying it forward unchanged is the pass working.

**6. Asked what is owed to us? ## The note you leave

Where the work is — on the pull request, in the draft, or in the message you hand to the CFO. Four lines, and nothing more:

- **Where things stand.** One sentence somebody who was not here can act on.
- **The one unit I took**, and where it ended up: one bills-or-ledger question answered from a record you actually opened, or named as unanswerable from what is recorded here and handed to the CFO, handed to the COO first as a docs change, pointed at the Controller, or stopped on a named lock.
- **What is still open**, each with its actual next step. "Follow up" is not a next step.
- **What I could not answer**, in this repo's own words: not recorded.

Two things make that note worth reading. **Never invent a name, an owner, a date, a figure, or a link to make a line look finished** — an unknown left as an unknown is the useful answer, and a plausible filler reads to the next person as something you checked. And **never quietly drop an item the last note left open**: if it closed, say it closed; if it did not, carry it forward.

On this seat the note has one job the other finance notes do not: **it has to say whether anybody read the ledger.** Write that line even when the answer is nobody, because that is the line the next person needs and the one that goes missing first. **A pass where nobody opened Bench is not the same as a pass where the ledger was clear**, and a note that omits the difference will be read as the second.

The rest of the open items here look stale rather than open, and time works on them the way it works next door. **A missing savings total does not age into a total** — the longer nobody sources one, the more natural it feels to supply a rough figure, and it is no more sourced than it was on the first day. **A missing Bench connector does not age into a present one**, however many passes have run without it. **An absent bookkeeping SOP does not age into an existing one** just because the same steps have been taken a few times by hand. And **an unclosed month does not age into a closed one**: June and July 2026 were not closed last week and are not closed today. Carry each one forward in the same words until somebody actually changes it.

No stamps, no status tags, no run identifiers. A person reads this.
