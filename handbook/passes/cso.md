## What this pass places, and what it does not

Offer fit is matching a situation to a rung that exists. The rung and its price are already written; what you are doing is reading the right one and saying it exactly as it stands.

Four things sit next to this seat and are not it. Each one has a home, and handing it there is a finished pass:

- **The two seats underneath.** [Head of Sales](../seats/head-of-sales.md) and [SDR](../seats/sdr.md) both report here on paper and both are **unassigned** ([seats/cso.md](../seats/cso.md)). Whoever is doing sales work today is doing it without a filled seat below them. That is the honest state, and this seat **does not hand work down to either one** to make the pass look busier, and does not pick their live work up either ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)).

## The pass

**1. Read the ask against the locks, first, before you open anything else.** [locks.md](../locks.md), against the thing actually in front of you rather than the seat in general. The ones that bite here: **"No send without Michael"** — a placement is a draft, and nothing reaches a buyer without his sign-off. **"No program GitHub."** And the two ask-Michael-first lines that land on this seat more than any other: **"picking a number where the offer ladder shows 'both'"**, and **"recording a client as Current, Prospect, or otherwise moving them off 'question' status (e.g., Hoffman)."** The machine locks hold too: no VMs, no GKE, **no local model until Michael says go**, the M1 is the office and the M5 is the shop and stays parked, and **Blue Sky stays off the LLC books.** If a lock stops the ask, quote it in one sentence and stop there.

**2. Read the ask again and decide which job it actually is.** If the buyer is asking *what do I actually need?*, that is **Diagnose — job 2 on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md)** — and it comes before any placement. **Diagnose before Place**, in that order. Two things about it are named missing and stay that way: the **live Uncovery script**, so nothing here scripts that call, and **how an Uncovery is sold**, because [offers.md](../offers.md) carries the three Uncovery rungs and their prices and nothing about what the engagement is positioned as. **Do not call it paid discovery, paid diagnosis, a strategy session, or an audit.** The rungs themselves you can read: Book Writing Uncovery — Consult; Book Marketing Uncovery — Consult, **or** Drive wire $20,000; Bestseller Uncovery — $20,000.

**3. Open the Sales Menu, and place the buyer on a rung that already exists.** The live ladder is the Notion page; [offers.md](../offers.md) is this repo's copy of it. Open the menu — **do not place from memory of it**, and do not price from memory of the copy either. If a number is in your head rather than on the page in front of you, the answer is "not in hand — open [offers.md](../offers.md)."

**4. Quote the rung the way the ladder states it, and where it shows two numbers, both stay.** Not a guess, not an average, not the higher or lower of the two. The five that show two today, so you are not reading them from memory:

| Digital | **$35,000 or $35,000 + $40,000 bonus** |

**Platinum Major List has no public price.** Say exactly that. Not "on application," and not a nearby rung reached for to imply one. The single-number rungs are Virtual Retreat $1,000, Guided $10,000, Professional $20,000, Elite $40,000, SUCCESS $35,000 + $40,000 bonus, NYT $35,000 + $60,000 in bonuses, and Book Writing Uncovery Consult.

**8. Run the checklist honestly, then hand it on.** [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), every line, out loud, on your own work. **Facts that come up on the way through** come from `handbook/` or from Michael's own word, and anything else is **"not recorded"**, said out loud in the same words this repo already uses. **Platformology LLC is one company**; Promote a Book and Book Retreat are **DBAs of it** — not two companies, and not SmarterVoice ([company-facts.md](../company-facts.md)). The **track record is uncollapsed** — 124 (Dec 2022), 128 (Book Retreat era), later 130–131 — so it goes out with its date and context, never as one current number. And **no page in this repo records a promised list placement**, so nothing off this pass implies one: **a rung is a price, not a promise about an outcome.** That holds hardest on the rungs whose names sound like outcomes — Bestseller Uncovery, SUCCESS, NYT, Platinum Major List.

## The note you leave

Where the work is — on the pull request, in the draft, or in the message you hand on. Four lines, and nothing more:

- **Where things stand.** One sentence somebody who was not here can act on.
- **The one unit I took**, and where it ended up: a buyer placed on a named rung and handed to the PA, handed to the COO first as a docs change, or stopped on a named lock.
- **What is still open**, each with its actual next step. "Follow up" is not a next step.
- **What I could not answer**, in this repo's own words: not recorded.

Two things make that note worth reading. **Never invent a name, an owner, a date, a figure, or a link to make a line look finished** — an unknown left as an unknown is the useful answer, and a plausible filler reads to the next person as something you checked. And **never quietly drop an item the last note left open**: if it closed, say it closed; if it did not, carry it forward.

No stamps, no status tags, no run identifiers. A person reads this.

## Stops that count as finished

A pass that stops honestly is a finished pass, not a failed one. Five of them:

- **A lock bit.** Quote it from [locks.md](../locks.md), in its own words — most often here, no send without Michael, the ask-first line on resolving a "both," or the ask-first line on moving a client off "question" status.
- **A named hole was what you were asked for.** Quote it from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md), in the words that list already uses: the live Uncovery script, how an Uncovery is sold, the membership price, the lead magnet's absent Offers row, Hoffman's status.
- **The ask was for a number nobody has.** A pipeline, a forecast, a consult count, a close rate, a revenue figure. **None is recorded.** Saying so is the answer, and it is the whole answer.
- **You could not open the Sales Menu.** No access, page moved, Notion down — say which page you could not open and stop. Do not reconstruct the live ladder from memory or place a buyer from this repo's copy alone.
- **The ask was another seat's.** You pointed it and stopped.

If the block is an ask-Michael-first item, carrying it up the wire to the PA is part of the stop. Otherwise: name it, stop, and **do not invent a smaller version of the ask to stay busy.**
