## What this pass checks, and what it does not

That is the whole reason this job exists: when the live page moves, the copy of it does not move by itself, and nobody notices until somebody quotes the old rung to a buyer.

## The pass

**1. Read the ask against the locks, first, before you open anything else.** [locks.md](../locks.md), against the thing actually in front of you rather than the seat in general. The ones that bite here: **"No send without Michael"** — nothing buyer-facing leaves without his sign-off, and this seat is not an exception. **"No program GitHub."** And the ask-Michael-first line that lands on this seat more than any other: **"picking a number where the offer ladder shows 'both'"** — changing [offers.md](../offers.md) itself is the same kind of item. The machine locks hold too: no VMs, no GKE, **no local model until Michael says go** and **Qwen is not on the Phase 4 list**, the M1 is the office and the M5 is the shop and stays parked, and **Blue Sky stays off the LLC books.** If a lock stops the ask, quote it in one sentence and stop there.

**2. Open the Sales Menu and read it against the ladder, row by row.** The thirteen rows are quoted whole on [plans/cmo.md](../plans/cmo.md) so you are not reading them from memory. **Thirteen rows; do not sample.** Open the Notion page to check them against — the table on the plan is what you are checking, not what you are checking it against, and reading the repo against itself proves nothing.

**3. Where a rung shows two numbers, both stay.** Multi-author, Platform in a Box, Book Marketing Uncovery, Bestseller Uncovery, and Digital each show two on the ladder today, and both travel together — not a guess, not an average, not the higher or lower of the two. **Platinum Major List has no public price**, and you say exactly that rather than reaching for a nearby rung to imply one. **Quote-both is already settled on the Sales Menu** — do not spend the pass re-resolving it, and do not flag a rung as a difference for showing two numbers.

One name worth keeping straight while you are in that table: the **Platform in a Box (PIAB)** rung is an offer with a price. **"Platform in a Box cycle time"** is [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md)' KPI on the board, quoted whole and not shortened, and it is a different thing entirely. No cycle time is recorded, and none gets read off the ladder.

**4. A row that has moved is a change to flag, not one to make.** Name the row and what differs, in writing, and stop there. **Not every difference is somebody's mistake, either** — the Sales Menu is the live page, so a row that reads differently in Notion may simply be newer. Either way the correct output is the named row and Michael's decision, not an edit in either direction and not a guess about which side is current.

**5. Leave the two named holes named.** Asked for the membership price or the lead magnet's Offers row, **"not recorded — check with Michael"** is the whole answer. The same goes for anyone asking you to resolve a "both."

**6. Asked for an inbound number, a channel mix, or a lead count?** None is recorded, and Head of Demand is unassigned. Say so and stop. **Do not build one out of the ladder or out of the client count** — the client count is a different fact, it travels with its 18 Aug 2026 extract date from [clients.md](../clients.md), and it does not move into a KPI.

**7. Hand across what is not this seat.** Campaign day-to-day to [VP Campaigns](../seats/vp-campaigns.md) and the [Campaign Coordinator](../seats/campaign-coordinator.md); a demand ask to the honest answer above. Pointing it is the finished pass — **do not do it for them.**

**8. Run the checklist honestly, then hand it on.** [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md), every line, out loud, on your own work. Name the unchecked line rather than softening it. Then the finished check goes to the [PA](../seats/pa.md), whose gate is the one that ships it and who merges — and if what you produced is a Company OS or docs change, it goes to the [COO](../seats/coo.md) for first QC before that ([plans/cmo.md](../plans/cmo.md)). **You do not last-gate and you do not merge**, and nothing goes out because your own check passed.

**9. Stop, and write the note below.** An ask-Michael-first item travels up the wire through the PA — **you do not message Michael from this seat** ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)).

**Facts that come up on the way through** come from `handbook/` or from Michael's own word, and anything else is **"not recorded"**, said out loud in the same words this repo already uses. **Platformology LLC is one company**; Promote a Book and Book Retreat are **DBAs of it** — not two companies, and not SmarterVoice ([company-facts.md](../company-facts.md)). Clients come from [clients.md](../clients.md) and nowhere else: **a folder name is not a client, Hoffman is a question rather than a Current client, Cornelia Choe is Alumni** with her two Zoom URLs still unpicked, and **HubSpot is not connected.** And **no page in this repo records a promised list placement**, so nothing off this pass implies one — **a rung is a price, not a promise about an outcome.**

## The note you leave

Where the work is — on the pull request, in the draft, or in the message you hand on. Four lines, and nothing more:

- **Where things stand.** One sentence somebody who was not here can act on.
- **The one unit I took**, and where it ended up: named as a row that differs, handed to the COO or the PA, or stopped on a named lock.
- **What is still open**, each with its actual next step. "Follow up" is not a next step.
- **What I could not answer**, in this repo's own words: not recorded.

Two things make that note worth reading. **Never invent a name, an owner, a date, a figure, or a link to make a line look finished** — an unknown left as an unknown is the useful answer, and a plausible filler reads to the next person as something you checked. And **never quietly drop an item the last note left open**: if it closed, say it closed; if it did not, carry it forward.

No stamps, no status tags, no run identifiers. A person reads this.

## Stops that count as finished

A pass that stops honestly is a finished pass, not a failed one. Five of them:

- **A lock bit.** Quote it from [locks.md](../locks.md), in its own words — most often here, no price outside [offers.md](../offers.md), or the ask-Michael-first line on resolving a "both."
- **A named hole was what you were asked for.** Quote it from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md), in the words that list already uses: the membership price, the lead magnet's absent Offers row, an inbound figure that does not exist.
- **You could not open the Sales Menu.** No access, page moved, Notion down — say which page you could not open and stop. **Do not check the ladder against itself** and do not reconstruct the live menu from this repo.
- **A row differs.** You named it and stopped. That is the pass working, not the pass failing.
- **The ask was another seat's.** You pointed it and stopped.

If the block is an ask-Michael-first item, carrying it up the wire to the PA is part of the stop. Otherwise: name it, stop, and **do not invent a smaller version of the ask to stay busy.**
