## What this seat records, and what it does not

**What this pass produces is one retreat step, run off a named part of the Retreat SOP, with every unsourced detail written down as unsourced rather than filled.** That second clause is the job, not the caveat on it. The hard part of this seat is what it refuses to fill in, and the refusals all fail the same way: a plausible value is easier to write than an absence, and afterwards it reads exactly like a sourced one.

What it does **not** produce, each for its own reason rather than as one shrug:

- **A retreat date, a guest list, or a venue.** **None of the three is sourced.** See below.
- **An answer to an ownership question.** That is [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md)'. Hand it up.
- **A sixth field on the guest intake, or a blank one filled.** See below.
- **A roster.** See below; the KPI is empty for want of a retreat, and drafting one is how the field stops being empty without anybody sourcing a thing.
- **Retreat-experience QC on your own work.** That is the [CCO](../seats/cco.md)'s, in parallel. Leave it there.
- **A price you worked out.** Prices come off [offers.md](../offers.md) and nowhere else.
- **A single, current track-record number.** See below.

**A "live retreat" needs a sourced client behind it.** The **18 Aug 2026 extract records 0 Current clients** ([clients.md](../clients.md)) — 1 Alumni (Cornelia Choe, The Leaders Alliance), 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect — and that count travels with its date. So an ask that opens "on the retreat we're running" is an ask about something this repo does not record, and **saying so, with the date attached, is a finished pass** rather than the preamble to producing one anyway. **A folder name is not a client. Hoffman is a question, not a Current client. HubSpot is closed**, and **Notion Clients is the CRM**; recording anybody as Current or Prospect is ask-Michael-first ([locks.md](../locks.md)). **Cornelia Choe is Alumni, and her communication is support, not a pitch** — she is not a guest to seat at a retreat.

**Honestly empty is a finished answer** ([runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md)). Nothing arrived, or no live retreat is recorded, is a real pass — said plainly, with where you looked named. **Do not invent a retreat to have an output.**

### No date, no guest list, no venue — and no plausible stand-in

**A retreat date, a guest list, and a venue are not sourced in this repo.** Check the [Retreat SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8185b9a9f19dd56389e5) and the live guest records in Notion; **if it is not there, it is not known**, and that sentence is the answer rather than the problem to solve.

**None of the three gets a plausible stand-in** — not as a placeholder, not as an example, and not as a "typical" shape. A "say a Thursday" or a "somewhere like the usual room" written down to keep a draft moving is indistinguishable a week later from a date and a room somebody agreed, and the next person will produce against it.

**"Not sourced" written down where the next seat will look is a finished pass.** A plausible stand-in is not.

### Guest details: five fields, no sixth

**Dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, and incident stay named missing until a guest's own completed intake says otherwise.** [blank-guest-intake](../../skills/blank-guest-intake/SKILL.md) is the skill and the [draft](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b812486c8eac90777658c) is the page — **open it rather than restating it from here.**

Two ways that goes wrong, and both look like helpfulness while you are doing them:

- **Answering from a typical guest.** Asked what a guest needs, and it is not on their intake: **say so.** What guests like this one usually need is not a record of what this one said.
- **Filling a blank from a comparable retreat's form.** A value carried across reads afterwards exactly like one the guest gave you, and nobody downstream can tell which it was.

**The starter form stays a starter form: five fields, and no sixth gets added here.** That was already settled on [plans/vp-retreats.md](../plans/vp-retreats.md) — **guest intake YES, the starter form stays** — so it does not get re-argued on this pass. **A pass that finds the form thin has found the form as agreed**, and **a sixth field is not missing, it is not wanted.** Asked to add one? **Say it is not on the form yet and flag it to [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md) or Michael.** That is a finished pass.

### The track record travels uncollapsed

This seat owns quoting the **Book Retreat-era** numbers ([company-facts.md](../company-facts.md)), and they go out the way that page records them: **124 (Dec 2022), 128 (Book Retreat era), later reported as 130–131.** **Each figure with its date or context. Never averaged, never rounded, and not one of them presented as "current"** — including 130–131, which is the newest and still not current. Two readings that get made here and neither is true. **Those figures are not a retreat result** — they are not what a retreat this repo does not record produced, and quoting them next to a retreat does not make them one. And **they are not a list placement.** **No list guarantee:** no page in this repo records a promised placement, and **a rung on [offers.md](../offers.md) is a price, not a promise about an outcome.**

## The note you leave

Where the work is — on the pull request, or in the message you hand to VP Retreats. Four lines, and nothing more:

- **Where things stand.** One sentence somebody who was not here can act on.
- **The one unit I took**, and where it ended up: one named step produced off a named part of the Retreat SOP, or a stop on a named lock.
- **What is still open**, each with its actual next step. "Follow up" is not a next step.
- **What I could not answer**, in this repo's own words: not recorded.

Two things make that note worth reading. **Never invent a name, an owner, a date, a figure, or a link to make a line look finished** — an unknown left as an unknown is the useful answer, and a plausible filler reads to the next person as something you checked. And **never quietly drop an item the last note left open**: if it closed, say it closed; if it did not, carry it forward.

That second rule bites in a particular way here, because the open items on this seat are absences that time makes feel resolved. **An absent retreat date does not age into a date**, however many passes have run without one. **A blank intake field does not age into a filled one**, however many guests have since been through something like it. **A seat with nobody recorded in it does not become filled by habit.** **Hoffman being a question does not age into being a Current client**, and **0 Current clients on an extract does not age into a live retreat** because time has passed since the extract. Carry each one forward in the same words until somebody actually changes it.

Two things have to be in the note explicitly, because the whole pass is a produced thing plus a list of blanks and the blanks are the half that goes missing. **Say which part of the Retreat SOP you produced off** — a note reading "handled the retreat" reads like a note where a step ran, and the next person cannot go and read the same lines. And **write down every detail you did not have**, by name: the date, the guest list, the venue, the intake fields. A note that is silent about them reads like a note where nothing was missing.

No stamps, no status tags, no run identifiers. A person reads this.

## Stops that count as finished

A pass that stops honestly is a finished pass, not a failed one. Six of them:

- **A lock bit.** Quote it from [locks.md](../locks.md), in its own words — most often here, no send without Michael, the ask-first line on resolving a "both," or the ask-first line on moving a client off "question."
- **You could not open the SOP, or the intake draft.** No access, page moved, Notion down — say which page you could not open and stop. **Do not reconstruct the SOP from this repo, from the desks, or from what a retreat process usually looks like**, and do not produce the step anyway. This repo has never held either page's contents, so there is nothing here to fall back on and nothing that looks like a fallback. **Not being able to check is itself the finding**, and it gets written down rather than worked around.
- **A named hole was what the step needed.** Quote it from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md), in the words that list already uses: **a retreat date, guest list, or venue for any specific Book Retreat**, the **guest intake draft's dietary, rooming, arrival, AV, and incident fields**, how an Uncovery is sold, Platinum Major List's absent public price, Hoffman's status.
- **The step needed a figure, a status, or a send that is not yours.** A "both" on the ladder resolved, a date set, a field added, a roster written, a client recorded as Current, a message actually sent. Naming it and carrying it up the wire is the finished pass.
- **There is no live retreat.** **0 Current clients on the 18 Aug 2026 extract.** Saying so, with the date attached, is the whole answer — not the preamble to producing one.
- **The ask was another seat's, or nothing arrived.** An ownership question is [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md)'; retreat-experience QC is the [CCO](../seats/cco.md)'s; a campaign is [VP Campaigns](../seats/vp-campaigns.md)' and its day-to-day the [Campaign Coordinator](../seats/campaign-coordinator.md)'s; positioning and the public ladder are the [CMO](../seats/cmo.md)'s; placing a buyer on a rung is the [CSO](../seats/cso.md)'s. Whichever it is, the message to [VP Retreats](../seats/vp-retreats.md) says one of exactly two things ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)): **the lock**, quoted from [locks.md](../locks.md), or **the named-missing hole**, quoted from [SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md) in that list's own words. Then stop. **Do not ping Michael, and do not route around the lock.** A block is not permission to invent a retreat, a guest, or a venue to hang a smaller version of the step on, and the response to a blocked ask does not get worked out from what a normal pass would have produced.
