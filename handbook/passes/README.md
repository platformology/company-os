**Written: 21 Aug 2026**

Where [plans/](../plans/README.md) is the spec for one pass — what to open, the one job, the SOP, the KPI — a page here is somebody running it: what you read at each stop, what you write down, and the note you leave so the next person can pick up cold.

The assigned seats with a job to run come first, in the order they sit in on [plans/README.md](../plans/README.md): PA is the first of them, COO is the second, CMO is the third, CSO is the fourth, CFO is the fifth, CCO is the sixth, CHRO is the seventh, Controller is the eighth, Bookkeeper is the ninth, People Ops is the tenth, Head of Content is the eleventh, Head of Author Success is the twelfth. The rest have no pass page yet. That is a gap in this folder and nothing else — a missing row here does not mean a seat has no job, no plan, or no router. Which seats are assigned with a job to run is the seventeen-row table on that page, and this page does not copy it.

**The PA and COO rows are one docs change seen from both ends, and they run in this order.** The COO is first QC on a Company OS or docs change; the PA runs the last gate after that and merges. First is not only, and the COO does not last-gate or merge. The layers themselves are on [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md), and neither page here restates them.

**The CMO row is a producing seat on that same wire.** What it finishes goes to the COO for first QC if it is a docs change, then to the PA's gate — so the third page is the front of the line those two already describe, not a third layer of sign-off. It does not last-gate or merge either.

**The CSO row is the same wire again, and it is the seat that quotes prices.** What it produces is a buyer placed on a rung that already exists, priced exactly as [offers.md](../offers.md) states it — and then handed on, because **no send happens without Michael** ([locks.md](../locks.md)). It does not last-gate or merge. **CSO is Chief Sales Officer, not Chief Strategy Officer**, and [Head of Sales](../seats/head-of-sales.md) and [SDR](../seats/sdr.md) report into it on paper and stay unassigned — a pass page for the CSO hands neither of them work.

**The CFO row is the first one two other seats report into, and the first where the answer's natural shape is a number.** The [Controller](../seats/controller.md) owns the monthly close and the [Bookkeeper](../seats/bookkeeper.md) owns bills and the general ledger; that pass checks each of those functions and nothing wider, and it neither redoes their work nor puts a second mark on it ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)). What it produces is one point where money moves, described in four terms off the Notion **How money moves** page — and no figure, because **no revenue, cost, margin, cash, or accounts-receivable figure is recorded in this repo**, and **a price on [offers.md](../offers.md) is not revenue.** It does not last-gate or merge either. **Blue Sky stays off the LLC books** is the lock that seat holds on every pass.

**The CCO row is the customer side of that same wire, and it is a QC seat with three functions and no SOP of its own.** **CCO is Chief Customer Officer** — Anthony C. Garcia's recorded title, COO / Chief Persona Architect, is a **different seat**. What that pass checks is author success, campaign QA, or retreat experience, and **a thing that sits in none of the three is not that seat's QC** — naming the seat that owns it is the finished answer. Work arrives from the [Head of Author Success](../seats/head-of-author-success.md), and from the [Campaign Coordinator](../seats/campaign-coordinator.md) for **campaign QA only**: that seat has **two owners on purpose**, the **Campaign SOP half belongs to [VP Campaigns](../seats/vp-campaigns.md)**, and neither owner signs off on the other's half ([runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md)). **There is no campaign-QA SOP recorded** — named missing, and that pass does not draft one from a template. It invents no health score, churn tier, NPS figure, referral rate, or bestseller hit rate, because **none is recorded**; the **track record stays uncollapsed** — 124, 128, 130–131 — and is not a hit rate. It does not last-gate or merge either, and **nothing client-facing leaves without Michael** ([locks.md](../locks.md)).

**The CHRO row is the one where the answer is most often a blank, and the blank is the whole answer.** That pass answers one question off the recorded roster in [people.md](../people.md) — who works here, how somebody is classified, or who sits in a seat — and **Jhana and Bob have no Role recorded**, which is said in those words and then stopped. **Giving either of them a Role is ask-Michael-first, every time** ([locks.md](../locks.md)). **A seat page existing is not a person**: every seat except COO reads "not recorded," and twenty-three seat pages do not make twenty-three staff. [People Ops](../seats/people-ops.md) owns day-to-day roster mechanics and records what is already decided, while **classification questions come back up to this seat**. **There is no hiring, onboarding, or classification SOP recorded** — named missing, and that pass does not draft one. It does not size the team, plan headcount, or model capacity — **three people and two vendors is not a distribution** — and it puts no figure in **Seats filled vs charter**, because **a 0 is a figure and a ratio is a figure twice over**. It does not last-gate or merge either.

**The Controller row is the first one that reports into another seat on this list, and the one where the answer is a status rather than a figure or a blank.** That pass answers or advances **one monthly-close question**, carrying forward that **June and July 2026 are not closed** — **a fact to report, not a problem to solve with an estimate.** The closed status of those two months beyond those words is named missing, **no closing date and no variance is recorded**, and **a projected date is a guess wearing a number** — which is why that page refuses "substantially complete" as firmly as it refuses a date. **There is no monthly-close SOP recorded** — named missing, and that pass does not write one from a generic close checklist; the Notion **How money moves** page is its one hand, and **Bench is denied because no connector exists**, so a ledger figure is either attributed to somebody who opened Bench by hand or **not in hand**. The close sits inside **Deliver**, job 4 of the five on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md), named in the same four terms the CFO's pass uses, with any term that page does not carry left as not recorded. **Bills, Bench, and savings are the [Bookkeeper](../seats/bookkeeper.md)'s** — a sibling beside it under the CFO, and pointing such a question there is the finished pass. Its KPI is **Clean close by day 10** and **Current stays empty**: no close is recorded for any month, so **"by day 10" has nothing to be on time against**, the 10 in the name is a target somebody named rather than a result somebody measured, and the **0 Current client count is a different fact** that does not get borrowed to fill the field. **Blue Sky stays off the LLC books** is the boundary it holds on every pass. **The finished thing goes to the CFO, and the PA last-gates after that** — this pass does not last-gate and does not merge.

**The Bookkeeper row is the Controller's sibling under the CFO, and the one where the honest answer is a report of what a person actually did.** That pass answers or advances **one bills-or-ledger question**, and **"the number is not in hand" is a finished answer** rather than a failed pass. **Bench is the Platformology general ledger and no connector exists**, so nothing here pulls from it: the report is that **a person opened Bench and read it, or nobody did**, and **never a live pull that does not exist.** That distinction is the whole of the page, because an unread ledger reads exactly like a clean one and **an absence of a reading is not a zero.** **Any savings total is not sourced and does not get invented** — the live leftover on that seat — and no invoice, accounts-receivable figure, vendor list, bill amount, payment schedule, or approval threshold is recorded either, so the bill in front of you is the only record. **There is no bookkeeping SOP recorded** — named missing, and that pass does not write one from a generic accounts-payable checklist; the Notion **How money moves** page is its one hand. Bills sit inside **Deliver**, job 4 of the five on [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md), named in the same four terms the CFO's and Controller's passes use, with any term that page does not carry left as not recorded. **The monthly close is the [Controller](../seats/controller.md)'s** — pointing it there is the finished pass, **June and July 2026 are not closed**, and no bill gets described as posted into a closed month. Its KPI is **Bills and collections** and **Current stays empty**: not an amount outstanding, **not a count of open bills**, not a 0, and **the 0 Current client count is a different fact** that does not get borrowed to fill it. A price off [offers.md](../offers.md) travels with both numbers where the ladder shows two, and **a price is not revenue and not a receivable** — no offer on that page has a revenue figure attached. **Blue Sky stays off the LLC books** is the boundary it holds on every pass. **The finished thing goes to the CFO, and the PA last-gates after that** — this pass does not last-gate and does not merge.

**The People Ops row is the CHRO's report, and the one where the honest answer is a blank that has to be said out loud rather than left off the page.** That pass answers **one roster question** exactly as [people.md](../people.md) records it: who is staff, who is a vendor, and which Roles are genuinely blank. **Jhana and Bob have no Role recorded**, and **assigning either one is ask-Michael-first, every time** ([locks.md](../locks.md)) — never inferred from a task either of them did. **Staff-or-vendor and Role are two different things written down**, and neither is worked out from the other: **Dave / Lehi Drew is a vendor for Platformology and David McInnis is a vendor for Cranberry Press**, what he does stays a dash, and **a vendor doing regular ongoing work does not become staff by doing more of it.** **No rate, term, date, or scope is recorded for either vendor**, and adding one is the same failure as promoting one to staff. **Editing the roster is denied on that seat** — a change is **a draft for Michael, never typed in.** **Classification judgements go up to the [CHRO](../seats/chro.md), and this seat records what is already decided**, with day-to-day mechanics staying here. Asked who sits in a seat, **every seat except COO reads "not recorded"** — a seat page existing is not a person. **There is no onboarding SOP recorded** — named missing, and that pass does not draft one, sketch a first week, or write a 30-60-90. Its KPI is **Time-to-onboard a campaign coordinator** and **Current stays empty**: the [Campaign Coordinator](../seats/campaign-coordinator.md) seat has **no recorded holder** and there is **no onboarding SOP**, so there is nothing measured and nothing to measure it with, and **no onboarding has happened, so there is no elapsed time to read** — not a target number of days, not an average, not a 0. **A named KPI with no recorded process behind it is a named KPI, not a licence to draft the process.** **This repo has a URL only for Grok Bot staff**, and no second **Team** URL gets invented beside it. **The finished thing goes to the CHRO, and the PA last-gates after that** — this pass does not last-gate and does not merge.

**The Head of Content row is the [CMO](../seats/cmo.md)'s report, and the one seat whose whole discipline is refusing to write the thing it is best placed to write.** That pass takes **one field on the Competitive Writing Guide draft** and either answers it from **a sourced extract Michael provides, with the source kept next to the words**, or writes **"not sourced"** and stops — and **"not sourced" in the field is itself the finished pass**, not a shortfall in one. **"Competitive" names the reader, not a rival company:** it is a buying style, so **no competitor teardown gets built and no rival, feature, price, or claim gets invented to fill a field.** **The five Methodical fields are already sourced** — Characteristics, Buying needs, Likes and dislikes, Use, Avoid — and **that list is settled**: not reworded, not reordered, and **never a sixth.** **One style per page**, so no Spontaneous or Humanistic copy comes off that draft, and **training names in the old persona decks are not clients.** The wider job is librarian of the **27-year body of personas, proposals, white papers, ebooks, and Pendulum material**: **this seat catalogues what exists and does not write new material to fill a hole in the catalogue** — **writing new copy and filing it as corpus is denied on its hands row**, and a corpus question is answered by **pointing at the file or saying it is not in hand**, never by composing a stand-in and shelving it. **The guide is a draft, not a live campaign asset**, unpublished until Michael says otherwise. Its KPI is **Content output** and **Current stays empty**: **no output record of any kind is recorded** — no piece count, no catalogue inventory, no publication log — so not a count of pieces, not a cadence, not a 0, and **not a tally of Google Drive files**, because **a librarian's pass is not measured by how much new copy it produced.** One word covers two things there and the page keeps them apart: **Google Drive is a tool, while "Drive" on the offer ladder is how a price gets paid.** **The finished thing goes to the CMO, and the PA last-gates after that** — this pass does not last-gate and does not merge.

**The Head of Author Success row is the [CCO](../seats/cco.md)'s report, and the one where the job is to say how something is going without a single instrument to say it with.** That pass takes **one author's engagement** and either **states how it is going from recorded facts, or names it honestly as unrecorded** — then **holds the FAQ gate on anything drafted back to that author.** **Author outcomes are checked against [clients.md](../clients.md)** and nowhere else: **a folder name is not a client**, a filename and a calendar entry are not statuses either, and **HubSpot is closed** while **Notion Clients is the CRM.** **Silence is not a status** on that page — an author nobody has heard from has reported nothing, in either direction — and **the absence of a complaint is not a reading.** The recorded picture travels with its date: **1 Alumni (Cornelia Choe, The Leaders Alliance), 67 Past, 0 Current, 0 Prospect — 18 Aug 2026 extract**, and **Hoffman is a question rather than a Current client**, with **recording anyone as Current ask-Michael-first** ([locks.md](../locks.md)). **[If a client writes](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ada7b7cbf1d50b0d9f) is the only source anything drafted from that seat, or from [Client Success](../seats/client-success.md) underneath it, can come from**, and the **[Buyer data room](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8135bdcdc9010e649855) is what a diligence reader already sees** — those two, and no third URL. Where the page runs out the line is **"Not in the FAQ — flag for Michael before sending"**: **you still draft, you do not answer past the gate**, and **"Not in the FAQ" is a finished answer once it is written down** rather than the preamble to working one out — the gap gets logged for whoever owns that page, because **noticing it and closing it are two different jobs.** **There is no author-success SOP recorded** — named missing, and that pass does not draft one from a template; the FAQ is a gate on client-facing words, not a procedure for running an engagement. It writes **no health score, satisfaction score, or risk tier**, because **none is recorded and no such model exists here**, and **no page in this repo records a promised list placement.** Its KPI is **On-time author milestones** and **Current stays empty**: **no milestone schedule for any author is recorded**, so "on-time" has nothing to be on time against, and with **0 Current clients on that extract there is no live engagement to read a milestone off** — not a percentage, not a count of milestones hit, not a 0, and **the 0 Current client count is a different fact** that does not get carried into the field. **Cornelia Choe is Alumni and her communication is support, not a pitch** — her Thu 20 Aug 2026, 11:00–11:30am America/Denver meeting is Alumni support, and **her two Zoom URLs stay unresolved: both flagged, neither picked.** **[Client Success](../seats/client-success.md) reports into it and hands its drafts there**, and that pass checks **author outcomes and the FAQ gate and nothing wider** — **the day-to-day message traffic stays that seat's**, and taking it means nobody ran this one. **The finished thing goes to the CCO, and the PA last-gates after that** — this pass does not last-gate and does not merge, and **nothing reaches an author without Michael** ([locks.md](../locks.md)).

## What this folder is not

- **Not another seat index.** [seats/](../seats/README.md) is what a seat owns, [bodies/](../bodies/README.md) is the seat as one runnable file, [desks/](../desks/README.md) is the sit-down pack, [plans/](../plans/README.md) is the pass spec. A page here points at those; it does not restate them, and when it disagrees with the seat page, **the seat page wins**.
- **Not a tracker, a board, or a second place to look for work.** See [runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md).
- **Not evidence that a seat is staffed.** A pass page existing does not fill a seat, unpark one, or lift a lock. **Head of Demand, Head of Sales, and SDR stay unassigned**, and a pass page for one of them later would not change that. The COO page names a holder because [people.md](../people.md) already recorded one — Anthony C. Garcia, COO / Chief Persona Architect — and it was recorded before this folder existed. [pa.md](pa.md), [cmo.md](cmo.md), [cso.md](cso.md), [cfo.md](cfo.md), [cco.md](cco.md), [chro.md](chro.md), [controller.md](controller.md), [bookkeeper.md](bookkeeper.md), and [head-of-content.md](head-of-content.md) all read **Holder: not recorded** for the same reason: the page reports what the roster says, in either direction. **Head of Demand reports into the CMO on paper, and Head of Sales and SDR report into the CSO on paper. All three stay unassigned** — a pass page for the seat above hands them no work, no holder, and no pipeline. **The Controller and Bookkeeper report into the CFO on paper and have no holder recorded either**, and the CFO pass does neither seat's job on their behalf — both now have a pass page of their own, and **each reads Holder: not recorded**, because a page existing is not a person. **The two are siblings, not a line**, and neither pass does the other's job: bills, Bench, and savings get pointed at the Bookkeeper, and the monthly close gets pointed at the Controller. **The Head of Author Success and the Campaign Coordinator report into the CCO on paper and have no holder recorded either**, and that pass checks the function each one sits inside without doing, redoing, or restamping their work. **[People Ops](../seats/people-ops.md) reports into the CHRO on paper and has no holder recorded either** — and that page is the one that says plainly why a folder full of seat pages is not a staff list. That seat now has a pass page of its own and it reads **Holder: not recorded** too, which is the same point made from underneath: the seat that keeps the roster honest is itself a seat with nobody recorded in it. **[Head of Content](../seats/head-of-content.md) reports into the [CMO](../seats/cmo.md) on paper and has no holder recorded either** — and neither does the CMO seat it hands to, so that page names both blanks rather than reading the one recorded holder onto either. It does not read a second COO out of the **Persona Architect** leftover, and a pass page for it staffs nothing: the [Head of Demand](../seats/head-of-demand.md) sitting beside it under the same owner **stays unassigned**, and so do [Head of Sales](../seats/head-of-sales.md) and [SDR](../seats/sdr.md). **[Head of Author Success](../seats/head-of-author-success.md) reports into the [CCO](../seats/cco.md) on paper and has no holder recorded either** — and neither does the CCO seat it hands to, so that page names both blanks rather than reading the one recorded holder onto either, and it does not read a second COO out of the **Persona Architect** leftover. It is the second page in this folder written from underneath the CCO's three functions, and it owns only the first of them: **author outcomes and the FAQ gate**, with campaign QA and retreat experience staying above it. **[Client Success](../seats/client-success.md) reports into it on paper and has no holder recorded either**, and that pass checks the function that seat's drafts sit inside without doing, redoing, or restamping its day-to-day work.
- **Not the place the leftover-file seats get indexed.** Those seats stay UNASSIGNED, and their index rows stay unwritten here for the same reason they are unwritten on [plans/README.md](../plans/README.md) — that is Michael's call, not this folder's.

## Run one

1. Read [locks.md](../locks.md).
2. Open your seat's plan, then the pass page beside it, and follow it in order.
3. Stop where it says stop, and run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) — or, where the last gate is not your seat's step, read the lines the work will have to pass anyway and hand it on.
4. Hand anything external to Michael. **Nothing sends.**

One pass, one gate, one stop. [cheap-loop.md](../cheap-loop.md) already says why, and no page here restates it.

Named gaps stay named. Where a page here says a thing is not recorded — who fills the PA seat, the COO's accountabilities beyond the public title, the membership price and the lead magnet's absent Offers row on the CMO's, the live Uncovery script and how an Uncovery is sold on the CSO's, the missing Bench connector and the unclosed June and July 2026 on the CFO's, the absent campaign-QA SOP and the missing NPS survey and referral log on the CCO's, Jhana's and Bob's Roles and the absent hiring, onboarding, and classification SOP on the CHRO's, the absent monthly-close SOP and the missing closing date, variance, and day-count on the Controller's, the absent bookkeeping SOP and the unsourced savings total on the Bookkeeper's, the absent onboarding SOP and the missing vendor rate, term, and dates on the People Ops one, the unsourced Competitive answers and the absent content-output record on the Head of Content's, the absent author-success SOP and the missing author milestone schedule on the Head of Author Success's — that is the answer until Michael sources one.
