# handbook/passes/ — one pass at a time

**Written: 21 Aug 2026**

This folder holds one thing per file: a seat's pass, walked through by a person. Where [plans/](../plans/README.md) is the spec for one pass — what to open, the one job, the SOP, the KPI — a page here is somebody running it: what you read at each stop, what you write down, and the note you leave so the next person can pick up cold.

**This slice is PA, COO, CMO, and CSO.**

The assigned seats with a job to run come first, in the order they sit in on [plans/README.md](../plans/README.md): PA is the first of them, COO is the second, CMO is the third, CSO is the fourth. The rest have no pass page yet. That is a gap in this folder and nothing else — a missing row here does not mean a seat has no job, no plan, or no router. Which seats are assigned with a job to run is the seventeen-row table on that page, and this page does not copy it.

**The PA and COO rows are one docs change seen from both ends, and they run in this order.** The COO is first QC on a Company OS or docs change; the PA runs the last gate after that and merges. First is not only, and the COO does not last-gate or merge. The layers themselves are on [runtime-wiring.md](../runtime-wiring.md), and neither page here restates them.

**The CMO row is a producing seat on that same wire.** What it finishes goes to the COO for first QC if it is a docs change, then to the PA's gate — so the third page is the front of the line those two already describe, not a third layer of sign-off. It does not last-gate or merge either.

**The CSO row is the same wire again, and it is the seat that quotes prices.** What it produces is a buyer placed on a rung that already exists, priced exactly as [offers.md](../offers.md) states it — and then handed on, because **no send happens without Michael** ([locks.md](../locks.md)). It does not last-gate or merge. **CSO is Chief Sales Officer, not Chief Strategy Officer**, and [Head of Sales](../seats/head-of-sales.md) and [SDR](../seats/sdr.md) report into it on paper and stay unassigned — a pass page for the CSO hands neither of them work.

## What this folder is not

- **Not another seat index.** [seats/](../seats/README.md) is what a seat owns, [bodies/](../bodies/README.md) is the seat as one runnable file, [desks/](../desks/README.md) is the sit-down pack, [plans/](../plans/README.md) is the pass spec. A page here points at those; it does not restate them, and when it disagrees with the seat page, **the seat page wins**.
- **Not a tracker, a board, or a second place to look for work.** See [runtime-guts.md](../runtime-guts.md).
- **Not evidence that a seat is staffed.** A pass page existing does not fill a seat, unpark one, or lift a lock. **Head of Demand, Head of Sales, and SDR stay unassigned**, and a pass page for one of them later would not change that. The COO page names a holder because [people.md](../people.md) already recorded one — Anthony C. Garcia, COO / Chief Persona Architect — and it was recorded before this folder existed. [pa.md](pa.md), [cmo.md](cmo.md), and [cso.md](cso.md) all read **Holder: not recorded** for the same reason: the page reports what the roster says, in either direction. **Head of Demand reports into the CMO on paper, and Head of Sales and SDR report into the CSO on paper. All three stay unassigned** — a pass page for the seat above hands them no work, no holder, and no pipeline.
- **Not the place the leftover-file seats get indexed.** Those seats stay UNASSIGNED, and their index rows stay unwritten here for the same reason they are unwritten on [plans/README.md](../plans/README.md) — that is Michael's call, not this folder's.

## Run one

1. Read [locks.md](../locks.md).
2. Open your seat's plan, then the pass page beside it, and follow it in order.
3. Stop where it says stop, and run [reviews/last-gate-checklist.md](../../reviews/last-gate-checklist.md) — or, where the last gate is not your seat's step, read the lines the work will have to pass anyway and hand it on.
4. Hand anything external to Michael. **Nothing sends.**

One pass, one gate, one stop. [cheap-loop.md](../cheap-loop.md) already says why, and no page here restates it.

Named gaps stay named. Where a page here says a thing is not recorded — who fills the PA seat, the COO's accountabilities beyond the public title, the membership price and the lead magnet's absent Offers row on the CMO's, the live Uncovery script and how an Uncovery is sold on the CSO's — that is the answer until Michael sources one.
