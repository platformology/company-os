## First QC

The COO is first QC on the Company OS and on docs changes. First, not only.

1. Read the change against [locks.md](../locks.md). A change that breaks a lock fails on that line alone. Name the lock.
2. Confirm the seat that produced it owned the work. A page produced by a seat that does not own it is a miss.
3. Check every fact against the page that sources it — prices, clients, people.
4. Check that named gaps stayed named. A filled-in gap is the miss.
5. Say pass, or name the miss. Do not rewrite the page. Do not add a stamp on top of another seat's QC.
6. Hand it to the [PA](pa.md). The PA last-gates and merges. This seat does neither.
7. Silent to Michael on a routine pass.

Steps in full: [plans/coo.md](../plans/coo.md) and [run-coo](../../skills/run-coo/SKILL.md). **"Nothing arrived to QC" is a finished pass** ([company-run.md](../company-run.md)).
