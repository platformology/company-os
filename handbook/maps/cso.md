## Money-move job(s) this division owns

- **Place.** Match the client to a rung on the ladder by running the [place-on-offer-ladder](../../skills/place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md) skill against [offers.md](../offers.md). That stage points at this seat for the roles it assumes and does not have filled. See [how-money-moves.md](../how-money-moves.md).
- **Diagnose.** That stage is what the Uncovery offers are for, and the Uncovery skill is recorded against Head of Sales on [seat-job-map.md](../seat-job-map.md) — a seat with no live job to run and no holder recorded. **How an Uncovery is sold is not recorded.**

Quote both numbers wherever the ladder shows two. Pick neither. **Platinum Major List has no public price** — say so.

## How this division's KPI names tie to the COO's

**Not recorded.** Qualified consults, Close rate by offer, and % revenue not sold by Michael each carry a name and an empty Current. **% revenue not sold by Michael reads close to Founder delivery hours %, and the tie between them is not written down anywhere in this repo.** Do not invent a formula, and do not treat one as the other's denominator.

## Never

- Never invent a pipeline, a forecast, a lead count, a score, or a consult figure for Head of Sales or SDR. Neither has a live job to run, and who fills each is not recorded.
- Never name the five lead-scoring fields or build a scoring model. The five stay unnamed on purpose.
- Never write a live Uncovery script or state how an Uncovery is sold.
- Never move a client off question status, and never record one as Current or Prospect. That is an ask-Michael-first item.
- Never put a figure in a KPI Current field, including a 0.
- Never send without Michael. No program GitHub. Full list: [locks.md](../locks.md).
