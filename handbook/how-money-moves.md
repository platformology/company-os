# How money moves — the five jobs a coordinator can run

Shape: the loop pattern from `departments/_canvas-template.md` — [dswh/company-os](https://github.com/dswh/company-os) (trigger → what to do → where the real instructions live → gate), the same shape used in [weekday-loop.md](weekday-loop.md). The "name the obligation at the point money moves" framing for **Deliver** is adapted from the `money-flow` concept in [geraldmaron/construct](https://github.com/geraldmaron/construct) `docs/org-map.md`: "each point money moves: the obligation that attaches there, who computes it, who remits it, and what evidences it afterward."

Five jobs, in order. This page tells you what triggers each one and which Notion page runs it — it does not restate the SOP.

## 1. Attract

**Trigger:** Someone hears about Promote a Book or Book Retreat for the first time.

**Do:** Point them at the public ladder, not a pitch you improvise. See [offers.md](offers.md) and the [place-on-offer-ladder](../skills/place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md) skill.

**Open in Notion:** [Sales Menu](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b817dae2eeb55c5902ad7) · [Campaign SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ea99c0dcf96db7cd2e)

**Named missing:** the lead magnet has no Offers row — this is exactly the stage that would use one, and there isn't one to quote (see [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md)).

## 2. Diagnose

**Trigger:** Someone wants to know what they actually need before they commit.

**Do:** This is what the Uncovery offers are for. Quote both prices where the ladder shows both:
- Book Writing Uncovery — Consult.
- Book Marketing Uncovery — Consult, **or** Drive wire $20,000.
- Bestseller Uncovery — $20,000, credited toward Platinum in one telling, an additional charge in another (quote both, do not pick).

**Open in Notion:** [Uncovery SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b815d9dbbd7e59102af3e)

**Locked:** No price gets quoted outside [offers.md](offers.md). See [locks.md](locks.md).

## 3. Place

**Trigger:** Diagnosis is done; it's time to match the client to a rung on the ladder.

**Do:** Run the [place-on-offer-ladder](../skills/place-on-offer-ladder/SKILL.md) skill against [offers.md](offers.md). Quote both numbers wherever the ladder shows two — Multi-author, PIAB, Digital, and so on. Platinum Major List has no public price; say so.

**Open in Notion:** [Sales Menu](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b817dae2eeb55c5902ad7)

**Roles this job assumes but doesn't have filled:** Head of Demand, Head of Sales, and SDR all stay unassigned (see [seats/cso.md](seats/cso.md)). Whoever is actually running Place today is doing it without a filled seat under them — don't invent one to make the org chart look complete.

## 4. Deliver

**Trigger:** A campaign, retreat, or PIAB engagement is live and money is moving.

**Do:** Wherever money actually moves at this stage, name it: what's the obligation, who computes it, who remits it, and what evidences it afterward. This repo doesn't restate the answers — [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81) (Notion) does.

**Open in Notion:** [Campaign SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b81ea99c0dcf96db7cd2e) · [Retreat SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c025e30d68b8185b9a9f19dd56389e5) · [PIAB SOP](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81d5beecda81a111e6b3) · [How money moves](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81b49524fe7bc9f65c81)

**Locked:** No send without Michael. Blue Sky stays off the LLC books. See [locks.md](locks.md).

## Across all five

- **KPI "Current" stays empty.** Nothing goes in that field — not a guess, not a placeholder for a future one, and not a 0, because a 0 is a figure and empty means no figure was recorded.
- **The client counts are a separate fact, and they carry their date.** 0 Current and 0 Prospect on the 18 Aug 2026 extract. Quote them with that date, and do not move either number into the KPI. See [clients.md](clients.md).
- **No program GitHub.** None of these five jobs touches `smarter-voice`, `academic-research-platform`, `book-campaign-platform`, `styleguide-os`, `persona-standalone-programs`, or `tlpF-b01-research-engine`.
