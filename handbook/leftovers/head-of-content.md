# Head of Content leftover

**Status: Assigned** · **Holder: not recorded** · **Written: 21 Aug 2026**

This is the leftover a new hire picks up in the Head of Content seat today. It is already named on [seats/head-of-content.md](../seats/head-of-content.md) and [plans/head-of-content.md](../plans/head-of-content.md).

**The leftover:** the **Competitive-style answers** against the five Methodical fields are still unsourced unless Michael provides an extract, and **no content-output record of any kind is recorded** — no piece count, no catalogue inventory, no publication log.

This seat is the **librarian, not the writer.** The corpus is a 27-year body of personas, proposals, white papers, ebooks, and Pendulum material. The job is cataloguing what exists. **Writing new copy and filing it as corpus is denied.** A stand-in is corpus the moment somebody files it.

**"Competitive" names the reader, not a rival company.** No competitor teardown. No rival, feature, price, or claim gets invented to fill a field.

## What you do with it today

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). Then open [seats/head-of-content.md](../seats/head-of-content.md), [desks/head-of-content.md](../desks/head-of-content.md), [plans/head-of-content.md](../plans/head-of-content.md), and [passes/head-of-content.md](../passes/head-of-content.md).
2. Take **one field** on the Notion [Competitive Writing Guide draft](https://app.notion.com/p/3c125e30d68b81239c5fc767bddc0823). Answer it from a sourced extract Michael provides, source kept next to the words — or write **"not sourced"** and stop. **"Not sourced" in the field is itself the finished pass.**
3. The five Methodical fields are already sourced and settled: Characteristics, Buying needs, Likes and dislikes, Use, Avoid. **Not reworded, not reordered, never a sixth.** One style per page.
4. A corpus question is answered by **pointing at the file or saying it is not in hand**, never by composing a stand-in.
5. **Content output** stays empty. Not a count of pieces, not a cadence, not a 0, and **not a tally of Google Drive files.** Google Drive is a tool; "Drive" on the ladder is how a price gets paid.
6. Hand the finished thing to the [CMO](../seats/cmo.md).
