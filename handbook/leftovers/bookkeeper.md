# Bookkeeper leftover

**Status: Assigned** · **Holder: not recorded** · **Written: 21 Aug 2026**

This is the leftover a new hire picks up in the Bookkeeper seat today. It is already named on [seats/bookkeeper.md](../seats/bookkeeper.md).

**The leftover:** **any savings total is not sourced, and it does not get invented.** That is the live leftover on this seat.

**Bench is the Platformology general ledger and no connector exists.** Nothing here pulls from it. The honest report is what a person actually did: **a person opened Bench and read it, or nobody did.** Never a live pull that does not exist. An unread ledger reads exactly like a clean one. **An absence of a reading is not a zero.** **"The number is not in hand" is a finished answer.**

## What you do with it today

1. Open [locks.md](../locks.md). Then open [seats/bookkeeper.md](../seats/bookkeeper.md), [desks/bookkeeper.md](../desks/bookkeeper.md), [plans/bookkeeper.md](../plans/bookkeeper.md), and [passes/bookkeeper.md](../passes/bookkeeper.md).
2. Answer or advance **one bills-or-ledger question.** The bill in front of you is the only record. No invoice, AR figure, vendor list, bill amount, payment schedule, or approval threshold is recorded in this repo.
3. On the leftover itself: say **the savings total is not sourced.** Do not invent one. If nobody opened Bench, say nobody opened Bench.
4. The monthly close is the [Controller](../seats/controller.md)'s. **June and July 2026 are not closed.** No bill gets described as posted into a closed month.
5. **Blue Sky stays off the LLC books.** A price off [offers.md](../offers.md) travels with both numbers where the ladder shows two, and **a price is not revenue and not a receivable.**
6. Hand the finished thing to the [CFO](../seats/cfo.md). The PA last-gates after that.
